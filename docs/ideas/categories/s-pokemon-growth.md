## Approved ideas
- S45 Nature Tuning: spend dust to nudge a nature a small step.
- S46 Training Camps: assign benched mons to camps that grant passive stats.
- S47 Bond Levels: partner bonds unlock paired moves and dialogue.
- S48 Mentor System: an evolved mon can teach one trait to a younger teammate.
- S49 Breeding Nursery: hatch eggs with inherited moves and a chosen ability.
- S51 Fossil Lab: revive fossils and upgrade them with excavated parts.
- S52 Regional Form Shifts: swap a mon to a regional variant via a ritual item.
- S54 Move Tutor Rotation: tutors change per run with a limited pick count.
- S55 Ability Capsule Quests: earn ability swaps by clearing themed challenges.
- S56 Individual Quirks: each mon rolls a small personality quirk (shy, brave, lucky).
- S57 Friendship Mood: a mon's mood shifts from your choices and affects obedience.
- S58 Pokémon Retirement: retire a loved mon to a Hall that gives run-start perks.
- S59 Legacy Lines: a mon's descendants inherit a signature move across runs.
- S61 Level Cap Gates: caps rise with each boss; overleveling converts to bonus items.
- S62 Shared Experience Pool: pick how XP splits (even, focus, catch-up).
- S63 Respec Shrine: reset EV-style training for a price once per run.
- S64 Pokémon Diaries: every mon logs memorable moments that surface in the Hall.
- S65 Team Synergy Tags: tags like Sun Team or Trick Room Team unlock bonuses.
