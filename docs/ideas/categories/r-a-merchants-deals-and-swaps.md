# Run events A: Merchants, deals and swaps

All 50 approved ("YES TO ALL", no individual comments).

Source: `docs/ideas/review-2026-11-02.md`. Build as event data through the existing events pipeline (`src/data/events.json`, `eventsW5.json`, content gate). Check each idea against existing events first.

## Ideas
- R1 Honest Hawker: sells one item at exactly shop price, but throws in a free berry if you say thanks.
- R2 Barter Barge: trade any 2 held items for 1 better-tier item.
- R3 Mismatched Socks Sale: buy a pair of boots for speed boost on the next 3 battles; one boot is lost if you skip.
- R4 Coupon Clipper: 30% off coupon that works at the next shop only.
- R5 Subscription Box: pay once, receive a surprise item after each of the next 3 battles.
- R6 Layaway Lady: pay half now, get the item after 2 more map nodes.
- R7 Rental Counter: rent a rare held item for 5 battles, auto-returns.
- R8 Price Match Guy: shows the cheapest price he has seen; matches it for one item.
- R9 Bulk Buyer: sells nothing; pays 2x for stacks of 5+ identical items.
- R10 Wholesale Warehouse: buy 5 of one consumable at a steep discount.
- R11 Clearance Rack of Doom: random items at 90% off, one is cursed (random debuff).
- R12 Auction Pop-up: three bidders (an NPC, a rival, a Rocket grunt); outbid them with money.
- R13 Silent Auction: write a bid; closest to the hidden value wins the item.
- R14 Raffle Seller: buy tickets; more tickets, better odds at a tier-3 item.
- R15 Pawn Sharks: sell a held item now, buy it back later at a mark-up.
- R16 Loan Shark Lanturn: borrow 500; pay 700 back at the next shop or lose a held item.
- R17 Street Tailor: swaps your trainer's outfit cosmetics for a small stat perk.
- R18 Charm Tinkerer: combines two charms into one weaker blended charm.
- R19 Mystery Meat Cart: cheap food heals 30% HP, small chance of Poison.
- R20 Poké Ball Pitchman: sells three odd Balls, names them like infomercial items.
- R21 Vending Machine: insert coins, random item; shake it for a free one at a risk of an angry Rotom.
- R22 Capsule Gacha: a toy machine; each spin is a random cosmetic or tiny perk.
- R23 Flea Market Fossil: a "fossil" that might be a rock; reveal costs money, maybe a rare revive.
- R24 Bait Shop Bargain: buy lures that raise rare encounter chance for 3 nodes.
- R25 Repel Reseller: sells expired Repels (half effect) dirt cheap.
- R26 Scalper Alley: sells a Tier-3 item at triple price; haggle with a Speech check.
- R27 Estate Sale: a house full of items; take two, owner's relative glares.
- R28 Garage Sale of a Retired Champion: ex-champion sells 3 signature items.
- R29 Lost \& Found Auction: unclaimed items from past runs (ghost of earlier loot).
- R30 Gift Wrap Station: wrap an item; the receiver (next NPC) gives a bonus.
- R31 Delivery Rider: carry a parcel across 3 nodes for payment; opening it is tempting.
- R32 Pawn Berry Smoothie: blend 3 berries into a temporary buff drink.
- R33 Mobile Poké Mart Truck: appears for one node only, stock is random.
- R34 Black Friday Stampede: crowd rush; fight a swarm of shoppers for first pick.
- R35 Counterfeit Charm Cop: report the fake seller for a reward, or buy the fake cheap.
- R36 Magnet Salesman: sells Magnemite-powered gadgets that pull coins toward you.
- R37 Smuggler's Crate: buy unopened crate; contents may include a banned item.
- R38 Rebate Booth: refund 20% of your last purchase this run.
- R39 Bank Teller: deposit money to earn 10% interest at the next bank stop.
- R40 Currency Exchanger: turn money into Battle Points or reverse.
- R41 Mismatched Mart: shop sells only one type's items today (rotating).
- R42 Seller of Second Chances: pays to undo your last event choice (once).
- R43 Quiet Antiquarian: gives lore plus a small relic.
- R44 Itinerant Moves Tutor: teaches one move per visit for an odd price (a berry, a coin, a secret).
- R45 Retired Gym Merch Stall: type-themed badge pins with tiny type boosts.
- R46 Sock Puppet Salesman: Mimikyu in disguise tries to sell you an item; battle if you see through him.
- R47 Trade Wind Caravan: three merchants, you may trade with only one.
- R48 Stolen Goods Stall: buy cheap; a Rocket grunt may reclaim the item later.
- R49 Apothecary Experiment: test-drink a random potion for money back.
- R50 Final Sale Banner: everything on a stall is 1 money; the catch is a long skit you must sit through.
