# Beta 0.6 — INSTRUMENTALITY: "You Are (Not) Alone"

**What this file is.** My definition of the update in the `pocket-raiders-upd.zip` pack. It covers what the update is, its core ideas and themes, its name, what it adds, and where it clashes with the game's current rules. The build plan is in the companion file, `INSTRUMENTALITY_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

Written 2026-11-13. Sources: the evil team pack (Expanded Edition), the expanded notes pack, the sprite folders and the handoff. I checked them against the game repo at `origin/main` 552e29ef.

## 1. The name

### Title: **INSTRUMENTALITY**
### Subtitle: **You Are (Not) Alone**

Your working title, "You Are (Not) Alone", is the subtitle of *Evangelion 1.0*, so the Evangelion theme was already there. **Instrumentality** is the Evangelion plan to end all loneliness and pain by melting every person into one being with no self. The hero has to choose: let it happen, or stay a separate, hurt person who can still reach other people.

That choice runs through the whole pack:

- **Every villain is selling Instrumentality.** Cyrus wants to subtract the spirit. Lusamine wants to keep everything still. Ghetsis says "let go". Lysandre has his guest list. Rose has his thousand-year plan. The Distortion Division says "no far, no after". Cipher builds **XD001 Shadow Lugia out of nine stolen methods**, nine wills sewn into one thing with no self.
- **The player's answer is bonds, not power.** Rivals turn up, the Friend holds a district, crowds chant a Pokémon's name, Team Star stands next to you. Purifying a Shadow means giving a Pokémon its own heart back. In Evangelion that is the **A.T. Field**: the wall that keeps you yourself, and which you must lower to let someone in.
- **"(Not)" is the whole game.** You are alone in the run, and you are not alone, because the run is full of people who choose you.

## 2. What the update is, in one paragraph

INSTRUMENTALITY gives every region a **living evil team** with its own long story. It also gives the run **rivals who feel like people**, **legends and mythicals who make deals with you**, and **one dark story that can take over a whole run**. Each region's team grows from rumours into raids, then into a long multi-phase **Extended Cut** climax. Behind all of them is Cipher, collecting one method from each team to build XD001. Rivals keep score of the Lead, react to you along six feelings, and can save you once. A Legendary blesses each run. Mythicals offer promises. Ultra Beasts come when your play habits call them. About one run in three, a **Whole Run Event** such as *Guidance of Giratina* runs from Stage 2 to the Champion. Balance work also changes which Pokémon you meet, how trainers build teams, and how sweeps get stopped.

## 3. Core ideas (the pillars)

1. **Villains are organisations, not bosses.** Teams have ranks (Associate up to Leader), named officers, Heat, Intel, a reputation with you, raids that can succeed or fail, and a **world-state track (0–5)** that changes the region. Examples: shops cost more, Pokémon Centers heal 80%, the PC holds four fewer Pokémon, bridges close. Every step is announced and none of them raises enemy levels.
2. **Scale.** Five scales (Street, City, Region, Border, World). Kanto alone goes from 73 to 188 canon spaces. 143 named characters, 110 special Pokémon with signature traits, 58 special Shadows, 14 variant lines, 206 run events and 100 Greenneedle events.
3. **The last act is longer and wider.** The Extended Cut climax runs 13–18 battles across set phases: Siege Map, Blackout/Convoy, Gauntlet, Duels/Front, **Breather** (heal 50%, revive 1), Set Piece, Leader stage, **Colossus vs Apex**, an optional Phase 7, then Resolution. There are two save points, and the Standard Cut stays available.
4. **One thread through every region.** Cipher's Grand Arc: a liaison and a Data ROM in each region, the Stock track, and an optional finale at Citadark. Collect all nine ROMs and the last fight is not a fight.
5. **Rivals as people.** Lead −3..+3, Marks, six feelings (Friendship, Respect, Trust, Rivalry, Resentment, Admiration), 8 personalities, 8 battle styles, 8 story arcs, tier-5 AI, a Rival Report, the Rival Rescue Plan, a Friend who never betrays you, and Second Chance (5%, once per run).
6. **Legends make deals.** 71 Legendary Blessings with tiers (Lesser to Origin), Influence, and Communion when you catch that Legendary. 23 Mythical Promises (keep the rule and they stay; break it and they leave). 11 Ultra Beast Incursions that come because of how you play, not at random.
7. **The whole run can be someone's story.** 5 Whole Run Events, at most one per run, in about 35% of runs. *Guidance of Giratina* is fully written, with 10 beats and 6 endings. The other four are outlined.
8. **Shadows mean something.** Corruption tiers, keys that purify a named Shadow, heart scars and the Snag machines. Using a Shadow is always optional.
9. **Fair play rules.** Nothing is unwinnable. Every hard rule is announced first. You get two shots at every climax, and a story loss never ends the run. One of each transformation (Mega, Z, Dynamax, Tera) per side per battle. +30% cap on permanent bonuses. Rally stops sweeps. Level clamps. Every setting can be switched off, and with all of them off the game plays exactly like today.

## 4. Themes

- **Loneliness vs connection.** Each villain offers an end to pain by giving up the self. The player wins by staying themselves and still reaching out.
- **Villains are people.** Officers have a conscience, what-if versions can always be won back, and Team Star turn out not to be the villains at all.
- **Failure is a story, not a game over.** Lost raids change the world, and lost climaxes go to the Doom Ledger. (Section 7 says where the pack breaks this.)
- **What you leave behind comes back.** The Region of Doom is built from what you didn't finish.
- **Kindness is a key.** The "Mirror" purification key needs a real kind act. The game never invents one for you.

### Rule changes these decisions make to the game

- **Off list:** Triple Battle is now *in*. Evolution Encounters are *on*. Mic input is *in*.
- **Battle items:** 10 healing + 6 revives per battle (today: 6 revives).
- **Transformations:** one of each gimmick per side per battle (today: one in total).
- **Party size:** listed story bosses may have 7.
