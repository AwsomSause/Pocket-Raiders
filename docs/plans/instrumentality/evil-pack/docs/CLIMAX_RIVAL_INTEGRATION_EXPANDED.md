## X1. What this file adds

The base document guarantees that a rival is part of every regional climax, in one of three ways: **Help (60%)**, **Beat-you (30%)**, **Side-with (10%)**. The roll, its odds and its permanence are unchanged.

The Expanded Edition made the climax longer and stranger: a city-wide Siege Map, a Blackout, a Breather, an Apex Shadow fought as a Colossus. The base rules say what the rival does in four phases. This file says what the rival does in ten.

## X2. The rival in the Extended Cut

The Extended Cut's phases (`EXPANSION_DESIGN.md` §6) and what the rival is doing in each.

| # | Phase | Help (60%) | Beat-you (30%) | Side-with (10%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | News and choice | Not mentioned | Named in the report | Not mentioned |
| 0b | **Siege Map** | Holds one district of the player's choosing. It cannot be Lost while they stand | One district is already **Held** when the map opens, marked with the rival's initial. Nobody is there now | One district is **Lost** from the start, and the effect it adds is the one that hurts the player's team most. In hindsight: they knew |
| 1 | **Blackout** | Calls out the switch: the objective that ends the condition is marked from turn 1 | The condition is already half-broken: it ends one turn sooner. There is wreckage | Normal. The rival is seen once, on a balcony or a monitor |
| 2 | Gauntlet | One battle fewer | One or two fewer, with aftermath hazards | Normal length |
| 3 | Officer duels | Co-op: the rival takes one duel entirely | **The rescue** (X7). The rival is found here or in Phase 4, as the script says | Normal. The last officer names the rival afterwards |
| — | **Breather** | The rival is here. See X9 | The rival is here if rescued, quieter. See X9 | The rival is not here. Their bag is |
| 4 | Set piece | Holds one objective | Objective count +1; the rival, if rescued, holds the extra one | The rival sabotages one objective, as in the base rules |
| 4b | **Phase 2.5 duel** | — | — | The Rival Duel and the Reveal Scene (X6) |
| 5 | Leader | One Assist per three turns | One Assist in total | If turned: +1 Assist. If not: one Rival Interference, announced |
| 6 | **Colossus** | Joins for two turns and uses **Crack a Seam** (X3). Has a line when the Apex first speaks | Arrives for the last part only. "I'm late. I know. Which one's left?" | If turned: breaks one part alone, off-screen, and the plate updates. If not: stands between the player and the last part for one turn, then cannot do it, and steps aside |
| 7 | Optional phase | Holds a lane or an island, as the script says | Sits it out, recovering | If turned, takes the hardest lane without being asked |
| 8 | Resolution | Shared scene | By archetype | By the Turn |

**Fairness.** The base swing targets stand: Help about 8% easier, Beat-you neutral, Side-with about 6% harder. The Extended Cut adds battles, and the rival's larger role is sized so that those percentages do not move. The Rival Assist budget grows with the phase count and no faster (X3.2).

## X3. Rival Assists for the wide formats

### X3.1 Five new Assists

The base six (Cover, Opening, Cure, Bait, Rally, Intel) work in any battle. These five work only in the format named.

| Assist | Format | Effect | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hold District** | Siege Map | The rival's district cannot be Lost this climax | Chosen once, in Phase 0b |
| **Find the Switch** | Blackout | The objective that ends the condition is marked on the map | Once |
| **Take a Lane** | Front | The rival fights one of the three lanes. The player still acts in one lane per turn, but the rival's lane cannot be lost while they stand | Once per Front |
| **Close the Gap** | Convoy | The distance meter moves one step in the player's favor | Once per Convoy |
| **Crack a Seam** | Colossus | One standing part of the player's choice is cracked (reduced to half its shield) | Once |

## X5. The Turn, in depth

### X5.1 The formula, with two new terms

`turnScore = attitude + dialogueChoice + raidBonus + memoryBonus + keepsakeBonus + shadowTerm`

Turn if `turnScore ≥ 3`, as before. The first four terms are the base document's.

| New term | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `keepsakeBonus` | **+1** if the player picked up the rival's small object at the climax entrance (foreshadow beat 3) and gives it back during the Reveal Scene | The object was always there. Now it does something |
| `shadowTerm` | **−1** if the player's active Pokémon in the Rival Duel is an unstabilized Gripped or Fallen Shadow; **+1** if the player has purified any Shadow Pokémon this region; 0 otherwise | The rival is being told they chose wrongly by someone. It matters who |

The two terms can cancel. Neither can exceed ±1, so the Turn remains mostly about attitude and the key response.

### X5.2 Worked examples

| Case | attitude | choice | raids | memory | keepsake | shadow | Score | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A warm rival, the key response, nothing else | +2 | +2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **4** | Turns |
| A cool rival, an acceptable response, both raids won, the keepsake returned | 0 | +1 | +1 | 0 | +1 | 0 | **3** | Turns, barely. The rival's line is the "…Fine." version |
| A hostile rival, the key response, a shared-rest memory, a purified Shadow | −2 | +2 | 0 | +1 | 0 | +1 | **2** | Stays. One short of enough; the Aftermath line says so |
| A friendly rival, the wrong response, a Fallen Shadow on the field | +3 | −1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | −1 | **1** | Stays. "You're the one telling me about bad choices?" |

### X5.3 The Rival's Shadow

Where the rival's extra Pokémon is a Shadow (Johto, Sinnoh, and any region where Snag'em is the lead team), the Turn has a second half.

- **If the rival turns**, the Shadow does not turn with them. It is still bound to the team's method. The rival asks for help: the Pokémon is handed to the player's care for purification, or the rival keeps it and the player's next purification method used in their presence also applies to it at half strength.
- **If its key is met** (expanded Shadow document, X8), it is freed at once and the rival gains the memory `shadow-freed` (base Shadow document §9): +1 Attitude and a line.
- **If the rival stays**, the Shadow stays with them. It is met again in the Region of Doom, at Gym 6.

### X5.4 Nine Turns that are not arguments

The base rules give every archetype a key response. The Expanded scripts add, for each region, the thing the player *does* at the moment of the Turn. It replaces the third dialogue option when its condition is met, and counts as the key response.

| Region | The act | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kanto | Nothing. The rival tears off their own tag-pin, and takes Matori's duel for the player | Turn score reached |
| Johto | Hand them the microphone. They finish the broadcast with the truth | The player freed Mary, or the rival did |
| Hoenn | Nothing. Their own leader releases them: "Take the bandana off, kid. I'm about to." | The *Common Cause* scene is reached |
| Sinnoh | Tell them the worst joke in the game. It works | The joke was heard earlier, from a Grunt on the Spiritless ward |
| Unova | Hand them a carrier tag with a town written on it. Say nothing | The player kept a tag from a raid |
| Kalos | Stand aside. They hand their Flare Key to a member's child in the queue | The player read the list (Raid 4) |
| Alola | Take the ladle. The Turn happens in the Shady House kitchen, not in a battle | Po Town was fed (Raid 1 or the Loyalty Job's mercy) |
| Galar | Show them the Draft Release. They read "if low", and take the jacket off | The player has the Draft Release |
| Paldea | **Optional.** The rival was right to join. They leave the squad only to stand next to the player in Phase 6, and go back afterwards | Always |

If the condition is not met, the ordinary three dialogue options are shown and the Turn is decided by the formula alone.

## X6. Eight Reveal Scenes

The base rules require a Reveal Scene with three response options. These are the eight, one per archetype, to be dressed with each team's name and place. `{teamName}` is the rival's name from X4. **Bold** marks the key response (+2); the others are acceptable (+1), neutral (0) or wrong (−1). Four are written for each scene and **three are shown**, as the base rules require: the key response and two of the others. A rival at Warm or better is not shown the wrong response. A rival at Cool or worse is not shown the neutral one.

### X6.1 Hotshot — *Tempted by power*

*The rival steps out with their new Pokémon beside them. They have clearly rehearsed this.*

> **RIVAL:** "Ta-da. Go on. Say something. …They gave me *this*. On the first day! You know how long I've been asking somebody, anybody, to notice I was good?"
> **RIVAL:** "You never said it. Not once. You just kept winning and looking sort of sorry about it."
>
> - **["You are good. Prove it without theirs. One on one, your own six."]** *(key)* → "…My own six. …Yeah. Yeah, all right. Hold this." *(They recall the team's Pokémon.)* "If I win I'm still mad at you."
> - ["You don't need them."] *(+1)* → "Easy for you to say. You've never needed anyone."
> - ["Is it worth it?"] *(0)* → "Ask me after I beat you."
> - ["They're using you."] *(−1)* → "Everyone's using me! At least they *pay*!"

### X6.2 Prodigy — *Ideological*

*The rival is at a desk, or a console, or a lectern. They do not look up at once.*

> **RIVAL:** "I checked their reasoning four times. I wanted to find the flaw. I could not. Do you understand how rarely that happens to me?"
> **RIVAL:** "You will say it is wrong. Everyone says it is wrong. Nobody *shows* me."
>
> - **[Show them: the cage tags, the reading, the list. Whatever the player carried out of a raid.]** *(key)* → *(a long silence)* "…This was not in the model. They did not put this in the model. …They knew I would check."
> - ["It's wrong because people get hurt."] *(+1)* → "A cost. Costs can be weighed. Show me the weights."
> - ["You're smarter than this."] *(0)* → "That is a compliment shaped like an argument. It is not one."
> - ["You've been brainwashed."] *(−1)* → "And you have been told what to think since you were ten. We are both products. Mine has footnotes."

### X6.3 Friendly — *Deceived*

*The rival is crying before the scene starts and is cross about it.*

> **RIVAL:** "They said they'd help. They were so *nice*, {player}. Nobody's nice like that for no reason, I know that, I'm not stupid. I just… wanted somebody to be."
> **RIVAL:** "And now I've promised. I don't break promises. That's the only thing I'm any good at."
>
> - **["I believe you meant it. I believe in you. Not in them."]** *(key)* → "…You're not angry?" *(They wipe their face.)* "You should be angry. Okay. Okay. I'm coming. I have to say sorry to someone first."
> - ["They lied to you. A promise to a liar doesn't count."] *(+1)* → "It counts to *me*."
> - ["Come home."] *(0)* → "I don't know if I'm allowed."
> - ["How could you be so naive?"] *(−1)* → *(very quietly)* "…Right. Yeah. That's what they said you'd say."

### X6.4 Bitter — *Coerced by grievance*

*The rival is leaning on a wall with their arms crossed. They are wearing the uniform badly, on purpose.*

> **RIVAL:** "Don't. Whatever speech you've got. You got the starter, the sponsor, the *luck*. I got told to try harder. So I found people who don't care how I got here."
> **RIVAL:** "They're awful. I know they're awful. They're awful and they *picked me*."
>
> - **["It wasn't fair. It still isn't. I can't fix that. I can stand next to you while you're angry."]** *(key)* → "…Don't you dare be decent about this. …Ugh." *(They push off the wall.)* "No pity. I see one bit of pity and I'm gone."
> - ["You earned everything you have."] *(+1)* → "Then why's it so much less than yours?"
> - ["I'm sorry."] *(0)* → "Great. I'll put it with the others."
> - ["Stop feeling sorry for yourself."] *(−1)* → "There it is. There's the one I remember."

### X6.5 Scholar — *Curiosity*

*The rival is taking notes. They hold up one finger: wait. They finish the sentence.*

> **RIVAL:** "I know what you are going to say, and I have no rebuttal, which is uncomfortable. I am not here because I agree with them. I am here because nobody else would let me *look*."
> **RIVAL:** "Do you have any idea what they have built? Nobody has ever seen one of these awake."
>
> - **["What have you found? …And what did it cost the thing you were looking at?"]** *(key)* → *(They look at their notes. They turn back several pages.)* "…I recorded its vital signs every ten minutes. I did not once record whether it was frightened. That is a hole in the data. That is a hole in *me*."
> - ["Some things shouldn't be studied."] *(+1)* → "Everything should be studied. Not everything should be *done*. I may have confused the two."
> - ["Was it interesting?"] *(0)* → "Extremely. That is rather the problem."
> - ["You're as bad as they are."] *(−1)* → "A testable claim. Let us test it."

### X6.6 Heir — *Obligation*

*The rival bows. They are immaculate. Their hands are not quite steady.*

> **RIVAL:** "My family has owed this organization a debt since before I was born. I was told its size on my twelfth birthday. I was told I would be the one to pay it."
> **RIVAL:** "I do not expect you to understand. I ask only that you do not make me explain it twice."
>
> - **["Then I release you from it. Somebody has to say it. It may as well be someone who's beaten you."]** *(key)* → "…That is not how debts—" *(They stop.)* "…No one has ever said that. I had assumed it could not be said." *(They remove one glove, slowly.)*
> - ["A debt to them isn't a debt."] *(+1)* → "It is a debt to my *name*."
> - ["What do you want?"] *(0)* → "I was not raised to have an answer to that."
> - ["Your family sounds terrible."] *(−1)* → "You will not speak of them. Draw."

### X6.7 Lone Wolf — *Own cause*

*The rival is already there. They may have been there for some time.*

> **RIVAL:** "…Our goals overlap. Mine and theirs. Not for long."
> **RIVAL:** "…Stay out of it."
>
> - **[Don't argue. Show them another way in: the route, the key, the door the player found.]** *(key)* → *(They look at it. They look at the player.)* "…That works." *(They walk past, toward it. At the door:)* "…Coming?"
> - ["What's your goal?"] *(+1)* → "…Mine."
> - ["You can't trust them."] *(0)* → "…Don't."
> - ["You're being used."] *(−1)* → "…So are you."

### X6.8 Mirror — *Reflection*

*The rival is standing exactly as the player is standing.*

> **RIVAL:** "I asked myself what you'd do. If you'd started with nothing. If nobody had been waiting at the end of Route 1. I think you'd be here. I think you'd be wearing this."
> **RIVAL:** "Tell me I'm wrong. You can't. You don't know either."
>
> - **["I don't know what I'd have done. I know what *you* did last week."]** *(key; the player names one thing the rival did this run: a shared rest, a gift, a warning, drawn from rival memory)* → "…I did that. …That wasn't you. You didn't even know about that." *(They shift their weight, and for the first time are not standing like the player.)*
> - ["You're not me."] *(+1)* → "No. I'm the one it didn't work out for."
> - ["Maybe I would be."] *(0)* → "Thank you for not lying."
> - ["I'd never."] *(−1)* → "That's exactly what I'd say."

If the rival has no positive memory for the Mirror's key response to name, the option is not shown, and the Mirror can only be turned by attitude and the other terms. The game does not invent a kindness that did not happen.

## X7. Four rescues, staged for the wide formats

In Beat-you the rival is found in one of four states (base §6.1). The Extended Cut gives each state a larger stage.

| State | Where it happens | The rescue | What the rival can do afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Captured** | Phase 3, behind the last officer | A **Convoy of two**: the rival is being moved. Catch the escort before the lift, the truck or the boat leaves | One Assist in Phase 5; arrives for the last part of the Colossus |
| **Shaken** | The Breather | No battle. The rival is sitting very still. An item (anything warm: a drink, a berry, a blanket) or the right line brings them back | No Assists. They hold the player's bag in Phase 6 and say one thing |
| **Hurt** | Phase 2, mid-gauntlet | An **escort**: two battles with the rival beside the player and unable to fight. If the rival's position is hit, the escort restarts from the last door | One *Cure* in Phase 5, given from a chair |
| **Defiant but outnumbered** | Phase 1, under the Blackout | A **Front**: the rival is already fighting in one lane and losing. The player takes the other two | Full Help-variant Assists from then on. They will not admit it was a rescue |

### X7.1 Where each region puts it

| Region | Found | In what condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kanto | The Specimen Hall, not the supply room | They opened one cage before they were caught |
| Johto | The Record Library | Tied up with tape, under a pile of jingles |
| Hoenn | Between the two pedestals | Shielding a bound twin from both teams |
| Sinnoh | The Overhang | Bronzed: one of Hunter J's statues. Beating her restores them |
| Unova | Frozen to a Relay | Shielding one of the released Pokémon with their coat |
| Kalos | The Lift Queue | In a stolen red suit, stuck behind six members and too polite to push |
| Alola | The gallery | In an empty tube that had been made ready. Unhurt, cold, furious |
| Galar | The 30th floor | Wearing the Ball Guy's discarded head, "to get past security." It is stuck |
| Paldea | A Base's garage | Covered in glitter, being bandaged by three Star Grunts who are arguing about how |

The region decides *where*. The archetype decides *which state* (base §6). Where the two disagree (a Lone Wolf in the Ball Guy's head), the region's staging wins and the archetype's line is used, which is usually funnier.

### X7.2 Rescue lines

| Archetype | When found | When freed |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Prodigy | "I have reviewed my error eleven times. It does not improve." | "Your arrival was outside my projections. I am revising my projections of you. Upward." |
| Friendly | "Oh no. Oh, I'm sorry. You've got enough to do. I didn't want to be one more thing." | "You came all this way. With everything else going on. …I'm going to cry. Don't wait for me, I can cry and walk." |

## X8. The rival and the Apex

Every regional Apex speaks at least twice as its parts break. The rival is the one person besides the player who hears it.

### X8.1 What the rival does at the Colossus

| Variant | Arrives | Does | Has a line when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Help | With the player | Fights for two turns; uses **Crack a Seam** once | The Apex first speaks |
| Beat-you (rescued) | For the last part | Takes one hit meant for the player (a free *Cover*) | The last part breaks |
| Beat-you (not rescued, or Shaken) | Not at all, or holding the bag | — | After, in the Resolution |
| Side-with, turned | Mid-fight, from the far side | Breaks one part alone | They see what they were standing next to |
| Side-with, stayed | With the Leader | Stands in front of the last part for one turn, then steps aside | They step aside |

### X8.2 The rival's Pokémon and the Apex

A Shadow Pokémon gains +10 Surge per turn while an Apex is on the field (expanded Shadow document, X9.2). In the Side-with variant where the rival carries a Shadow, the player can see the rival's own Pokémon straining toward the thing on the pedestal. It is the strongest argument in the room, and nobody has to make it.

### X8.3 When the rival hears it speak

| Friendly | "Oh. Oh, sweetheart. Hold on. We're coming. {player}, tell it we're coming." |

## X9. The Breather conversation

At the Breather the rival, if present, sits down. This is the one scene in the climax with no stakes and no clock.

### X9.1 Who speaks first, by attitude

| Attitude | The scene |
| --- | --- |
| Close (+4, +5) | The rival is already asleep against the player's bag. They wake for one line and hand over a held item they have been saving |
| Warm (+2, +3) | The rival talks. See the archetype questions below |
| Neutral (−1 to +1) | The rival sits at the other end of the bench. Halfway through the rest, they move one seat closer and say nothing |
| Cool (−3, −2) | The rival checks their Pokémon with their back turned. They leave a berry on the bench when they go |
| Hostile (−5, −4) | The rival is not at the Breather. Their voice comes over a radio once: where the next door is |

### X9.2 The question

At Warm or better the rival asks one question. There is no right answer and no score. The answer is stored as `rival.x.breather` and is quoted back once, in the Resolution.

| Archetype | The question | Asked while |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotshot | "If we lose. Just, if. Do you think anyone'll know we were here?" | Retying a shoe that does not need it |
| Prodigy | "What is the first thing you will do tomorrow? I find I have not planned past tonight. That is new." | Rearranging their Poké Balls by type |
| Friendly | "Are *you* okay? Everyone keeps asking your Pokémon. I'm asking you." | Handing round food |

## X14. Tests

- The 60/30/10 roll and its determinism are unchanged by `climax.cut`.
- Each variant has a defined role in every Extended Cut phase, including formats a given region does not use (the row is skipped, never empty).
- Wide-format Assists: at most three are used per climax; each works only in its format.
- Win-rate swing by variant stays within the base targets in the Extended Cut for the reference party.
- In Side-with, exactly one district starts Lost; in Beat-you, exactly one starts Held.
- `turnScore` with the two new terms: unit tests across archetype × choice × attitude × keepsake × shadow; the new terms never contribute more than ±1 each.
- A region-specific Turn act appears only when its condition is met, and counts as the key response.
- Paldea's Turn is optional and the climax is completable either way.
- The Mirror's key response is hidden when no positive memory exists.
- Each Reveal Scene shows the key response and two others, selected by attitude.
- Every rescue state has a completable rescue in its wide format; the *Hurt* escort restarts from a door, never from the phase start.
- The rival has a line for the Apex's first words in every archetype, and is silent in variants where they are absent.
- The Breather scene exists for all five attitude bands; the question is asked only at Warm or better.
- The bag appears only in Side-with, and any of its three items can be returned.
- 96 new lines resolve with no empty slots; stand-ins never use a memory callout.
- At the finale the rival's position follows the run's majority variant; ties resolve to the most recent region.
