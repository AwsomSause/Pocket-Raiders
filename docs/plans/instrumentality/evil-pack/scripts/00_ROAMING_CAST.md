# 00 — ROAMING CAST: THE RECURRING TRIO AND TEAM SNAG'EM
### Script: *Wherever You Go*

> *"We were never supposed to still be here. Somehow we always are."* — James

**Team type:** Two roaming groups with no home region · **Region id:** `any` · **Teams:** Rocket Remnants (the trio and their old Rocket colleagues) and Team Snag'em
**Climax:** none of their own; they feed other teams' climaxes (see §9)
**Placement modes:** *Returning team* (either can be placed as a region's own evil team, per decision D3) and *Side faction* (in regions that already have an evil team, per the user's choice "1 and 3")
**Canon note:** Jessie, James, Meowth, Butch, Cassidy and Domino are canon Team Rocket characters from the anime and games; Gonzap, Wakin, Biden, Agrev and Wes are canon Team Snag'em / Colosseum characters. Dialogue, scenes and plots are original. Species are my best recall of what the anime and games show (marked approximate); verify before shipping.

## 3. Story Overview

**Logline:** The trio keep turning up, and a syndicate is selling stolen Pokémon to people who turn them into weapons. One group is a running joke. The other is not.

**Rocket Remnants.** After the organization fell, some people never stopped working for it. The trio are the loudest. They are not the best at anything but have an unmatched track record of surviving what should have killed them.

**Team Snag'em.** Gonzap and his officers take Pokémon from trainers with the Snag Machine and hand them to a backer called Cipher. Every Shadow Pokémon in the Snag'em stock is a theft victim.

**Placement modes.**
- *Returning team:* either group can be a region's own evil team (D3). As a returning team they use their own raids and climax adapter (§8).
- *Side faction:* in a region that already has an evil team, either group appears as a minor second front. Snag'em takes the **Side Faction** slot in 50% of regions (seeded); the trio take **Recurring Trio** beats in every region.

## 7. The Recurring Trio — Beats

The trio appear in every region, not just where they are placed. Per region, seed exactly **one beat from each of two slots** (a *comic* slot and an *allied/wild* slot).

**Comic slots (pick one per region):**

- **Sighting.** A balloon crosses the sky while the player walks. No battle. Optional: Intel +1 if the player follows it.
  > **JESSIE:** "Look at us. High above the world."
  > **JAMES:** "That's because the engine is broken."
- **Collision.** The trio interrupts a raid in a crowded room by crashing through the ceiling. They are not part of the objective, but Failure makes them a hazard and Triumph lets them take the blame.
  > **MEOWTH:** "Okay, who left a trap door?"
- **Cache.** The trio left a hidden stash on a route (a supply crate, a gadget, a half-eaten sandwich). A free item for the player who finds it; the trio come back for it two nodes later and ambush with a comic battle.
- **Cameo.** The trio appear as unnamed extras at the edge of a news report or a climax cutaway. A one-line gag, e.g. *"…and in the background, three people in a hot-air balloon shaped like a cat."*

**Allied / wild slots (pick one per region):**

- **Uneasy ally.** The trio offer a deal: they will stay out of your way during one raid if you do not battle them. Accepting makes the raid's Complication reduce one hazard; declining leaves them as a comic ambush.
- **Wild card (Stand with them).** If the player has `<team>.insideman`, the trio may be among the Inside Man's contacts. They can be a **comic third voice** in the briefing and a cameo in `PS-CLIMAX-ALLIED` (holding a door, creating a distraction). They never replace the Inside Man; they decorate it.
- **Genuine help.** Once per run, if the player's Heat ≥ 30 and the trio's reputation is ≥ +15, they appear in an ambush as a *rescue*: they pull the player's captured party member out of a holding site, then flee. The beat is canceled if the player has battled them more than twice in the region.
- **Snag'em collision.** If Snag'em is in the region, the trio appear in the same node and Snag'em steal their Wobbuffet. The player may return it.

**Trio rules.**
- The trio are always **Specialist (2)** and never become Executives.
- Their Pokémon are never impounded by a failed raid (they are always a comic outcome).
- The trio never appear as the primary team's leader, and never in `PS-CLIMAX` or `PS-AFTER` except as a cameo or a Stand-with decoration.
- Trio reputation: starts at 0; +5 for sparing them, +10 for returning a stolen item; −5 for battling them more than twice.

## 8. Placement Mode Kits

### 8.1 Rocket Remnants as a returning team

**PR:** `pr.any.balloon-dock` (Raid 1), `pr.any.sub-hideout` (Raid 2).

**Raid 1 — "Balloon Dock"**
**Offer (a harbor worker):** *"A balloon's been parked here for three weeks. Three people and a cat live in it."*
**PR:** `pr.any.balloon-dock` — 2 floors: Dock → Balloon deck. **Security:** nets (movement hazard), pit traps.
**Objectives:** *Primary:* recover the stolen Pokémon from the balloon's hold. *Secondary A:* cut the balloon's tether. *Secondary B:* defeat Butch and Cassidy.
**Key scene:**
> **BUTCH:** "That's our line!" **CASSIDY:** "No, it's mine!"
> *(Battle: Butch and Cassidy, double battle; Trait: **Rivalry**: when one is KO'd, the other's Attack rises +1; announced.)*
**Results (Lever: *The Hold*):**

| Result | Immediate | Climax effect | OS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Triumph** | The hold opens; all stolen Pokémon are freed | **Hold open:** Phase 1 −1 battle | −3 |
| **Success** | Most are freed | Phase 3 timer +1 | −2 |

### 8.3 Side faction kits (Snag'em in regions with an evil team)

Seeded at 50%. One short **side-front** of 1–2 nodes, resolved before the climax:

- **The Machine on the road.** A Snag'em outpost sits on a route between nodes. Defeating it grants Intel +3 and removes one of the primary team's Shadow-method perks. Failure: a Shadow Pokémon joins the primary team's Phase 1.
- **The Backer's Man.** A Snag'em officer approaches the primary team's leader offering the Machine. If the player intercepts, the primary team's OS −1; if not, OS +1 and a new Shadow method is available to the primary team.
- **Wes sighting.** A defector in a dark jacket (Wes, NPC only) shares a Machine schematic. Taking it gives the player a one-time ability to purify a Shadow Pokémon (D5), refused by the team that built it.
