## 3. Story Overview

**Logline:** A beloved Chairman is about to prove his energy plan with a catastrophe that only he can stop. A fan club on the side is making everyone's day slightly worse.

**Backstory.** Rose built the Galar region's energy system and then discovered that it would not last. His answer was Eternatus, a Pokémon whose arrival would force everyone to depend on his solution. He planted young talent (Bede), stalled the League's investigation and funded the Gym Challenge.

**The twist.** The Darkest Day is not an accident he hopes to avert; it is a product launch. The Chairman has already written the press release for the solution.

**Climax arc:** Eternatus is awakened at the Energy Plant and Dynamax pulses blanket the region. The player must climb Rose Tower, cut the pulses and stop a Chairman who has never lost a negotiation.

### 10.9 Resolution — The Red Star

Eternatus slumps, the red star dims, and the Chairman stands alone on the roof.

**Choice:**
1. **[Return Eternatus to the Weald]** — The great Pokémon is sealed back where it came from. *Effects:* Peacemaker Emblem; `legend-eternal-crisis` resolved.
2. **[Hand the Plant to the League]** — The Energy Plant is placed under League control. *Effects:* region reputation +15; the energy crisis is addressed honestly.
3. **[Let Rose face the cameras]** — The Chairman is left to explain himself on live broadcast. *Effects:* Macro Cosmos collapses; a later event: Rose reappears as a consultant.

**Rose (if Eternatus is returned):** "Well. I suppose that's one way to end the day."
