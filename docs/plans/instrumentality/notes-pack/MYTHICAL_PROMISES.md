# MYTHICAL PROMISES

**Goes with:** `LEGENDARY_BLESSINGS.md` (Legendaries) and `ULTRA_BEAST_INCURSIONS.md` (Ultra Beasts).
**What this is:** a separate system for the 23 Mythical Pokémon in your Pokédex. A Legendary gives a blessing at the start of a run whether the player wants it or not. A Mythical does nothing of the kind. It has to be **believed in** first; then it **comes to the player** in its own time; it **asks for a promise**; and what it leaves behind lasts **beyond the run**.
*Numbers are starting values. Nothing here has been playtested. The ten Mythicals that were blessings in the first version of the blessings document have moved here, with their effects kept.*

---

## 1. How a Mythical differs from a Legendary

| | Legendary Blessing | Mythical Promise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When | Start of every run | Mid-run, rarely, if at all |
| Chosen by | The game | The player says yes or no |
| Requires first | Nothing | Its story heard three times, across runs |
| What it costs | Nothing (or a Price) | A rule the player must keep |
| The Pokémon | Caught like any other, if found | **Cannot be battled or caught.** It travels as a Guest |
| If it goes wrong | Nothing | It leaves |
| What lasts | Nothing after the run | A **Keepsake**, permanent on the save |

---

## 2. The five steps

### 2.1 Tellings

A Mythical does not exist on a save until its story has been heard **three times**.

- Tellings come from storytellers at rests, books in the Museum, murals, festival songs, a rival's aside, the old woman in *The Storyteller's Road*.
- Each Telling is a few lines. The three do not agree. One detail in one of them is wrong, and the player finds out which only by meeting it.
- The **Storybook** in the hub holds a page per Mythical. A page with three Tellings is *believed*.
- A Telling can be heard in any run. At most two new Tellings are offered per run.

### 2.2 The Visit

Once believed, a Mythical may visit.

- Each has a **Comes when**: something the player must have done in this run. Meeting it makes a visit possible.
- Then: 10% per region. Each Telling heard beyond the third adds 3%, to 25%.
- **One Mythical per run.** Never the same one two runs running.
- The Visit is a scene, not a battle. No Ball works. It cannot be attacked.

### 2.3 The Promise

It asks one thing of the player, for the rest of the run. The rule is stated exactly, and the game shows a small mark by the party whenever an action would break it.

- **Decline:** it leaves a small gift and goes. No harm.
- **Accept:** it stays as a **Guest**.

### 2.4 The Guest and its Wonder

- A Guest is a seventh companion outside the party. It walks behind the player on the map, sits at rests, and appears in events.
- While the Promise holds, the player has its **Wonder**: a power stranger than a blessing, usually with charges.
- **If the Promise is broken, it goes at once.** There is no punishment beyond losing it. Its Storybook page loses one Telling, so it must be heard of once more before it will come again.
- Each Promise says whether the Mythical **forgives once**.

### 2.5 The Parting

At the end of a run in which the Promise was kept to the last node, win or lose:

- The Mythical says goodbye and leaves a **Keepsake**: a small, permanent effect on the save, kept in the hub.
- **Kept twice:** the Keepsake improves.
- **Kept three times:** it offers to **stay**. From then on, once per run, the player may bring it as a real party member (one Mythical party member per run; all normal rules apply to it).

## 3. The 23

Each entry: when it **comes**; the **Promise**; the **Wonder**; the **Keepsake**.

### Mew — The Ancestor
- **Comes when:** the player has caught 20 different species this run.
- **Promise:** *Bring everyone.* No two Pokémon in the party may share the same pair of types. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** every catch knows one extra move, chosen from three. Once per stage Mew *transforms* and stands in for a fainted party member for one battle, as a copy of it at full HP.
- **Keepsake:** a bubble. In every future run the first Pokémon caught knows one extra TM move.

### Celebi — The Voice of the Forest
- **Comes when:** the player has planted, mended or healed something in a forest stage.
- **Promise:** *Harm no wood.* Knock out no wild Grass- or Bug-type. Catching and fleeing are fine. Forgives once.
- **Wonder (your design):** once per region, recover a lost opportunity: an event failed or skipped this region returns. Once in the run, **revisit one entire previous stage** with the party's levels kept.
- **Keepsake:** a sapling. Every future run begins with one lost-opportunity token.

### Jirachi — The Wish Keeper
- **Comes when:** the player has declined three rewards this run.
- **Promise:** *Refuse no one.* Agree to every request made of the player in an event. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** three **Wishes** for the run. A Wish redraws any reward node, or names one item of that node's tier.
- **Keepsake:** a tag on the hub's tree. One Wish at the start of every run.

### Deoxys — The Visitor
- **Comes when:** the player passes a meteor or crater node having changed their lead Pokémon at least four times.
- **Promise:** *Change with me.* Never lead with the same Pokémon two stages running. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** four Formes. At each rest the party takes one stance: **Attack** (+15% damage dealt), **Defense** (−15% damage taken), **Speed** (acts first on turn one), or **Normal** (+5% to everything).
- **Keepsake:** a shard. Future runs begin with one free stance change usable mid-battle.

### Phione — The Drifter
- **Comes when:** the player has a Water-type at High Bond on a sea stage.
- **Promise:** *Take me along the coast.* Whenever a path by water is offered, take it. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** wild Water-types never flee, and one current per region carries the player past a node.
- **Keepsake:** a sea-glass egg. Water starters begin with more Bond. **Keeping Phione's Promise counts as two Tellings of Manaphy.**

### Manaphy — The Sea's Prince
- **Comes when:** Phione's Promise has been kept on this save, and the player reaches open sea.
- **Promise:** *Leave no one.* Release no Pokémon this run. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** every Pokémon caught starts with more Bond. Once per region, *Heart Swap*: trade places with a rival on the map, reversing their Lead.
- **Keepsake:** a blue stone. Every catch in every future run starts one Bond step higher.

### Darkrai — The Nightmare
- **Comes when:** the player has passed two rests in a row without stopping.
- **Promise:** *Keep away from them.* Use at most every second rest node. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** at the start of every battle the opposing lead loses 1/8 of its HP. Once in the run, *the long night*: walk past every ordinary trainer in one stage.
- **Keepsake:** an empty frame. In future runs, sleep never holds the player's Pokémon longer than one turn.

### Shaymin — The Gratitude
- **Comes when:** the player has ended two events generously in a flowering or grassland stage.
- **Promise:** *Take no payment for kindness.* Whenever an event offers a generous ending, choose it. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** every reward declined comes back a region later, improved. Once per region, make a ruined place bloom: one event that ended badly is re-opened.
- **Keepsake:** flowers in the hub. The starter begins every run with more Bond.

### Arceus — The Original One
- **Comes when:** **ten other Promises** have been kept on this save. It appears at the last rest before the League.
- **Promise:** *The Judgment.* It is for the **next** run, not this one. That whole run is played under three commandments it names (drawn from the Promises the player has already kept). Does not forgive.
- **Wonder (during the judged run):** the starter carries a **Plate** and is that Plate's type, changeable at rests; and once, the player may rewrite one rule of the run (raise every level cap by 3; make revival as common as it used to be; double Epic encounters; set every rival's Lead to zero; undo one death).
- **Keepsake:** the Azure Flute. **At the start of every run from now on, the player chooses their Legendary Blessing** from those discovered.

### Victini — The Victory Star
- **Comes when:** the player has won ten battles in a row without a faint.
- **Promise:** *Never turn away.* Flee no battle and decline no challenge. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** in the first battle of every stage the player's moves cannot miss, and it pays double. Three times in the run, declare a battle *fated*: win without a faint and its reward is one tier higher.
- **Keepsake:** a singed charm. The first battle of every future run cannot be missed in.

### Keldeo — The Fourth Sword
- **Comes when:** the player has seen a sign of Cobalion, Terrakion or Virizion in any run, and has just lost a battle.
- **Promise:** *Fight your own battles.* Accept no assist from a rival or ally in battle. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** once per battle one of the lead's moves strikes the target's weaker defense. Keldeo itself gets visibly braver across the run.
- **Keepsake:** a small horn. Unevolved starters have +5% to all stats in future runs.

### Meloetta — The Melody
- **Comes when:** the player has won a performance event without a mistake.
- **Promise:** *Finish the song.* Never quit or skip a minigame or performance once begun. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** one free retry in every minigame; sound moves gain 10%; once per region, *retune* a Pokémon (change its nature and switch to any of its legal abilities).
- **Keepsake:** a music box. One retune per run, and the hub's music changes.

### Genesect — The Revived Hunter
- **Comes when:** the player has revived a fossil and visited a laboratory event.
- **Promise:** *No more machines.* Use no TM or technical item on any Pokémon this run. It was altered without being asked. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** at each rest fit a **Drive**: the lead's first move becomes Fire, Water, Electric or Ice. At the start of each battle the lead gains +1 to whichever attacking stat suits the opposing lead's weaker defense.
- **Keepsake:** a drive casing. That opening boost applies in the first battle of every future run.

### Diancie — The Jewel
- **Comes when:** the player has finished the Crystal Caverns without breaking a crystal they did not need.
- **Promise:** *Sell nothing.* Sell no item this run. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** once per region she makes a diamond: a copy of one held item the player owns.
- **Keepsake:** a pink diamond. Every future run starts with 10% more money.

### Hoopa — The Unbound
- **Comes when:** something of the player's has been stolen or tithed this run.
- **Promise:** *Let me play.* Once per stage it takes one random item. The player must keep at least three items in the bag for it to choose from. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** once per region, reach through a ring: name a type, and the next wild encounter is a Rare Pokémon of that type. Once in the run, open a ring to another journey: bring one Pokémon from the Rival Archive or the player's last run.
- **Keepsake:** a gold ring. Everything it took is returned at the Parting; and one item may be carried from each run into the next.

### Volcanion — The Steam
- **Comes when:** the player has rescued a mistreated Pokémon (*Cold Night's Rain*, the Smuggling Ring, the fight ring). It does not like people.
- **Promise:** *Finish what you started.* Every Pokémon rescued in an event must be taken in or found a home; never the ending that walks away. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** once per battle one Water move also burns its target. Fire moves used against the lead on its first turn do half damage.
- **Keepsake:** a brass valve. Rescued Pokémon join with more Bond in future runs.

### Magearna — The Soul-Heart
- **Comes when:** the player has repaired something (the Appliance Shop, the Museum's restoration, the lamp at Bill's Lighthouse).
- **Promise:** *Carry this for me.* One party Pokémon holds her bouquet and no other item, until the last stage, where it is to be given to someone. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** whenever any Pokémon faints, on either side, the player's active Pokémon gains +1 Sp. Atk, to +2 per battle.
- **Keepsake:** a gear. One free repair of a broken or used-up item per run.

### Marshadow — The Shadow at Your Heels
- **Comes when:** the player has won at least three runs on this save. It has been in their shadow for some time.
- **Promise:** *Do not look back.* Retry no lost event battle, undo no choice, and revisit no stage. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** in each battle, the first stat boost an opponent gains is stolen and given to the player's active Pokémon.
- **Keepsake:** the player's shadow in the hub has eyes. The theft applies in boss battles in every future run.

### Zeraora — The Lightning That Walks Alone
- **Comes when:** the player has taken a wild Pokémon's side against people (*The Warning*, *The Drought Ranch*, *The Second Nest*).
- **Promise:** *No more cages.* Catch nothing more this run. The team is the team. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** once per battle the lead's next move becomes Electric and gains 20%. It joins every raid and giant-Pokémon battle as an ally.
- **Keepsake:** a scorched scarf. It assists in the first raid of every future run.

### Meltan — The Hex Nut
- **Comes when:** the player is carrying five or more metal held items.
- **Promise:** *Feed me.* Give it one held item per stage. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** each item fed adds a Meltan to the line trotting behind the player, and each gives the party +2% Defense. **At eight, they merge**, and Melmetal's Promise is offered on the spot.
- **Keepsake:** a Mystery Box. One random Steel-type held item at the start of every run.

### Melmetal — The Lord of Iron
- **Comes when:** eight Meltan have merged this run, or Meltan's Promise has been kept three times.
- **Promise:** *Forge; do not hoard.* At the end of each region, give the most valuable held item the player owns to someone in that region. Does not forgive.
- **Wonder:** the lead's first contact move each battle strikes twice at 60% power. Once per region it forges a held item: choose from three.
- **Keepsake:** an anvil in the hub. Every future run starts with one Uncommon held item of the player's choice.

### Zarude — The Rogue of the Jungle
- **Comes when:** the player hatched or adopted a young Pokémon this run and enters a jungle or forest stage.
- **Promise:** *It is yours now.* The youngest Pokémon in the party must never faint. Forgives once.
- **Wonder:** after every battle the party recovers 10% HP and is cured of status. Vines give one shortcut in every forest stage.
- **Keepsake:** a pink scarf. Hatchlings begin at a higher level in future runs.

### Pecharunt — The Peach That Gives
- **Comes when:** the player has accepted three gifts this run from people they had reason to distrust.
- **Promise:** *Accept every gift.* Take whatever is offered to the player, by anyone, for the rest of the run.
- **Wonder:** once per region its mochi turns one trainer who is not a boss into an ally for a battle. The party's poison is always bad poison.
- **The catch:** this is the one Mythical that is lying. At the Parting it offers one last gift, and it is a chain. **The Promise is truly kept only by breaking it then.** A player who takes the last gift completes the run with Pecharunt in charge of the Hall of Fame screen and gets no Keepsake.
- **Keepsake:** a peach stone. The player can never again be charmed, bribed or commanded by an event; and the three Wish blessings (Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti) lose their hold: their Rites become free.

## 4. How Promises meet the other systems

- **Blessings:** a Promise and a blessing can be held together. A Rite cannot keep or break a Promise for the player.
- **The Ideal (Zekrom)** is a vow too. The two do not merge: break one and the other stands.
- **Whole Run Events:** a Guest reacts in every beat. In *Guidance of Giratina* the Avatar cannot see a Guest, and it unsettles him.
- **Rivals:** a rival who sees the Guest says so, by personality, and the Rival Report never shows it.
- **Second Chance:** the Guest is beside the player too.

## 5. Data and tests

**Per Mythical:** `id`, `title`, `tellings[3]`, `comesWhen`, `promise` (a rule the game can check), `forgivesOnce`, `wonder`, `keepsake[3]`.
**Per run:** `guest`, `promiseKept`, `forgivenessUsed`, `wonderCharges`.
**Per save:** `storybook{}` (Tellings per Mythical), `promisesKept{}` (count per Mythical), `keepsakes[]`, `stayed[]`.

Tests:

1. No Mythical visits before three Tellings.
2. At most one Guest per run, and never the same Mythical two runs running.
3. Every Promise is a condition the game can evaluate; the warning mark shows before any breaking action is confirmed.
4. Breaking a Promise removes the Guest and nothing else.
5. A kept Promise always grants its Keepsake, win or lose.
6. Arceus cannot visit before ten other Promises are kept.
7. Melmetal can only be reached through Meltan.
8. Pecharunt's Keepsake is granted only when the last gift is refused.
9. No Mythical can be battled, caught or traded before its third kept Promise.
10. No Promise can make a run unwinnable: each can always be broken by choice, at the cost of the Guest.
