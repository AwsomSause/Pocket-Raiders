# LEGENDARY BLESSINGS

**Builds on:** `Legendary_Blessing_Framing.md` and the Blessings section of `Stuff_2.md`.
**What this adds:** the full opening sequence with timings, odds for the five tiers, Influence with thresholds, the rules of Communion, and a roster of **71 blessings: one for every Legendary in your Pokédex**, each with its object, an original passage, its effect, the signs it leaves through a run, and what changes when the player obtains the Legendary. Your two blessings (Time Keeper, Space Traveller) are kept as you wrote them and given exact rules. Mythical Pokémon, your Celebi example among them, have their own system in `MYTHICAL_PROMISES.md`; Ultra Beasts have theirs in `ULTRA_BEAST_INCURSIONS.md`.
*Numbers are starting values. Passages are original; none quotes scripture.*

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

A Legendary has taken an interest in this journey. It has not chosen the player, and it may not approve of them: some protect, some test, some have reasons of their own. The **Blessing** is a small share of what that Legendary can do, held from the first step of the run. **Communion** is what happens if the player actually obtains it: the blessing becomes something new, and the player gains one act they can perform **once in the whole run**. The blessing lends influence over a domain; Communion hands over the authority.

---

## 2. The opening sequence

Plays immediately after starter selection. About 25 seconds; it cannot be skipped on a save's first run and can be skipped afterwards.

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | The starter-selection screen fades fully to black. Two seconds of silence. |
| 0:02 | The blessing's **sound** begins, quietly: ticking, surf, wind in a bell. |
| 0:05 | Its **object** fades up in the centre of the screen, lit from one side, turning slowly. |
| 0:09 | The object begins to glow. The Legendary's **cry** plays once, from far away. |
| 0:11 | The **title** appears in capitals, then the **passage**, a line at a time. |
| 0:19 | "*[Legendary]* has blessed your journey." |
| 0:21 | The blessing's two-line summary: a headline in capitals, then one plain sentence. |
| 0:25 | Fade into the opening gameplay. |

**Presentation rules**

- Each blessing has its own sound and its own background (drifting sand for Dialga; a slow fold in the dark for Palkia).
- **Divine and above** add something: the object casts a shadow that is the Legendary's outline.
- **Primordial and Origin** interrupt the sequence. The sound cuts out; the screen holds black for three seconds longer than is comfortable; the passage appears all at once.
- The summary always tells the player **what they can now do**, in one sentence. Details live in the pause menu.
- The Legendary is never shown.

**Your Dialga text, kept exactly:**

> **THE TIME KEEPER**
> "And from the beginning, time was set into motion. Yet even the river of eternity may be made to flow backward, for the Keeper remembers what has passed."
> *Dialga has blessed your journey.*
> **TIME BENDS TO YOUR WILL**
> The next five turns need not be your last.

## 3. Tiers and how a blessing is chosen

| Tier | Chance | What it means | Starting Influence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lesser | 45% | A small, steady advantage | 2 |
| Greater | 30% | A noticeable change to how the run plays | 2 |
| Divine | 17% | A mechanic the run is built around | 1 |
| Primordial | 6.5% | A rule of the run is different. **Always has a Price.** | 1 |
| Origin | 1.5% | One of a kind. **Always has a Price.** | 0 |

- **One blessing per run.** It cannot be refused, swapped or lost.
- **Rarer is not better.** Lesser blessings are simple and always on. Primordial and Origin blessings change the rules in both directions. A player who gets Tapu Bulu has had a good day.
- **Evenness.** Within a tier, blessings are dealt from a shuffled bag, so the player sees each one before any repeats.
- **Discovery.** A new save can draw from 10 blessings (four Lesser, three Greater, both of yours, and Ho-Oh). The rest join the pool as the player finds their Legendary's signs in any run. The **Reliquary** in the hub shows every blessing seen and a blank plinth for each one not yet met.

## 4. Influence

Influence is hidden. It measures how far the Legendary's attention has turned toward the player.

| The player | Influence |
| --- | --- |
| Completes a related event | +1 |
| Makes a choice the Legendary would make (each blessing lists its **Aligned** choices) | +1 |
| Discovers a related location | +1 |
| Completes its special challenge | +2 |
| Defeats its guardian | +2 |
| Ignores or refuses any of these | no change |

There is **no penalty** for ignoring a blessing. Influence never falls.

| Influence | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | **Signs:** small related events are a step more likely |
| 3–4 | **Echoes:** the Legendary's shadow, a distortion, an inscription, an NPC changed by it |
| 5–6 | **The Trial:** its special challenge can appear |
| 7–8 | **Recognition:** the Legendary knows the player; its encounter can appear, and it may offer a test instead of a battle |
| 9+ | **The Presence:** the encounter is likely, and the Legendary may come to the player |

Chance per region that the Legendary's own encounter is placed:

| Influence | 0–2 | 3–4 | 5–6 | 7–8 | 9+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chance | 4% | 12% | 25% | 45% | 65% |

It is never guaranteed. And finding it is never "a Legendary in a random room": every blessing has several kinds of encounter, and the Presence is only the last of them.

## 5. Communion

When the player **obtains the Legendary of their own blessing**, three things happen in one scene:

1. The Legendary joins.
2. The blessing changes into its **Communion** form: a new mechanic, never just a bigger number.
3. The player gains one **Rite**: an act usable once in the entire run.

Rules:

- A Legendary obtained **without** its blessing is a strong Pokémon and nothing more.
- A Legendary in the party obeys every normal rule: the level clamp, the anti-sweep rules, one Mega per battle.
- A Rite can be used in any battle or on any map screen where it makes sense, including the Champion's. Once used it is gone, and the pause menu shows the object cracked.
- If the Legendary is released, lost or taken, the Communion form stays; the Rite, if unused, is lost.

## 6. The roster

**One blessing for every Legendary: 71 in all.** Each entry gives the **object** and **sound**; the **passage**; the **Blessing**; a **Price** where there is one; **Aligned** choices; the **Signs** it leaves; and **Communion** (the new form, then the Rite).

Mythical Pokémon do not give blessings. They make **Promises**, in `MYTHICAL_PROMISES.md`. Ultra Beasts give nothing at all; they **arrive**, in `ULTRA_BEAST_INCURSIONS.md`.

Two blessings **grow** during a run: The Child of the Stars (Cosmog) and The Little Fist (Kubfu) each become a later blessing as the regions pass.

## 8. How blessings meet the other systems

- **The Cartographer** (new events): a prophecy from the Xatu can only be changed by a Rite.
- **Second Chance:** unaffected by blessings. The Time Keeper's rewind is offered first, since the loss has not yet been recorded.
- **Rivals:** a rival may have a blessing of their own, shown in the Rival Report as an object on their belt. Rivals never get a Rite.
- **Whole Run Events:** The Renegade and *Guidance of Giratina* know about each other. The Rainbow's Rite can undo that story's worst moment.
- **The Museum** (reworked events): its Legendary hall is worth 1 Influence when it displays the player's Legendary.

## 9. Data and tests

**Per blessing:** `id`, `legendary`, `tier`, `object`, `sound`, `passage`, `headline`, `summary`, `effect`, `price`, `aligned[]`, `signs[]`, `trial`, `communion`, `rite`, `starterNotes[]`.
**Per run:** `blessing`, `influence`, `blessingUsesThisStage`, `blessingUsesThisRegion`, `communion` (bool), `riteUsed` (bool), `choice` (for blessings that ask one).

Tests:

1. Every run has exactly one blessing.
2. Tier frequencies over 20,000 runs match section 3 within one point.
3. No blessing repeats until its tier's bag is empty.
4. Influence never decreases.
5. Legendary encounter chance matches the Influence table.
6. Communion triggers only for the blessing's own Legendary.
7. A Rite can be used exactly once.
8. The Time Keeper's rewind restores state exactly and never repeats a random roll.
9. The Space Traveller never moves a stage's final node.
10. Every Primordial and Origin blessing has a Price, and no Price can make a run unwinnable.
