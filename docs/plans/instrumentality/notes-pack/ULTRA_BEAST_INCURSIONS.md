# ULTRA BEAST INCURSIONS

**Goes with:** `LEGENDARY_BLESSINGS.md` and `MYTHICAL_PROMISES.md`.
**What this is:** the third system, for the 11 Ultra Beasts in your Pokédex, and the strangest of the three. A Legendary blesses. A Mythical asks. **An Ultra Beast is not interested in the player at all.** It comes through a hole in the sky because of *how the player has been playing*, it brings a rule from its own world that applies to **everyone**, it can be followed home, and a save that deals with them is changed for good.
*Numbers are starting values. Nothing here has been playtested.*

---

## 1. The three systems side by side

| | Legendary | Mythical | Ultra Beast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrives | At the start, assigned | Mid-run, if believed in | Mid-run, **called by the player's own habits** |
| The player's say | None | Accept or decline | Enter the Tear, or wait for it to come out |
| What it changes | The player's abilities | The player's conduct | **The rules of battle, for both sides** |
| Can be obtained | Yes, by normal means | Only after three kept Promises | Only with a **Beast Ball** |
| A place of its own | No | No | **Yes: its home world, a stage type found nowhere else** |
| After the run | Nothing | A Keepsake | **Faller rank**, which changes every later run |

---

## 2. Appetites: why a Beast comes

The game keeps ten hidden tallies. Each belongs to one Beast (Naganadel has none of its own) and counts one kind of **excess** in how the player plays. Nothing random decides which Beast appears: it is the one whose Appetite the player has fed most.

| Appetite | Counts | Beast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dependence** | Battles in which one Pokémon did more than 70% of the damage | Nihilego |
| **Strength** | Knockouts that did at least twice the target's remaining HP | Buzzwole |
| **Flawlessness** | Battles won without taking damage | Pheromosa |
| **Charge** | Stat-raising moves used | Xurkitree |
| **Patience** | Battles of twelve turns or more | Celesteela |
| **Precision** | Critical hits landed | Kartana |
| **Consumption** | Items used, bought or sold; Pokémon released | Guzzlord |
| **Formation** | Battles in which all six party members took the field | Stakataka |
| **Spectacle** | One-hit knockouts with moves of 120 power or more; self-fainting moves; performances won | Blacephalon |
| **Longing** | Pokémon lost, released or given up; stages spent with two or fewer in the party | Poipole |

- Each Appetite has a threshold (section 7). When one is crossed, contact begins.
- The player is never shown the tallies. Looker, if met, will say what he thinks is drawing it. He is usually right.

## 3. Contact, in four stages

### 3.1 Static

For one stage before anything opens, the world slips. A patch of sky is the wrong colour. The music loses a note. Wild Pokémon stand facing one direction. A map node flickers. If the player has met Looker, he calls.

### 3.2 The Tear

A **Tear** appears as a node on the stage map: a crack with a different sky behind it. Entering it is optional.

While a Tear is open, the Beast's **Ultra Rule** leaks into the whole stage. It applies to every battle there, to the player and to every opponent alike. An Ultra Rule **overrides** the game's ordinary limits where they conflict, including the anti-sweep rules. That is the point of it.

### 3.3 The Crossing

Entering the Tear leads to the Beast's **home world**: a short stage (four to six nodes) that exists nowhere else, where its Ultra Rule is at full strength and a second, local law applies. **Ultra Shards** are found there. The Beast is at the end, at home, and not hostile until approached.

### 3.4 The Incursion

If the Tear is left alone for a full stage, the Beast comes out. It appears on a node the player must pass, wrapped in an **Ultra Aura** (+1 to all its stats), with **Beast Boost** (+1 to its highest stat each time it knocks something out). It is confused and frightened, and it is the hardest wild battle in the game. Driving it off sends it home. Ordinary Balls barely work on it (section 4).

## 4. Beast Balls and the Task Force

- Ordinary Poké Balls work at one tenth of their usual rate on an Ultra Beast.
- **Beast Balls** come from the **UB Task Force**: Looker and Anabel, who run it out of a rented room and are short of everything. After the player's first Static they appear at the next rest and hand over **one**.
- More are forged from Ultra Shards: three Shards for one Ball.
- The Task Force also gives **missions** (observe a Tear without entering; drive a Beast off without knocking it out; bring back a Shard from each world). Missions pay in Balls and in information about Appetites.
- Anabel is a **Faller**. She came through a Tear herself, long ago, and does not remember from where. She is the one who explains section 6.

---

## 5. Holding a Beast

A captured Ultra Beast is not tame and gives no blessing. Three things come with it.

1. **Its Ultra Rule goes wherever the player goes.** Every battle the player fights from then on is fought under it. Both sides.
2. **Beast Boost.** In battle it gains +1 to its highest stat for each knockout. The Rally rule does not apply against it.
3. **Homesickness (0 to 10).** It rises by 1 each stage and falls when the Beast is given something that reminds it of home (each entry says what). At 10 it tears a hole and leaves, and it takes its Ultra Rule with it.

### Return or keep

At any Tear, and at the end of the run, the player may send it home.

- **Return it:** the Faller's Log gains its page, and the save gains its **Souvenir**: a small permanent thing in the hub.
- **Keep it to the end:** the run was stronger for it, and the save's **Faller rank** rises by 1.

---

## 6. Faller rank

Something that has been through a Tear smells of it, and the Beasts can tell. Faller rank belongs to the save and never falls.

| Rank | What changes in every later run |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | No Beast can appear until one run has been completed |
| 1 | Appetite thresholds are 10% lower. Static may be noticed by rivals |
| 2 | Tears show a glimpse of the world behind them |
| 3 | **Two** Beasts may come in one run. Their Ultra Rules stack |
| 4 | Wild Pokémon near a Tear may carry an Ultra Aura |
| 5 | **Ultra Megalopolis** opens: a world with no light, where Necrozma is. Poipole may be given to the player there |

Returning Beasts does not raise the rank. A player who only ever sends them home stays at 0 and still fills the Log.

## 7. Limits

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Earliest stage for Static | Stage 3 |
| Beasts per run | 1 (2 at Faller 3 or above) |
| Appetite thresholds | Tuned so a Tear opens in about one run in four at Faller 0 |
| Never | During a Whole Run Event beat; in the same stage as a Mythical's Visit; in the Champion's stage |
| Setting | The whole system can be switched off |

## 8. The eleven

Each entry: its **world**; its **Appetite**; its **Ultra Rule**; the **law of its world**; what it is **homesick for**; its **Souvenir**.

### UB-01 Symbiont — Nihilego
- **World:** Ultra Deep Sea: a dark cave of floating stone and glassy drifting shapes.
- **Appetite:** Dependence.
- **Ultra Rule, *Host*:** at the start of every battle each side's lead is **possessed**: +1 to all stats, and it loses 1/16 of its HP every turn. A Pokémon that switches out is released.
- **Law of its world:** nothing fights alone: every battle is a Double Battle, and partners share damage.
- **Homesick for:** being carried. Homesickness falls when it holds an item or shares the field in a Double Battle.
- **Souvenir:** a glass bell. It rings in the hub when a Tear will open in the coming run.

### UB-02 Absorption — Buzzwole
- **World:** Ultra Jungle: a volcanic rainforest where everything is posing.
- **Appetite:** Strength.
- **Ultra Rule, *Flex*:** contact moves heal the user for a quarter of the damage dealt. Special moves do 10% less.
- **Law of its world:** the higher Attack acts first. Speed is ignored.
- **Homesick for:** a worthy opponent. Homesickness falls with every boss or rival battle won.
- **Souvenir:** a dumbbell. The hub gains a strength contest that pays a small prize once per run.

### UB-02 Beauty — Pheromosa
- **World:** Ultra Desert: white dunes and crystal, and nothing out of place.
- **Appetite:** Flawlessness.
- **Ultra Rule, *Untouchable*:** every Pokémon enters battle with +1 Speed and −1 to both defenses.
- **Law of its world:** whoever lands the first hit of a battle has a raised critical-hit rate for the rest of it.
- **Homesick for:** cleanliness. Homesickness falls after any battle in which it took no damage, and rises faster in polluted stages.
- **Souvenir:** a crystal comb. One Pokémon per run may be given a cosmetic shine.

### UB-03 Lighting — Xurkitree
- **World:** Ultra Plant: a forest of cable under a sky that never stops discharging.
- **Appetite:** Charge.
- **Ultra Rule, *Overload*:** every stat boost is doubled, and the boost ceiling is lifted. A Pokémon with any boost loses 1/8 of its HP at the end of each turn.
- **Law of its world:** each turn, lightning strikes one random Pokémon on the field: 1/8 damage, and +1 Sp. Atk.
- **Homesick for:** current. Homesickness falls in storms, power stations and Electric Terrain.
- **Souvenir:** a length of cable. The hub's lights can be turned up, and one shop restocks once per run.

### UB-04 Blaster — Celesteela
- **World:** Ultra Crater: a launch field of great scorched stalks, under heavy gravity.
- **Appetite:** Patience.
- **Ultra Rule, *Slow Burn*:** on the first two turns of a battle no hit can remove more than a quarter of a Pokémon's HP. From the sixth turn on, all damage is increased by half.
- **Law of its world:** gravity. Nothing flies or floats, and accuracy is raised for everyone.
- **Homesick for:** stillness. Homesickness falls at every rest the player takes, and rises when a rest is skipped.
- **Souvenir:** a seed pod. Planted in the hub, it grows one random Berry tree per run.

### UB-04 Blade — Kartana
- **World:** Ultra Forest: a wood of folded paper, where every edge is sharp.
- **Appetite:** Precision.
- **Ultra Rule, *Paper Cut*:** any hit on a Pokémon at full HP is a critical hit.
- **Law of its world:** everything is thin: all Pokémon have half their usual HP, and no move can miss.
- **Homesick for:** a clean cut. Homesickness falls each time it wins a battle in a single hit.
- **Souvenir:** a folded crane. Once per run it cuts one obstacle from a map.

### UB-05 Glutton — Guzzlord
- **World:** Ultra Ruin: a city very like one the player knows, a long time after. Nothing is left in it.
- **Appetite:** Consumption.
- **Ultra Rule, *Devour*:** after every battle the winner loses one random item from the bag, eaten, and the winner's party recovers 20% HP. Opposing trainers arrive with no held items: theirs are already gone.
- **Law of its world:** every node costs one item to enter. There is nothing to find but Shards, and one person in a protective suit who has been waiting to tell someone what happened here.
- **Homesick for:** being full. Homesickness falls when it is fed an item, and nothing else lowers it.
- **Souvenir:** a tin of something. The hub shop buys items back at a better price.

### UB Assembly — Stakataka
- **World:** the Rampart: one wall, in every direction, made of things that are looking at the player.
- **Appetite:** Formation.
- **Ultra Rule, *Many as One*:** turn order is reversed: the slowest acts first. Each side's active Pokémon has +5% Defense for every healthy Pokémon behind it.
- **Law of its world:** nothing can switch out, and nothing can be forced out.
- **Homesick for:** company. Homesickness falls while the party is full and healthy, and rises with every faint.
- **Souvenir:** one brick with an eye. The hub gains a wall where every completed team is recorded.

### UB Burst — Blacephalon
- **World:** the Midway: an endless fairground at night with nobody on the rides.
- **Appetite:** Spectacle.
- **Ultra Rule, *Finale*:** when any Pokémon faints it bursts: whatever knocked it out loses 1/8 of its HP. Moves of 100 power or more gain 20%, and their user takes 10% recoil.
- **Law of its world:** every battle has an audience meter. Repeating a move bores it, and a bored crowd lowers the user's stats.
- **Homesick for:** applause. Homesickness falls after every performance event and every one-hit knockout.
- **Souvenir:** a spangled ball. A fireworks show plays in the hub after every completed run.

### UB Adhesive — Poipole
- **World:** Ultra Megalopolis: a great city with its light taken, where they are fond of these and give them to children.
- **Appetite:** Longing. It is the only Appetite that is not an excess, and Poipole is the only Beast that does not invade. It **follows**.
- **Ultra Rule, *Cling*:** none that harms anyone. It attaches itself to one Pokémon of the player's choice: that Pokémon cannot be forced out, and earns Bond twice as fast.
- **Needs no Beast Ball.** It decides. It does not raise Faller rank.
- **Homesick for:** light, which its home has none of. Homesickness falls at every sunlit rest, and it would rather stay.
- **Souvenir:** a sticker on the hub wall, in the shape of the Pokémon it clung to.
- **It can grow.** If the Pokémon it clings to uses Dragon-type moves ten times, it evolves, and the next entry applies.

### UB Stinger — Naganadel
- **World:** Ultra Megalopolis.
- **Appetite:** none of its own. It arrives only as a grown Poipole, or wild at Faller 5.
- **Ultra Rule, *Beast Boost for All*:** every Pokémon on the field, on both sides, gains +1 to its highest stat whenever it knocks something out.
- **Law of its world:** as the city's: no light. Accuracy is lowered for everyone, and Dark- and Ghost-type moves gain 20%.
- **Homesick for:** nothing, if it grew up with the player. A wild one is homesick for the city, and nothing in this world lowers that.
- **Souvenir:** a stinger tip, capped. The player may bring one Poipole from a past run into a new one.

## 9. How Incursions meet the other systems

- **The Sun (Solgaleo)** lets the player open Tears on purpose and prevents ambush; **The Moon (Lunala)** shows what is behind a Tear; **The Child of the Stars (Cosmog)** can warp out of a home world at any node.
- **The Light Devourer (Necrozma)** is why Ultra Megalopolis is dark. At Faller 5, with that blessing, the player can give the light back. That is its Trial.
- **The Space Traveller (Palkia):** a rearranged stage may move a Tear's node, but cannot remove it.
- **Mythical Guests** will not enter a Tear. They wait outside, and they are visibly relieved when the player comes back.
- **Rivals:** a rival may enter a Tear first. The Rival Report then shows only static.
- **The Rainbow's Rite (Ho-Oh)** cannot bring back anything lost in a home world. It is too far.

## 10. Data and tests

**Per Beast:** `id`, `code`, `world`, `appetite`, `threshold`, `ultraRule`, `worldLaw`, `homesickFalls[]`, `souvenir`.
**Per run:** `appetites{}`, `contactStage`, `tearNode`, `ultraRulesActive[]`, `beastBalls`, `shards`, `heldBeast`, `homesickness`.
**Per save:** `fallerRank`, `fallersLog[]`, `souvenirs[]`, `megalopolisOpen`.

Tests:

1. The Beast that appears is always the one with the highest Appetite over its threshold.
2. No Static before stage 3, and none on a save with no completed run.
3. An Ultra Rule applies identically to both sides in every battle of its stage.
4. A Tear left for a full stage always produces an Incursion on a node the player must pass.
5. Ordinary Balls catch at one tenth of the normal rate; Beast Balls at the normal rate.
6. Homesickness reaches 10 in at most ten stages if nothing lowers it, and the Beast then leaves with its Rule.
7. Returning a Beast never raises Faller rank; keeping one to the end always does.
8. Two Beasts never appear in one run below Faller 3.
9. Poipole never raises Faller rank and never needs a Ball.
10. With the system switched off, no Appetite is counted.
11. No Ultra Rule can make a battle impossible to win: every Rule is tested with 5,000 simulated battles between matched teams, and neither side wins more than 60%.
