- Beats: battle fades to a bare blurred arena, Pokémon recalled into its caught ball, trainer (Nate/Hilda) walks in, Dynamax Band appears, ball turns pink, blue burst and trainer close-up, empty platform, pink ball drops and pulses, camera pulls back from the trainer while a white silhouette grows on pink, field darkens, bubble pops, red giant, fade to battle.
- The foe's Dynamax mirrors it. Wild Pokémon and Fast Dynamax keep the short scene. Tera scene and its trainer layering are untouched.
- Presentation only: no save change, no RNG.
- Checked by recording the game and comparing side by side with the reference at the same timestamps.
