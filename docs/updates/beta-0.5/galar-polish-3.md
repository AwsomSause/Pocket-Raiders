# Galar polish, round 5 (interviews and crowd voices)

Galar gym, tournament and Champion battles.

- A reporter does a **pregame interview** with a random character, and a **postgame interview** with a different one (win or loss).
- The crowd pool is now 67 everyday characters (no evil-team members). Shouts and interruptions show the speaker plus two friends in the shot.
- 48 shouts, 40+ interview questions: lines pick by what is true of the fight (who is ahead, matchup, low HP, last Pokémon, rival met, starter alive, badges, many wins).
- Tournament rounds get all of it except the survive-and-evolve rule (gyms and the Champion keep it).
