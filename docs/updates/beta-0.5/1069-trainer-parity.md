# Trainer parity part 1 (#1069)
**Player gets:** every trainer has a record (meetings, wins, reputation, ace, bond); their Pokémon get friendship, bonds and mastered moves like yours. Mastered moves hit 10% harder (you too), bonded trainer Pokémon can hold on at 1 HP, richer trainers carry more potions, returning trainers greet you from their record.
**Save:** none (`run.story.trainers` optional); old-save test in `tests/unit/trainerWorld.test.ts`.
**Limits:** follow-up #1078 adds specialization AI, kit items, protect/refuse-to-recall, adapting returns.
