# Galar: crowd sounds (#1084)
**Player gets:** Galar stadium battles now play real crowd sounds: applause and a stadium roar on big moments and a swell for the last Pokémon, a murmur in calm moments, silence when the crowd goes quiet. Muted or reduced-animation settings are respected.
**Save:** none.
**Limits:** sounds are short loops/one-shots, not a continuous crowd bed.

**Also (same PR):** the stadium camera cuts now have art drawn in code: crowd and fan-group silhouettes, challengers with caps, the private box, commentators at a desk, an opponent close-up, a spotlit champion silhouette and a rival at the rail, all inside a broadcast-style "LIVE / STADIUM CAM" frame. Crowd rows bob unless motion is reduced.
