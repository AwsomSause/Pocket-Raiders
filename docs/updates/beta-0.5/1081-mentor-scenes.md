# Beta 0.5: mentor scenes (#1081)
**Player gets:** Mentor's Last Resort also works in double battles, with a "Mentor ♥" button that plays a short cinematic where the mentor lends its strength; the graduation spar and a defeated Pokémon's return now play their own scenes (the return scene cannot be skipped).
**Save:** none.
**Limits:** scenes use existing sprites and simple effects, no new art or audio yet. Enemy trainers do not use mentors yet.
