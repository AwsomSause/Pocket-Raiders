# Evil Team: the Legendary fight follows the leader's defeat

- Beating an Evil Team's leader (hideout win or exposure) now goes straight from "THE BETRAYAL" into a wild, catchable battle with the Legendary the team chased. No reward screen or map in between (unless a move-learn or similar decision is pending).
- The team's "A Legend Stirs" stop is spent by this, so you never meet the Legendary twice.
- Level: the leader's highest-level Pokémon + 5, capped by the region.
- No save change (uses story flags `evil:betrayal` and `ev:evil-legend`).
