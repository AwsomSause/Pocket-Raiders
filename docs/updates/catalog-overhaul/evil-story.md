# Evil Team storyline

- **One Evil Team per region.** Every region now has its own team, and its story moves one step each stage: first rumours, evidence, being recognised, an ambush, the rising star, trouble inside, the lieutenant, The Plan, the Hideout, and a Legend stirring once they're beaten.
- **The Plan shows four Operations at once.** Stop them by force, find another way, or let them happen. Each says what it will do to the region before you choose, and what gets through stays (higher prices, a worse crisis, flags later events remember).
- **The Hideout is a full map.** Rooms, keys, guards you can talk past, the boss, their last words, and the Holding Pen, where every Pokémon the team took from you comes back.
- **47 Operation scenes.** After The Plan, one scene from the team's own pool turns up: Rocket's roadblock, Aqua's flood, Magma's drill, Plasma's holding camp (its own small map), Team Neo, and more. Walk on, and they may take one of your Pokémon to their Holding Pen.
- **Six last stories:** Biscuit the lost Yamper, coaching a kids' team for the Little Cup, the honest Magikarp salesman, the reverse wishing fountain, and the Long Table and the Last Door before the final battle.

Known limits: "(a node)" costs don't take a node yet; some consequences (Diglett leaving, sun on every road, no Poké Balls in shops) are remembered but not yet acted on by those systems. Team Rocket from inside is not in this update yet.
