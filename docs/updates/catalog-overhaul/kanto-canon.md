# Kanto Canon Spaces, rebuilt (catalog overhaul, `kanto-canon`)

Source: `catalog_overhaul_pack/kanto_canon_spaces/KANTO_PLACED_1.md` to `_6.md`. Every space is played as the docs script it: the lines are kept, the text around them is longer and warmer, and each space sits on the map where the doc puts it.

## What players get
- **Viridian, Pewter, Cerulean, Vermilion, Lavender (Part 1, 19 spaces).** The Old Man's throwing lesson, the rival at the Route 22 gate (and the last rematch there at Stage 8), the professor's parcel, the Trainer School's three questions, Diglett's Cave, Brock's stew, the Pewter Museum, the Pidgey flock, the Cerulean burglary, the Route 5 Day-Care, the Fan Club Fish, the Harbor Market, and Lavender in full: Pokémon Tower floor by floor with the Memorial Wall and the Marowak, Mr. Fuji, the Fan Club, Rock Tunnel in the dark, the Power Plant and Zapdos, the Volunteers and Haunter's prank.
- Pokémon Tower, Rock Tunnel, the Power Plant and Diglett's Cave are pseudo-stages: a drawn map inside one node, never an extra stage.
- Must-see spaces sit on every path of their step; the others sit inside the step window their doc gives. Some spaces come back at a later stage (the parcel's thief, the flock, the Day-Care pick-up).
- New small rules: the Day-Care keeps up to two Pokémon for a stage and returns them three levels up (two compatible ones bring an Egg); stat gains from the School and the stew; Fossil Fragments (two make a fossil); the Volunteers revive one fainted Pokémon out of battle; the Memorial Wall answers each name the run has lost with a Parting Gift (up to three).
