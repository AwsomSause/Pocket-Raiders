# Kanto Canon Spaces, rebuilt (catalog overhaul, `kanto-canon`)

Source: `catalog_overhaul_pack/kanto_canon_spaces/KANTO_PLACED_1.md` to `_6.md`. Every space is played as the docs script it: the lines are kept, the text around them is longer and warmer, and each space sits on the map where the doc puts it.

## What players get
- **Viridian, Pewter, Cerulean, Vermilion, Lavender (Part 1, 19 spaces).** The Old Man's throwing lesson, the rival at the Route 22 gate (and the last rematch there at Stage 8), the professor's parcel, the Trainer School's three questions, Diglett's Cave, Brock's stew, the Pewter Museum, the Pidgey flock, the Cerulean burglary, the Route 5 Day-Care, the Fan Club Fish, the Harbor Market, and Lavender in full: Pokémon Tower floor by floor with the Memorial Wall and the Marowak, Mr. Fuji, the Fan Club, Rock Tunnel in the dark, the Power Plant and Zapdos, the Volunteers and Haunter's prank.
- Pokémon Tower, Rock Tunnel, the Power Plant and Diglett's Cave are pseudo-stages: a drawn map inside one node, never an extra stage.
- Must-see spaces sit on every path of their step; the others sit inside the step window their doc gives. Some spaces come back at a later stage (the parcel's thief, the flock, the Day-Care pick-up).
- **Celadon, Fuchsia, Saffron, Cinnabar, Indigo (Part 2).** The Department Store floor by floor and its roof, the Game Corner, the Fuchsia Warden and Safari, Silph Co. and the Dojo, the Cinnabar Lab, the Mansion, Seafoam, Victory Road, the Badge Check, Cerulean Cave, and the route moments with Charizard, Pidgeotto, Bulbasaur, Haunter and Lapras.
- **More of every town (Parts 3 and 4).** From Viridian's Spearow to the Village at the Gate on the League's second fight.
- **Roads and the Sevii Islands (Part 5).** Eleven road spaces (the Song, Dark City, the Fossil Rush and its egg, the Hero, the Buried City and more). The Sevii Islands: Captain Tidewell's ferry from Cinnabar. One Island is a map, and each of the other six islands is a map of its own. You get three trips, plus Three Island free after the Missing Girl. Celio's Ruby and Sapphire, the Rocket Warehouse, Trainer Tower and the far islands (with their tickets) are all there.
- **The second helping (Part 6, 25 spaces).** The Kind Stranger, the Triage, the rival at the Tower, the Princess Festival, the Secret House, the Bicycle Race, the Gamblers, the Aides, Treasure Beach, the Resort, the Labyrinth and the Last Ferry home.
- New small rules: the Day-Care keeps up to two Pokémon for a stage and returns them three levels up (two compatible ones bring an Egg); stat gains from the School and the stew; Fossil Fragments (two make a fossil); the Volunteers revive one fainted Pokémon out of battle; the Memorial Wall answers each name the run has lost with a Parting Gift (up to three).

## Known limits
- Everything is listed in `docs/status/co-kanto-canon.md` ("Limits"). In short:
- Anything the docs carry across runs stays inside one run, because memory is same-run only.
- Some rewards are stand-ins: rests instead of Potions or a Full Restore, run flags instead of a few sellable valuables, and +1 level instead of "+1 every stat".
- Some places are smaller than the docs describe: the Lost Cave, Birth Island's puzzles, and the Vs. Seeker and Fame Checker dossiers.
- The Island of Giants, the forest, Mt. Moon and the other spaces owned by another thread are not in this set.
