# Catalog Overhaul: people

**What players get**
- **The Road Roster:** all 51 named route trainers now have a three-meeting story across a run (a region apart), with side scenes, battles and rewards. Finishing a trainer's story still recruits them as a playable trainer. Ten crossovers play when two related stories are both finished (Elliot and Kai's Good Rod, the Swim Club, a double-double battle, and more).
- **Mentors:** each of the twelve mentors has a Lesson (a real perk for the run), a Recruit, a Story with a choice that changes their Final Lesson, a Final Lesson battle and a farewell gift. Twelve shared mentor scenes fill the road between. Fellow apprentice: Ressa.
- **New mentor perks in battle and on the map:** own-type power, speed after a turn-one knockout, no flinching on turn one in boss fights, a boost when a foe misses, a preview of the foe's first move, XP from lost battles, held items that sharpen the first move, hidden items shown a few steps ahead, "worth it" marks and deals in shops, and a bonus for a Pokémon you keep from evolving.
- **Past Gym Leaders:** a Leader's day off (eleven Leaders, each with their own problem), the Fan Club, the Retired Leader and Pin Stall, the Hidden Dojo, a play about that night, and the Leader questlines (the First Gym's wings, the Abandoned Ace, the Two-Gym Feud, the Borrowed Badge, the Stolen Badge, the Rebel Gym, the Championship That Wasn't, the Tower Apprentice).
- **Past Champions:** Red on the mountain, Blue's lost afternoon, coffee with Cynthia, the Dragon Master's last flight, the Champion who left, the Hall of Past Champions and Victory Hall, the garage sale, the Champion in disguise, the legendary trainer's echo, the Champion's ghost, lunch with a Champion, the landmark delves, and seven travelling trainers.
- **The Trivia Mentor** now asks about your own journey and teaches a forgotten tutor move.

**Known limits**
- Beats that would carry across runs are not built (Kice's rule: memory stays inside one run).
- Some beats wait for an engine piece and are recorded as run flags for now (list: `docs/status/co-people.md`, Needs Kice).
- Battle perks "foe move preview" and "speed after a turn-one KO" are not wired for double battles; the move preview text shows in the Old UI only.
