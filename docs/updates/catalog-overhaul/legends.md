# Legendary, Mythical, Ultra and Alpha questlines, rebuilt (catalog overhaul, `legends`)

Source: `catalog_overhaul_pack/catalog_overhaul/QUESTLINES_ALPHAS_AND_ANOMALIES.md`, `QUESTLINES_LEGENDARY.md`, `QUESTLINES_MYTHICAL.md`, `QUESTLINES_ULTRA.md`, the 382 RULE ids in `CATALOG_INDEX.md`, and the notes `LEGENDARY_BLESSINGS.md`, `MYTHICAL_PROMISES.md`, `ULTRA_BEAST_INCURSIONS.md`.

## What players get
- **Alphas (A2 to A11).** Huge wild Pokémon with a reason they are angry. Each can be fought or calmed: a Calm bar fills with balms and the moves it likes, and a calmed Alpha may join with the Alpha Mark.
- **Anomalies.** Mega Anomalies (M1 to M6), Linden's Delta Study (D1 to D8, the Delta forms) and the Z Anomalies: the eighteen Island Trials with Totems and a Grand Trial, the Sigils, the Cold Ghost, the Spoon, Doran's Snorlax and the Alphayena with its new ability Pack Call.
- **Legendary questlines (L2 to L15).** The three birds' cliffs, Ho-Oh's feather and the Bell Tower, Lugia under the whirl, Mewtwo's lab, the Weather War, the Eon twins, the Regi doors, the Lake Trio and Spear Pillar, Truth and Ideals, Kalos's tree, Nebby, Necrozma, the Hero wolves and the Ruinous wards. With a Legendary's blessing at high Influence, it may recognise you and offer itself.
- **Mythical Visits (MY1 to MY22).** Hear three Tellings of a Mythical and its Visit scene can find you: Mew's games, Celebi's saplings, Jirachi's wish, Deoxys's signal, and nineteen more. Each ends with its Promise; make it and the Mythical walks with you as your Guest. One Mythical per run. Z5, Darkrai's Darkest Dream, can follow.
- **Ultra Beast questlines (U1 to U9).** Looker and Anabel's Task Force, Lusamine and the Ultra Deep Sea, Buzzwole's posing contest, the empty Midway, the Ruined City, the paper crane, the watching wall, Poipole, and the Hole in the Sky. They need the Ultra Beasts setting on.
- **Signs and Tellings.** Old landmark, shrine, ruin and rumour stops became the places Signs and Tellings come from. Signs raise your blessing's Influence; at high Influence its Legendary is much more likely to appear on your map (the Presence). Legend weeks start your matching blessing one step higher.

## Known limits
- Everything is listed in `docs/status/co-legends.md` (a Limits table per section). In short:
- Memory is same-run only: Keepsakes, Souvenirs, Arceus's Visit and anything from a past run are cut.
- Most Mythical Wonders and Promise rules are still the Beta 0.7 limits: only Zarude's Wonder and rule act in the game.
- Mega Anomaly M7 waits on the rivals' rival slot; A1, L1 and L16 are built by another thread.
- 117 RULE ids stay live on purpose (pseudo stages, the Shadow chain, past-run Archive pieces, rival scenes, keys and form items).

### Whole Run Events and Arceus
- About one run in three now follows one long story from Stage 2 to the Champion: Guidance of Giratina, the Weather War, the Made One, the Seven Days, or the Weapon and the Flower. Ten beats each, choices that change how it ends, and an ending that changes the League. Switch it off under Settings > Gameplay > Whole Run Events.
- Arceus (the Sixteen Pedestals) can now appear at the last rest before the League, if your Mythical Guest is still with you and you never broke its Promise.

### Calendar weeks, the Shadow chain and your reputation (batch 5, row C)
- Rivalry Week and Roadside Rival Week open the Rival Circuit without qualifying, and your rival waits for you inside every pseudo-stage (fight, team up, or walk past).
- League weeks (Gym Circuit, Champion's Gauntlet, Gym Leader Rematch Week, Elite Four Sweep, Champion Cup Weekend) bring the Past Gym Leaders and Past Champions onto the road more often.
- Legendary weeks: Raid Rally and World Boss Week bring the Alpha questlines, Dragon Week the dragon Legendaries' questlines, Legendary Beasts Chase sends Raikou, Entei and Suicune's gates. Mega Evolution Showcase, Mega Stone Hunt and Z-Move Trial bring the Mega and Z Anomalies.
- Team Plasma Liberation Operation makes Team Plasma the run's team (the other team weeks already did this for theirs).
- Paradox Tide: a Koraidon, Miraidon or Dialga blessing starts with 1 more Influence. Leap Day: one free Telling of a Mythical at run start.
- The Shadow chain is part of the Shadow system now: sightings stir up the factions, the Sanctuary will look after a Shadow Pokémon (yours or a caged one) and give it back purified, steadying really soothes the Heart Gauge, The Haze Lifts purifies, and the Lieutenant who escapes a hideout comes back for you. Shadow Triad only appears when Team Plasma is hunting you.
- "An Echo of What You Did": a stranger reads your reputation back to you, with the numbers.
- Known limits: Koraidon and Miraidon blessings are not in the game yet, so Paradox Tide's bonus lands only on Dialga today. The Beasts (L1) and Tower of Legends (L16) questlines are still the older week events, not rebuilt from QUESTLINES_LEGENDARY.md.
