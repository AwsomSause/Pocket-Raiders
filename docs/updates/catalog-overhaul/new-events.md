# New events (Catalog Overhaul row 17)

**What players get.** 95 brand-new events from the pack (NE1 to NE100, less the five trades), each with longer, warmer text and its own follow-up scenes: the Alpha who lost its voice, the Museum of You, Swap Day with your rival, a shop with no shopkeeper, the Road Home at the end of a run, and many more. Some Pokémon you meet carry new quirks, like Protector (takes a hit for a teammate once per battle) and Dreamer (a Poliwhirl that becomes a Politoed at level 36).

New rules for these scenes: one-on-one fights, a foe that endures until a set hit, clear-sky stages, and trading teams with your rival until the next Gym.

**Known limits.** See `docs/status/co-new-events.md`: the five trade events (NE61 to NE65) come with the markets update; anything that would carry to another run stays inside the run; a few scenes have no trainer art yet.
