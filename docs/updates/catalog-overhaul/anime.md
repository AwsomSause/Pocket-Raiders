# Catalog Overhaul: anime and Pokémon-anime questlines

**What players get**
- **95 questlines, 597 scenes:** the 36 anime-inspired stories (a bathhouse at the edge of night, a walking house, the crew of the Rusted Anchor, a letter writer with a Chatot, the Paper City, the Ice Library, a dungeon cook...) and 59 Pokémon-anime stories (Pikachu who won't go in its Ball, the Butterfree migration, the Charmander in the rain, the shiny Hoothoot, Chimchar and Paul, Ash-Greninja's bond, Rockruff's sunset, Gary's Eevee, Ritchie's Sparky...). AQ8 The Grand Prix is the Fighting Tournament (gn.16), as the pack settles.
- **Companion stories follow one Pokémon:** you meet it, small scenes come as its Bond rises, its turning point comes two stages later, and some end with a farewell you choose. The story pauses while it is out of the party.
- **Stage-Three, Region, Trial and Run-Long stories** come back later in the same run (a few nodes on, a few stages on, or one region on). Eight Doors are full chain pseudo-stages (the Bathhouse, the Paper City, the Ice Library, the Mushroom Queen, the Descent, the Dungeon Cook, Togepi Paradise, and AQ18's climb).
- **Rival stories** (Gary, Paul, Alain, Gladion, Jessie and James, Ritchie, and Paul in AQ23) use the stage's rival slot. Every story fight is friendly: losing never ends a run.
- Catalog ids `ash-training` (AQ1's turning point, Ash arrives late), `brock-cooking` (AQ43) and `lillie-nebby` (AQ51, "Snowy") are rebuilt inside their questlines.

**Known limits**
- Nothing carries across runs (Kice's rule): "later runs" afterstories and "on this save" variants are not built.
- Where the game has no exact mechanic, the nearest real one is used; each difference is listed per questline in `docs/status/co-anime.md`.
- 51 remembered flags are set by other threads' events (Kanto canon, greenneedle, legends, families, rivals); until those land, those variants simply don't show.
