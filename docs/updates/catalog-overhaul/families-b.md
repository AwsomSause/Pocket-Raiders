# Story Families, part 6

- **Six Doors you can walk into.** The Lost Expedition, the Sunken Lighthouse, the Old Quarry, the Captain's Chest (four breaths of air, and a compass that shows Water-type stops), the Whispering Grove and the Windswept Spire. Each is a small map inside a node, with its own art.
- **Stories that remember you.** The One Far From Home, the Light at the Harbour, the One That Beat You (the wild Pokémon that beat your team comes back for a rematch, and may join you), and Who Keeps Watch (pick who stands watch at night, and what it sees depends on its type).
- **Eight short chains, each over one stage.** The Sunken City, Whale Song, the Stone Giant, the Glass Forest, the Fox at the Shrine, the Midnight Circus, the Mirror Lake (your reflection's team is the Pokémon you passed by and never caught), and the Day Everything Slept.
- **Over 100 road stories rewritten.** Every two-step story's second step now remembers what you did in the first: the berry girl comes back selling jam, the backpack's owner finds his letter. Small trades say plainly what you give and what you get. Twenty-six three-step episodes, from the Long Letter to the Long Night at the Inn, each end with a line in your run's journal.
- **The Last Train.** On the date on your ticket, a silent train comes to a night platform. If you lost a Pokémon this run, you can say goodbye properly.

Known limits: anything the pack said comes back "next run" comes back later in the same run instead (no memory across runs). The Last Train needs the ticket from The Last Stop. The Strange Footprints Alpha is a strong wild battle for now.
