# Story families 9 and 10 (Catalog Overhaul row 4)

**What players get.** Every scene from Story Families 9 and 10, rewritten longer and warmer: Nix's barter board, the
abandoned nest, the statue garden, the Lapras crossing (a pseudo stage), the scarecrow, the clock tower, Hargrove the
old veteran, thirty rule-battle trainers ("How bad is it for me?" shows the prize multiplier), the Mystery Dungeon (a
pseudo stage of random rooms with Luck), the cursed idol, the Storyteller's torn pages, the Great Berry Festival, the
Black Market, the Clockwork Vault, the Trapped Crypt, three factions, the Dragon's three questions, the Oath Stone and
the Oracle's prophecies.

Also built from the Legendary and future docs: the eight Frenzied Nobles (calm them, don't beat them), the Three
Beasts with Thorne the hunter, seven more roaming Legendaries, the five-floor Tower of Legends, the First Gym's vault,
the Letter to Future You, the Sealed Letter, the Time Capsule, the Professor's Time Machine and Rainbow Rocket.

**New rules.** Signs of a Legendary raise your blessing's Influence. A roamer's trail is found at a chance shown on
the choice. A Noble fight has a Calm meter: every turn you don't hurt it, it calms.

**Known limits.** See `docs/status/co-families-c.md`. Anything that would carry to another run stays inside the run.
