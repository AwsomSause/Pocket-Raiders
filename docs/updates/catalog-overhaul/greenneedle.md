# Greenneedle events (catalog overhaul)

**All 100 Greenneedle events are in: the 62 reworked ones and 38 new ones.** Each one is placed on the map where the pack says (a step, a window, a kind of stage), plays with the pack's own buttons, and has longer, warmer text.

- Story places you walk into: Mt. Moon, the S.S. Anne, Bill's Lighthouse, Sabrina's Labyrinth, the Safari Zone, a haunted mansion, a sunken city, a prison break, a city under siege.
- Pokémon with their own stories: an Eevee who won't pick an evolution, a Magikarp that wants to win the jump, an angry Bidoof, a Cubone looking for its mother, a Pokémon that follows you home and is only seen at the edges.
- Towns and contests: a travelling market, a day-care village, a museum, a Battle Frontier, an auction, a draft, a 1v1 championship, a Magikarp convention, a Little Cup.
- Region-long stories that pay off later in the same run: the Slowpoke Detective Agency's three cases, a former Champion's advice, a fox shrine's lanterns, a mural you paint one panel at a time, an egg that hatches knowing your voice.
- A last-region finale: the Final Reunion and the Long Table bring back the people and Pokémon you met in this run.
- New abilities and moves for the Pokémon these stories give you (Unbothered, Fair Share, Trust Earned, Welcome Home and others), each working in battle.

Memory is this run only: nothing carries over from earlier runs.
