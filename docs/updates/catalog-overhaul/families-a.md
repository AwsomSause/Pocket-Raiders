# Story families, parts 1 to 4 and 7

- **The Region in Crisis.** Each region can fall into one crisis (blackout, flood, factory smog, fever, fire, quake or blizzard). It gets worse when you walk past, ends in a climax where two places need you at once, and the people you helped along the way turn up to help.
- **The Apprentice.** Tib, a kid who copies what you do, good or bad, and later mirrors you in a battle on a bridge.
- **Rivals, mentors and old friends.** Scenes that remember what you did earlier in the same run: who you stood with, who you walked past, what you promised.
- **The Circuit and contests.** Ranked ladders, a seven-floor Battle Tower, the Exhibition Bracket (its own three-room stage), the Champion Cup, and Contest ribbons that lead to a Grand Festival.
- **Long stories on the road.** The convoy you escort to the next gym, the Hollow Lantern's ghost festival, the Grunt School, a heist, a village stuck in a time loop, the Kubfu tower choice, the Storm-Grey Coat's legendary bird hunt, the courier's egg, the treasure map, and more.
- **Hazards with three ways through.** Twenty weather and terrain hazards: push on and pay, wait and lose a node, or let a Pokémon of the right type lead (it bonds with you). Ten answered hazards earn the Pathfinder ribbon.
- **The Kindness Ledger.** Small kindnesses (a baker, a nurse, a stranger in the rain) add up, and Hettie writes them all down.
- **Strangers, camp and small rescues.** Strangers worth stopping for, an hour at camp, Biscuit the borrowed Growlithe, lost pets, a fortune teller's cards, a quiet funeral, Kit and the empty shelf, the Riolu on the ledge, honesty tests, and an old Lapras who sings once more at the sea.

Known limits: placement on the new 20-step map comes from the catalog lead (the events use their old spots until then). A few costs are simplified, and each one is listed in `docs/status/co-families-a.md`. `starter-fever-week` is a game-event week and is left for whoever reworks the weeks.
