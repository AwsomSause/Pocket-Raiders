# Revised single events (catalog overhaul)

**Batch 1: map stops and shortcuts (33 events).** Berry Tree, Pokémon Center, Training Ground, Treasure Cache, Lookout Tower, the Balloon, the Raft, the Zip-Line and the rest now play as the pack scripts them, with longer, warmer text.
- Small kindnesses come back: leave Berries on the tree and Pell Junior finds you later; ask the night nurse how she is and the next Center has a free Revive.
- New choices: carry R. Tanner's savings home, add your tag under your rival's, free the Sudowoodo the Bonsly is guarding.
- New item: Wooloo Wool (sells for ₽200).

**Batches 2 to 4: rests, road people, comic encounters and wild scenes (98 events).**
- Rests are real rests now: a hot spring, a starry camp, an aurora night, a sea that glows. Pick who soaks first or who watches with you, and that Pokémon's Bond grows.
- Road people remember you: the birthday kid comes back stronger, the wedding couple names their baby Pokémon after you, the soap box kid grows up and opens a bike shop.
- A critic outside a cafe tells you your team's real weaknesses, and a mentor tells you how you really fight. A clerk can point you to a Move Tutor.
- Wild scenes show who is there before you choose: watch Berries being eaten, play hide-and-seek with a Wynaut, sit down to tea with Fairy-types (say please).
- New items: Tiny Mushroom, Mareep Wool (₽300), and a child's Toy Pikachu (the next wild Pokémon is too busy staring at it to move first).

**Batch 5: hidden paths, mystery places and ability secrets (35 events).**
- Wend the road-keeper tests your eye with three drawings; a gap between boulders cuts the stage in half.
- The Memory Fog takes up to two named items and gives them back, in order, a stage later. Three keys open a sealed vault.
- Write one line in the Unwritten Library, about the Pokémon you choose, and it becomes your run's epitaph.
- A ghost duels you with your own team; a ghost crowd teaches a move after three bouts; Pokémon with the right Ability open tunnels, doors and chasms for a named item each.

**Batch 6: challenges, shops and workshops (39 events).**
- The Winstrate family, the relay, the held-item gauntlet, Joey and his top-percentage Rattata, a moot court where every point you win shakes her lead.
- An Eco Ranger test with no knockouts allowed, a veteran's exam with no items, and a lifeguard test that starts with a real rescue.
- A smoothie cart, a gift you wrap for a stranger that comes back better, and a ₽1 sale after a terrible skit about the region's Legendary.
- No event fight ends a run any more: lose one, and your team gets back on its feet where you stood.

**Batch 7: study stops, the evil team's pressure, and puzzles (29 events).**
- Study halls, a replay theater and a veteran's notebook that show the next Gym's team before you get there; a graduation ceremony for the Pokémon that carried you.
- Grunts who plant contraband, sabotage your gadgets and send a ransom note; what you do about it is remembered for the rest of the run.
- A bingo card that fills as you see events, a crossword cave, a fossil puzzle that wakes a fossil Pokémon, three trivia signboards in a row with a TM behind the last hatch.

**Batch 8: puzzle gates (24 events).**
- Real puzzles with real answers: a Ditto hiding in a line-up, a safe that opens to three whistled notes, a lock gate you level with three pulls, four gems and two weighs.
- "Who's that Pokémon?" three times in a row, and the last silhouette is your own lead.
- A pinball table whose bonus lane is always the left ramp, a mirror maze where one reflection is a window, and a portal that skips two stops.

**Batch 9: famous faces, returns, milestones and quiet moments (38 events).**
- An Elite Four member cooks for you, practises with you, and teaches your lead her ace's move if you win.
- A scout lends you a Pokémon and comes back for it; a professor gives you a Protean Eevee and checks on it a region later; a reporter's three questions end up in the paper.
- Milestones ask how you want to travel: fast, careful, or rich. Quiet moments let your team rest, hold your hand, or dream.

Known limits: "confused" and "Charged" for one battle are a damage change, the balloon lands two stops ahead (you cannot pick the spot), the Wooloo rolls on any day, and a few "shown before you choose" previews describe the Pokémon without naming it. Full list: `docs/status/co-singles.md`.
