# Rivals (Catalog Overhaul row 12)

**What players get.** All 24 rival questlines from the pack, rebuilt scene by scene with longer, warmer text: the Circuit ladder, the rival in trouble, the promise, the stone on the hill, their mentor, the post-mortem, swapped teams, the fake lead, a bad day, three photos, two rivals arguing, the fellow apprentice, the stranger who follows you, emptied shelves, the sponsor's gift, Kieran, the last battle of the year, the rival as Champion, greetings between teams, a night at a storm-bound inn, one for the road, a race for first pick (a small pseudo-stage), and the name they call you.

New fight rules: Kieran's "Wants to Stop" meter (stall and his Pokémon sits down), and a midnight clock fight (whoever is ahead when it runs out wins). Losing any questline fight never ends a run.

**Known limits.** See `docs/status/co-rivals.md`: stops land when a stage starts rather than at an exact step; the race's rival marker and a few report screens are text only for now; anything that would carry to another run stays inside the run.
