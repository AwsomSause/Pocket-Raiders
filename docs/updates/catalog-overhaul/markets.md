# Catalog Overhaul: markets, trades and games

**What players get**
- **The Honest Market:** 29 stalls in town, each with honest prices, a story and a perk for regulars; services, a Training Ground with 27 drills, and a Game Corner where every game prints its odds.
- **Cons and front booths:** seven cons with visible tells, and the evil team's front companies. What a con takes can be found again in the next hideout's vault.
- **The Fairground:** one fair per region with 21 games, festival stops and a lost child to find.
- **Money with honest terms:** tolls with a free way round, an oasis, a guide, insurance, loans, a bank with interest, pawn shops, rentals, layaway, subscriptions, wagers, fossils and tutors.
- **The Honest Trade Card:** before every Pokémon trade you see both sides: species, level, nature, all four moves, ability, quirk, history and a fair value. The trader warns you when you'd lose out. Traded Pokémon arrive fond of you, keep their trainer's name, and trade-evolve on arrival.
- **Trades everywhere:** the Trading Post, eight terrain pools, five Collectors, altars, a draft pool, the Global Trade booth, 107 specific trades with their own traders and stories, two trade chains, and Ivo's free trade-back service.
- **Quirks:** traded Pokémon can hate rain, love rain, show off in Gyms, be gentle, hold a grudge, see better at night, or refuse strangers.

**Known limits**
- Some quirks are a line on the card only for now (Won't lead, Always leads, Steals Berries, Sings at night, Afraid of Balls, Collector).
- Trades with Pokémon from past runs are not built (memory stays inside one run). The rival's trade and Ranger Willa's lend are not built yet.
