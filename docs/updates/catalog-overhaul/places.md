# Catalog Overhaul: places

**What players get**
- **Twelve Delves and the Celestial Observatory** are now drawn pseudo-stages you walk through room by room, each with its own rule, hazards, a boss and an exit gift. The Sky Bridge, the Deep Ocean (watch your breath), the Volcanic Core, the Crystal Hollow and the rest.
- **Places you visit:** Safari Land (steps, bait, slow approach), the Battle Frontier (gate rules, a harder second bracket), the League Exhibition, the Lost Island and the Underwater Ruins, the S.S. Anne cabin chain, the Haunted Mansion (lamps, the cursed doll, the vault), the Slowpoke Detective Agency's two cases, the Sevii ferries, the Day-Care Village (leave a Pokémon, collect it later in the run) and the Egg That Lies Warm.
- **Six Legendary stages:** walk three rooms toward an original Legendary, then choose to approach, watch or leave it in peace.
- **Calendar weeks:** a crier on your first stage tells you what this week is; join in for the week's bonus. **Challenge weeks are now opt-in:** sign up at the board, keep the rule for the region, collect the payout.
- **Relics:** plant a seed of one of three lines (ember, tide, leaf), grow it through the run into a bough and a crown, or trade lines at the shrine.
- **Heated moments:** a last-second resolve, a new move, an overdrive or a mid-battle evolution earns a scene after the fight (one a region, three a run).
- **Run events:** weather and storm chasers, the Lab, auctions, fishing, 25 biome hazards (push on, wait, or use the right type), 8 volunteer jobs, deliveries, the winter set, reputation scenes, standalone choices and Pacts.
- **Single merges:** 70 old one-off events are rebuilt as scenes with fuller, warmer text (the Ghost Ship, the Ghost Train, the Cartographer, the Court of the Road, Marta's shop, the Egg Hunt, the Poacher Camp, the Mirror Trainer, the rerolls and more).
- **No event fight ends a run.** Every fight in these events is friendly: lose it and the party gets back up on the same node.

**Known limits**
- Beats that would carry across runs are kept inside one run (Kice's rule).
- Where a beat's home belongs to another row (a questline, the Honest Market, the Evil Team's defector arc), the beat plays its own scene and leaves a run flag that home can read (`src/data/catalogOverhaul/flags/places.json`).
- Some bonuses are close approximations of the pack's numbers; the list is in `docs/status/co-places.md`.
