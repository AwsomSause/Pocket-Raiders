# Kanto traits: the Roadblock and the Count Room

**Player gets:** Playing the Poké Flute at *The Roadblock* now wakes a Snorlax that heals 1/8 of its HP every turn until you knock its food away (Knock Off or Thief). A new Kanto event, *The Count Room* (Celadon basement), lets you ask about Jackpot or play Pit Boss Vesper for her: Vesper's Dratini doubles the prize money if it knocks one of your Pokémon out, and winning gives you a Dratini.

**Save:** no change.

**Limits:** Traits last for the battle only, so the Snorlax you catch and the Dratini you win do not keep Fed and Paid or Payout. Jackpot is won as an ordinary Dratini; the script's "dealer returns her" branch (Secondary A) is not built. The Count Room is a new event outside the pack's 80-event list.
