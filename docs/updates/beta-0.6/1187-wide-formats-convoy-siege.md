# Wide formats: Convoy and Siege Map (E4, 3 and 4 of 7)

**Player gets:** the Siege Map before the Kanto climax (the "Rocket Ledger" page: each district Held, Contested or Lost, pick a gate; Held is free, the rest cost one battle; Lost districts' effects are listed and applied), and the optional Long Drive Convoy (back-to-back fights, then LEDGER: fight it or call it a witness). Neither is reachable until the Extended Cut director (E5) starts them.

**Save:** none (state is in `run.expansion.cut` and `run.expansion.siege`).

**Limits:** Kanto only; no "Retake left" counter or ally sprites on the Siege Map; LEDGER does not yet join the party as a Pokémon. Details in `docs/status/wide-formats-convoy.md` and `wide-formats-siege.md`.
