# Level ceiling (B4)

## Player gets
No enemy Pokémon is higher than the average of your three highest-level Pokémon plus 2 (`balance.json` `difficulty.partyStrength.levelCeiling`, 0 = off). The existing party-strength bonus still raises enemies when you are far ahead of the curve; this only stops them from running away above you.

## Save
None.

## Limits
Applies wherever enemy levels are rolled through the normal level scale (wild, trainers, gym leaders, E4, Champion). Fixed-level special fights (the first rival, scripted events) are not touched.
