# Triple Battles in runs (T3)

**Player gets:** a "Triple" format in the Battle Factory, where all three rentals fight at once. Triple trainer battles from the third region and the Triples Only rule are built but switched off until the battle screen can show three a side (T2 turns them on).

**Save:** none.

**Limits:** no wild triple battles, and no boss always fights in triples yet. Details in `docs/status/beta06-t3-triples-in-runs.md`.
