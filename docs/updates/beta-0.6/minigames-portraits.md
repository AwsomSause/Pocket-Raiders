# Minigames: performer and patient portraits (Orchestra and Triage picks)

**Player gets:** The Orchestra Pit shows its four performers (Loudred, Jigglypuff, Smoochum, Chatot) above the lanes, and Triage Beds shows Nurse Chansey and the waiting patients' icons above the cards, using the game's own Pokémon icons. Any game can now show a row of icons through `portraits` in its view.

**Save:** no change.

**Limits:** small icons in a row, not the full cabinet frame or the drag-and-drop paper layout from the picks (cards are still the plain menu). Icons load on demand and fall back to a coloured dot until they arrive.
