# Foresight Tax (Oracle Sabrina)

**Player gets:** Sabrina (the Blackout Triad's third anchor) sees the first move you choose each turn before she picks hers, and the battle says so. It ends for the battle once any of her Pokémon faints.

**Save:** none (optional `battle.foresight`).

**Limits:** single battles only; a switch or item reveals no move. Details in `docs/status/foresight-tax.md`.
