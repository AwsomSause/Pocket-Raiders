# Wide formats: Debate (E4, 7 of 7)

**Player gets:** Paldea's Assembly as a real debate: three exchanges, each with the opponent's claim, your argument (evidence you earned, or plain reasoning), his rebuttal, and your answer to it. Evidence wins an exchange on its own; reasoning wins it if you also answer his tactic correctly. Three wins: no battle. Two wins: an objection line. Fewer: one battle against Harrow. Losing never ends the run.

**Save:** none (result in `story.flags['star.x.debate']`).

**Limits:** the evidence flags and the brochure item are not given out by anything yet (the Paldea slices must set them); plain Format card; Harrow uses a generic Veteran team. Details in `docs/status/wide-formats-debate.md`.
