# Kanto Canon Spaces: Cerulean, part 2 (workstream 18)

**Player gets:** The rest of the Cerulean stage.
- **Cold Nights Rain:** a rainy night road. A shivering Fire-type (Charmander 31%, Growlithe 15%, Vulpix 12%, Ponyta 10%, the eight other Fire starters 4% each) stands on a rock: Save it or go on your way. If you save it, its **Warmth** starts at 3 and falls 1 on each rain node. A wild fight in forced rain, a lucky campfire, an Oran Berry, a Fire move, a Charcoal or a night under an overhang keep it from dropping. At the end you fight its previous owner (Damian if it is a Charmander) in forced rain. Win and it joins you with a Charcoal. If Warmth runs out, the owner takes it back and the run goes on.
- **Bill's Lighthouse:** find the beacon by following clues along a night coast (3 of 5 right clues; a wrong one sends you back a node). Press the console to free Bill: you get a Remote PC, the **S.S. Ticket** and an egg move.
- **Route 5 Day-Care:** pay 150 to raise your weakest Pokémon 3 levels, or 300 for an Egg.

**Save:** none. New data-only pieces: wild battles take `weather`; the canon state now copies flags that a later event of the same space sets.

**Limits:**
- Cold Nights Rain: no regional-form Fire-types (Hisuian Growlithe, Alolan Vulpix, Galarian Ponyta), no permanent night, no Walker follower on the map: the Warmth is a story counter, not a map meter. The owner is a plain trainer fight of three.
- Bill's PC is a Remote PC (the box already works on the map); Egg Notes is one egg move for your lead.
- The Day-Care is an on-the-spot service, not a one-stage stay.
- Cerulean Cave (after Indigo) belongs to the Indigo/roaming thread.
