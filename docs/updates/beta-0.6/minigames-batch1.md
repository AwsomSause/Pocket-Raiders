# Minigames batch 1 (MG2)

**Player gets:** three new minigames on map stops: Psyduck Migration (lead ducklings home, snake style), The Day the Center Closed (triage: choose who to treat first), and Sled Run (dodge rocks, grab berries). Each has Easy, Normal and Hard numbers, two tries, medals and the usual rewards. About 4 in 10 minigame stops now use a new game.

**Save:** no change. Games keep their state in the existing minigame block.

**Limits:** rules and data only. They use the generic grid screen until Kice picks the mock-ups; no art or sound yet. The 14 older games still pick the same way (a separate roll chooses new games). Rhythm, stealth, arcade, debate and Safari games come in later batches.
