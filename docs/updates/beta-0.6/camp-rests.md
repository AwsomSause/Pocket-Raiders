# Camp and rest scenes (NE81-85)

**Player gets:** five once-per-run rest-stop scenes: The One Who Won't Eat (Resolute: may hang on at 1 HP once), The Argument, The Bath (status cured, 5% off in shops for 5 battles), The Nightmare Watch (evening/night only; 10% less damage next battle), The Photograph Nobody Posed For (late run). They use the choice-memory framework: the pair from the first scene shows up in the Argument, and the Argument shows up in the photo.

**Save:** none. State is run story flags (`camp-pair`, `camp-argument`, `camp-photo`, `camp-photo-home`) and the usual temp effects.

**Limits:** "Bond" is friendship (1 Bond = 3 friendship) and goes to the whole party, because events cannot pick one Pokémon. Triggers the engine has no condition for (a team member just fainted, opposed natures, near water, after a hard loss) are approximated: party of 2+, a minimum stage, a Water-leaning stage, and evening/night. Flinch immunity is replaced by -10% damage taken. "Send it home / on the wall in later runs" is cross-run and was cut: the photo is a Chronicle line and a run flag only. Healing: none beyond the existing cure, so the 10-heal / 6-revive battle budget is untouched.
