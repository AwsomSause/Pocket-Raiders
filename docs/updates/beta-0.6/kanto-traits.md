# Kanto signature traits

**Player gets:** Team Rocket's named Pokémon now fight with their traits: Persian sweeps away your boosts, Rhyperior drills through Protect, the Nidos lift each other up, Porygon copies a stat change, Raticate pockets your Berry, Voltorb and Electrode black out the field, and Meowth translates the foe's next move (Rocket Remnants). Specialists and above only.

**Save:** no version bump. Battles gain optional `traits`, `darkTurns` and `payout`; old battles load with defaults (tested).

**Limits:** Fed and Paid (Snorlax) and Payout (Dratini) work and are tested but no fight fields those species yet (Roadblock event and Jackpot's raid scene). Not simmed.
