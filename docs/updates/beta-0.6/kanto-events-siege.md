# Kanto events feed the Siege Map and the Colossus

## Player gets
- Choices made in the Kanto story events now count at the climax: taking the envelope to Officer Jenny (`rocket-wire`) holds the East Gate, stopping Major Surge (`rocket-surge-down`) holds the South Gate, and Madame Boss's hint (`rocket-madame`) and the Seal Diagram (`rocket-seal-diagram`) help in the Colossus fight.

## Save
- None. The Siege Map and Colossus now also read the `ev:` form of these flags.

## Limits
- Intel and Operational Strength are still not built.
