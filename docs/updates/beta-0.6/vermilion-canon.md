# Kanto Canon Spaces: Vermilion City (workstream 18)

**Player gets:** In a Kanto run (Kanto setting on), the Vermilion stage always has **Flight North**: a 50% check; on a hit, Jessie, James and Meowth take your whole team and you wake with nothing. A Caterpie or Weedle beats off a Spearow and is your only Pokémon (level 5) through a 7-node stage (meadow, cocoon night, stream, final evolution, the trio's camp, the level 20 Venomoth). When the Bug-type falls under a quarter HP, a shiny copy knocks the Venomoth out. Win, lose, or just leave for the map and your team comes back.
Rolled for the stage: **S.S. Anne** (needs `kanto.ssanne.ticket`; 12 trainer fights on three decks, each followed by trade offers: a similar-strength Kanto Pokémon, the regional trade table, or any Pokémon for a regional one; a galley meal at node 3, optional Stowaways / Passenger Duel / Pokémon Cup, the Captain, a rival fight, then a **Network Machine**), **Kaiju Tentacruel** (raid with three shields and a poison-water hazard; reward Poison Barb, Mystic Water, and a Tentacool with Huge Power and Liquidation), **Maiden's Peak** (market of Lantern Row, an 8-board TM Hall, Swap Alley, Egg Row and the Tea House, then a five-sighting chase; reward Fairium Z, or three Fairy HMs if you own it, plus a Fairy Feather and a Tea House heal stop for two lucky turns), **Diglett Port** (fishing game for a Good Rod and a Basculin or Corphish), and the **Harbor Market** (Water goods, a replacement S.S. Anne Ticket, Hero's stock after the Kaiju).

**Save:** no version bump. New optional fields: `expansion.canon.stash` (the stolen team), join `level` / `moves` / `direct`, battle `level`, battle rule `shinyRescue`, effects `tradeFor` / `stashParty` / `restoreParty`, raid `canon` and `rewards.gift`, and the item `network-machine`. Older saves load as they are.

**Limits:**
- Flight North's later part, **Second Wind** (game-over screen cracks, the Bug-type revives the team in Mega/Gigantamax form, "North's Wind"), is not built; the flags `kanto.flight.rescue.used` / `.keeps` are listed but never set. Odds shifts (Squad, Rocket heat, Stowaways) are not built: the check is a flat 50%.
- The Bug-type's "evolution by love" happens as the boss fight starts, not mid-battle. The shiny rescue is a battle rule that fires once under 25% HP.
- Kaiju: Tidal Surge does not exist (the boss gets Wrap and Acid Armor besides its own moves); the Tentacool summons come from raid badge scaling; the "support boats" are a paid approach that weakens the boss 25%, not extra helper turns.
- Maiden's Peak is a chain of event rooms, not a walkable town. Egg trading and hatching-for-steps are not offered. The TM Hall's 24 moves rotate by board (one board per visit); Swap Alley's offers are three stat-total ranges, not three named traders.
- S.S. Anne: the "any Pokémon for a regional one" trade is always shown, not only when you have no matching Pokémon; one trade per node; Delta forms and the Captain's Compass are not built. Trade levels match the Pokémon given up. The ticket flag comes from Bill's Lighthouse (not built yet), so until then S.S. Anne is not rolled; the Market's ticket is the ferry item.
- Catalog items given by events (the Network Machine, Fairy HMs) are applied at once to the first Pokémon that can take them and sold for cash if none can.
