# Wide formats: Colossus (E4, 5 of 7)

**Player gets:** the Kanto roof fight against M2: four armor seals (right shoulder, left shoulder, chest, crown), each with a shield bar shown on a Blueprint plate beside the battle (press C to hide it). M2 cannot be knocked out while a seal remains; each broken seal takes one of its moves. A 10 turn timer: if it runs out the helicopter takes M2 away (the climax still counts). Not reachable until the Extended Cut director (E5) starts it.

**Save:** none (optional `battle.colossus` field; result in `story.flags['rocket.x.m2']`).

**Limits:** the plate sits beside the battle rather than full screen; M2's signature moves are not real moves yet, so seals take its strongest moves; Rig Room tier drop and the idle turn after the armor falls are not built. Details in `docs/status/wide-formats-colossus.md`.
