# Smarter foes in Triple Battles

**Player gets:** in a triple battle, a foe stuck on the far edge steps into the centre (Shift) instead of wasting turns, and foes stop choosing moves that can't reach anyone. Only the Battle Factory's triple format uses this today. Trainer triple battles will use it once the triple battle screen (T2) is in.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:** an edge foe with no target next to a centre partner that can attack still loses its turn, because swapping that partner out would be worse. Doubles play exactly as before.
