# #1333 New UI: the Second Shot box

**Player gets:**
- When you lose a battle of an Extended Cut climax, a box now tells you what that means before the screen leaves. The first loss: **Second Shot**, you go back to the last save point with your team patched up, and one more loss ends the climax (button "Try again"). The second loss: **The climax is lost**, it ends with its worst ending and the run goes on (button "Go on").
- New look: the mock-up's confirm box over the battle, with one button; it stays until Enter or a click. Old look: the same words as two text lines.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The mock-up box has two buttons; the rule gives no choice (the retry is automatic, decision A), so the box has one.
- Death-Block and Adventure runs keep their own wipe rule for a lost climax battle (back to the checkpoint), so they show no Second Shot box.
