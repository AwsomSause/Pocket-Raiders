# #1333 New UI: the Beta 0.6 battle pieces

**Player gets:** (New look; the battle screen's own styles are untouched)
- **Item budget:** while the Item window is open, a small window at the top right shows how many healing items and revives this battle has used out of the most it allows (10 and 6).
- **Rally:** when a boss or rival braces against a sweep, a yellow line over the field says what went up ("RALLY · the foe braces: Def and Sp. Def +1").
- **Foresight Tax:** while Sabrina's rule is on, a banner at the top says so each time you choose.
- **Variant ring:** the first time you choose against a foe with a variant line's ring, a hint says to press I. **The Info panel (I) now tells the line's name and rule** (both looks).
- **Signature trait:** when a special Pokémon's trait goes off, a callout names the trait and what it did, with the Pokémon's picture.
- **Shadow line:** a Shadow Pokémon's tier line ("Shadow · Fallen (tier 4) · Sealed 100/100") and what the shadow will do this turn stand in their own purple window over the text box, so the prompt keeps its size.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- These only show what the battle already decided; nothing about the rules changed.
- The Rally line and the trait callout stay for their own message and the stat lines right after it.
- The variant hint shows once per foe (the ring stays).
- The Old look keeps its text lines for all of these.
