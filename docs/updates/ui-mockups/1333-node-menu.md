# #1333 New UI: the node event menu

**Player gets:** (New look)
- The node's menu ("Here (4)" on the map) now opens in the New look: the map dims and the node's stops stand in a row of cards, each with its kind (Shop, Rest, Story...) in its colour, its name, one line about it, and where it stands (Required, Optional, Done, Locked).
- Left and right move the cursor, Enter picks, Esc or a click beside the cards closes it. Resting the pointer on a card moves the cursor; a click picks it.
- A stop that is done or locked is dimmed and cannot be picked.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The mock-up pack has no page for this menu (it came with Beta 0.7), so it follows the reward draft's row of cards.
- The rules are the same as before: the cards come from the same menu the Old look draws.
