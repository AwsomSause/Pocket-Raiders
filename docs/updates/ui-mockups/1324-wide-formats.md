# #1324 New UI: the wide-format pages (Breather, Blackout, Convoy, Colossus, Front, Debate)

**Player gets:**
- **Breather** as the mock-up: your party along the top with what the rest gives each one ("+50% HP", or "Revive?"), a "Bring one back" window with the fainted Pokémon and its Revive button, and "The quiet room" with the heal and save facts.
- **Blackout** as the mock-up: one card per anchor (nameless guards are black shapes, Sabrina is herself), fallen ones dimmed, and the Seal window with its rule and "Anchors fallen 1 / 2". Pick an anchor's card to fight it.
- **Convoy** as the mock-up: the road with one truck per leg (green cleared, yellow now, dark ahead), the fight button and your party.
- **Front** as the mock-up: one window per lane with the foe on top and your Pokémon below, HP bars, and "Lane won".
- **Debate** as the mock-up: the opponent with the exchange number and the score dots, the claim, your arguments as buttons marked Evidence or Reasoning, and the dimmed "rebuttal comes next" window.
- **Colossus**: the Apex's picture, the briefing and the Blueprint plate with its three shield marks before the fight.
- Arrow keys, hover or a tap move over the choices; Enter or a click picks. Any other format shows a plain page with its lines and buttons.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The headers keep the game's own names ("The Streets", "The Long Drive", "The Assembly") where the mock-up shows the format's name.
- The mock-up draws the Blueprint inside the Colossus fight; the battle screen keeps its own styles, so the plate is on the page before the fight.
- The Front page shows after the lanes are fought (the fight itself is the battle screen); before it, the plain page.
- No "Esc back" on these pages: a format cannot be left half-way.
