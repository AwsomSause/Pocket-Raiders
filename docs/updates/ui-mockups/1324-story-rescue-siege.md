# #1324 New UI: story scenes, Rescue, Siege map and the Field Trial card

**Player gets:**
- **Story scenes** (backstory, echoes, endings) as the mock-up talk page: you on the left, the scene's name and line count on the tag, the speaker on the name plate, choices at the side with the last line.
- **Rescue screen** as the mock-up: the team's impound receipt (who was taken, their levels, where they are held, the jailer, a stamp), the Holding clock, and the plans as buttons with an odds word (Risky, Even, Likely, Safe; never a number). The rival's picture stands by the text box and speaks first; the plans show once the lines are read. Moving over a plan makes the rival pitch it (or says why it is closed).
- The plan itself plays on the same page: the clock becomes the plan's steps (Approach, Door, Cage), answers are the buttons, guards and the result use it too. The stamp turns green ("HOME") when everyone is back.
- **Siege map** as the mock-up ledger page: one coloured cell per district (Held, Contested, Lost), the facts of the district under the cursor, the list of Lost effects, and one "Enter by …" button. Arrow keys, hover or a tap move the cursor; only the button goes in.
- **Field Trial** opens on the mock-up versus card (you against your rival) before the spar.
- A versus card or cutscene card now keeps notifications back until it leaves, so they no longer cover the names.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The Deal shows "Safe" (paying always works); the Exchange shows no word because it has no roll.
- The Rescue header says "Rescue Plan" and the wait choice "Wait for a better day" (the mock-up's words); Old keeps "Not now".
- The Field Trial's spar after the card is still the old drawing.
- A rival with no picture in the game shows no figure on the Rescue page.
