# #1324 New UI: Memories pages and the Hall of Fame team page

**Player gets:**
- **Memories** pages carry the mock-up names ("Memories: Run Journal", "Memories: Fallen Trainers", "Memories: Heirlooms") on the purple story band; Battle Replays has the red battle band.
- Each row has its tag at the right: Win or Loss on a journal run, Moment / Battle / Note in Memory Lane, Loss on a fallen trainer, Play on a replay.
- **Reading an entry now works in New.** Before, Enter on an entry opened the old page under the new one, so nothing showed. It now opens as a page of the journal over the list; the arrows scroll a long entry, and Enter, Esc or a click closes it.
- **Hall of Fame:** Enter on a champion row opens that run's winning team as the mock-up page (the lead on the left, the others beside it, the gold band). Esc goes back to the list.
- The Legacy Gallery has the gold band of finished runs.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- A run record keeps each team member's species, level and whether it was shiny, not its HP or items. So the team page shows a full bar and the types where the mock-up shows HP numbers and a held item.
- The Hall of Fame list itself stays on the blue hub band (the mock-up's list page); only the team page is gold.
- With no champion yet, the band says "Hall of Fame" and the Records line tells you to win a run (the long title did not fit the band).
