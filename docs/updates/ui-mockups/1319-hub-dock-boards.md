# #1319 New UI: Hub dock, quest boards, trade card

**Player gets:**
- The Hub opens as the mock-up's **dock** (New UI, unless Hub style is Arena Lobby): a Town tab (Settings, Trainers, New Run, Events, Unlocks, Town) and a Last run tab (Hall of Fame, Team, Run History, Memories, Moments, Seeds), under a line-up of Pokémon drawn at their real heights: the Pokémon you walk as, your last team, past Aces, your most used. Every hub room is reachable from the dock. **Town > Hub Town** walks the town as before; Esc there opens the menu, and its first row, **Hub dock**, leads back. The dock remembers its tab and button.
- **Quest Log, Trainer Quests, Achievement Hall** and the other list rooms: a count such as "2/5" is drawn as the mock-up's progress bar with a New, Active or Done tag, and the count in words under the title.
- **Honest Trade Card:** a swap shows both sides (the Pokémon that goes, the Pokémon that comes back) before you commit. It says "Not known yet" when the event does not tell.
- A **pseudo-stage's map** says it is inside a stage instead of naming a Gym Leader.
- A **long choice label** in an event shrinks and wraps inside its button.
- Fix: a mouse resting on a button no longer pulls the keyboard cursor back to it on every key press.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- Walking Hub Town and Pocket Town keep their own look (the mock-ups show Pocket Town as a dock; that is not built yet).
- The dock's "Team" button opens the Legacy Gallery (every past Trainer's team), not only the last run.
- Still drawn the Old way inside New: Battle default look, Party, Summary, Bag, run end, overlays, cutscenes, map pop-ups (node menu too), Field Trial, egg hatch, Legendary title cards.
