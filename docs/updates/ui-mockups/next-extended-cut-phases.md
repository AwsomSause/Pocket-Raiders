# New UI: the Extended Cut phase list

**Player gets:** (New look)
- During an Extended Cut climax, every wide page (Breather, the Seal, Convoy, Front, Debate, the Apex) shows a **P phases** hint at the top right. Press P (or click the hint) to open the **phase list**: every phase of the longer climax in order, with its kind and how many fights it holds, and a tag for Done, Now, Next or Ahead.
- The cursor starts on the phase that is running. Up and down walk the list; Esc or Enter goes back to the page.
- The text box says whether the Second Shot is still unused.

**Save:** none. The list only reads the run.

**Limits:**
- The list opens from the wide pages only: during the climax the map never rests, and battles have no room for it.
- The mock-up key hint is not X: X already means "back" in this game, so the key is P.
- The Old look has no phase list.
