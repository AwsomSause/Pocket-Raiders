# #1422 Title clips and springy buttons

**Player gets:** the title screen fades between six animated pixel-art scenes (New and Old looks). Buttons bounce: they spring on hover, squash on press and pop in when a screen opens. Motion settings and the device's "reduce motion" switch turn the bounces off.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:** a browser that cannot play H.264 video keeps the old title picture. Old look with a picked title screen style keeps that style's backdrop. Battle screen gets no new styles, only the shared press/hover motion. Details: `docs/status/title-clips.md`.
