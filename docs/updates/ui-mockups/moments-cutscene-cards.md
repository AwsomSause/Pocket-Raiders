# New UI: important moments as the mock-up cutscene cards

**Player gets:**
- In New, a battle's big moments now play as the mock-up cutscene card: a plate ("Gym Leader", "Rival", "The Betrayal"...), the name in big letters, one spoken line, the picture, and the five-step strip at the bottom (Situation, Anticipation, Moment, Reaction, Transition).
- **Bosses:** Gym Leader, Elite Four and Champion intros (then the versus card), the ace's entrance, the Champion's "Final Battle" on their last Pokémon, and "Defeated" with the boss's own last words.
- **Rivals:** first meeting, rematch, revenge, reunion, and "Final Rival Battle" for the Ultimate Rival.
- **Villains:** the Evil Team reveal and the Betrayal (dark red on the split-screen layout).
- **Pokémon:** raid intro and "Raid cleared", a wild Legendary or Mythical reveal, and your first Mega Evolution of a run. Your Trainer stands beside the Pokémon, as in the mock-ups.
- **Others:** Galar stadium cut-ins and tournament round openings, mentor assist, graduation, your protégé's return, and a Hall of Fame card after you beat a Champion.
- All three mock-up layouts (wide shot, burst close-up, split screen) follow the style you picked.
- Click, Esc or hold Space to skip a moment that can be skipped. A skipped boss intro stays skipped, as before.
- A card with words stays long enough to read them. With animations off, the card shows once and moves on.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The game has no "Sky High Pokémon"-style category for each species, so a Legendary's plate is its event title or "Legendary Pokémon", and the line is the same for all of them.
- "First Mega Evolution" is the first one since you opened the run; it shows again after a reload.
- The Hall of Fame card plays after every Champion win (there is no Hall of Fame screen yet).
- The Mega, Z-Move, Dynamax and Tera scenes themselves are still drawn the old way.
- Your Trainer's back view is used when it exists (Hilda, Nate and a few others); Red, Blue and Green show their usual picture.
