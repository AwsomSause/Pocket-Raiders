# #1324 New UI: loading, update splash, welcome log, prologue, tutorial and credits

**Player gets:**
- **Loading screen** as the mock-up: a Poké Ball (or Pikachu running, by style) over a gold bar, the title and a tip. The **update splash** uses the same page over the update's picture; click or press a key to move on.
- **Welcome log** and **tutorial** as the mock-up talk page: your Trainer or the professor on the left, the words in the text box under a name plate, the choices at the side, a tag at the top ("First launch", "Update log 2/8", "Tutorial 1/4"). Long text turns page by page; choices show once you have read it.
- The tutorial's lesson list shows all nine professors and "Leave" on one page in every style; pointing at a lesson shows who teaches it.
- **Prologue** as the mock-ups: the opening shots and the Departure pages are cutscene cards, Professor Vance's scenes are talk pages with her drawn large as in the mock-up, and the name entry is the mock-up form with on-screen keys, Paste, "That is me" and Clear. The keyboard still types too.
- **Credits** as the mock-up: gold title, small headings over bold names, your winning party along the bottom, rolling up the screen. With animations not on Full the page stands still and scrolls with Up / Down. Esc, Enter or a click leaves.
- Professor Vance no longer says "You were late." twice in a row (both styles).

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The name entry's last four keys are hyphen, apostrophe, space and delete (the mock-up shows 0 to 3): names need those, and without a delete key a touch player could not fix a letter. Numbers can still be typed on a keyboard.
- A prologue card that cannot be skipped shows "Enter next" where the mock-up shows "Esc skip".
- Professors Birch, Magnolia, Sada and Turo have no picture in the game, so their tutorial pages show the room only.
- A loading look picked in Settings > Screen styles still replaces the mock-up loading page.
