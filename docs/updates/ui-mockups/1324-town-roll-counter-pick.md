# #1324 New UI: Town Roll page and the League counter-pick page

**Player gets:**
- **Town Roll** (Kanto Canon runs, on a Kanto stage): press **O** on the map (or tap "O town") to see the town's spaces. Starred ones are always there, "Rolled" ones were drawn this run, "Unlocked" ones were opened by something you did, and the ones the roll left out are listed as "Not this run". The line under them says how many optional spaces were placed. Moving onto a rolled space shows what it is.
- **League counter-pick**: the League door is now the mock-up page. Your registered party with types, the one "The League will answer", the boxed Pokémon you can swap in (one marked "Pick"), and two buttons: "Swap … out" and "Keep my team". The Registrar speaks in the text box. The result stays until you press Enter.

**Save:** none. The Town Roll page only reads the menu the run already rolled; it rolls nothing.

**Limits:**
- Town Roll is in New only (Old has no such page). A real town has more spaces than the mock-up's four, so with more than four the left-out ones are small tags under the cards.
- A space with nothing to play yet is not put on the map, so it is not on the page.
- The card's third line is the kind of place (Event, Shop, Pseudo-stage, Town hub), not a list of what is inside.
- The boxed Pokémon rows also show the level (the mock-up shows only the name).
