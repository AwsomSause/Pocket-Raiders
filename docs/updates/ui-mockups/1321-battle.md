# #1321 New UI: the battle screen from the mock-ups

**Player gets:**
- In New, the **default battle look** is the mock-ups' battle screen, drawn over the live field (the arena, the Pokémon and every animation are the same). Four layouts by look: classic boxes, a full-width tray, a top VS bar, a side menu.
- **Data boxes:** name, level, HP bar that turns yellow and red, your HP numbers and Exp strip, status and Tera tags, the foe Trainer's team as balls.
- **Commands and moves:** the command tray and type-coloured move buttons with PP; keyboard, mouse and touch all steer them.
- **Party and Item windows** open over the field (Bag pockets as tabs), with the "who to use it on" window.
- **Info panel**, a **move's details**, the **battle log** with filter tabs, and the **transform buttons** (Mega, Tera, Dynamax, Z-Move).
- **Doubles and triples:** every Pokémon has its box; TARGET and OUT OF REACH tags show on the field.
- **Shadow Pokémon** get the purple tray. After a win: the **Exp panel**, the **level-up window** (old and new stats) and the **evolution picture**.
- **Trainer fights open on a versus card** (four layouts: split screen, name band, arena shot with the foe's line, two cards).
- **Hover help:** rest the pointer on a move for its tooltip, or on a data box for that Pokémon's Ability card. Click a box to pin its card; click again to let it go. The Tooltip delay setting decides how long to rest (Off shows none; pins still work).
- Every battle style you can pick is unchanged in New and Old.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- A foe's Ability shows on its card only after it has come into play.
- With the Item or Party window open, the text box says what the focused row does instead of showing the commands.
- The evolution picture has no "B stop" hint (the game cannot cancel an evolution).
- The versus card shows only the foe's first Pokémon as a picture; the rest are balls.
- "Seen in" on the Ability card counts earlier runs, not this one.
- Still drawn the Old way inside New: overlays, cutscenes, map pop-ups (node menu too), Field Trial, egg hatch, Legendary title cards.
