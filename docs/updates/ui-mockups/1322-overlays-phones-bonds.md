# #1322 New UI: overlays, phone fixes, Bond screen and hover help

**Player gets:**
- **Notifications** stack at the top right as the mock-up windows (the newest is bright, older ones fade back); tap one to send it away. Before, in New they were hidden under the screens.
- **Achievement toast:** the mock-up toast (ribbon, "Achievement", the name). Tap it to read what it was for and what it paid; tap again to close.
- **Interaction log (L):** the mock-up window down the left side, newest first, with how long ago each thing happened. L or a click closes it.
- **Touch pad:** in New it has the mock-up look (a round see-through pad and round A and B buttons) and can be pressed over the screens.
- **Phone held upright:** the New screens turn with the game and fill the screen. The turned game is centred now; before it sat off to one side and was cut off (in Old too).
- **Bond screen:** the mock-up board, one row per Pokémon with its Trust bar ("Max" at 100), trait, mood and role. The other bond values are read out in the text box.
- **Pokémon info card:** on the map, rest the pointer on a party Pokémon for its Ability card.
- **Move tooltip:** in the Summary's Overview, rest the pointer on a move for its details.
- Tooltips wait for the Tooltip delay setting; Off shows none.

**Save:** none.

**Limits:**
- The mock-up's "turn your phone sideways" notice is not built: the game turns itself on an upright phone instead.
- The mock-up's pause menu is not built yet (the game has no pause menu; the map's own buttons do the same jobs).
- In New the Bond screen shows Trust as the bar; the other six values are text, not bars (Old still draws all the bars).
- "Seen in" on the info card counts earlier runs, not this one.
- Song captions in New are plain text at the bottom (no mock-up for them).
