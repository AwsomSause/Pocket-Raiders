# Sinnoh and Hoenn Evil Team saga (Standard Cut)

- **Player gets:** in Sinnoh (Team Galactic, "The Subtraction") and Hoenn (Aqua and Magma, "The Long Weather") the same saga layer Kanto has.
  - **16 story events** (8 each, `src/data/events/xe-sinnoh.json`, `xe-hoenn.json`, built by `npm run data:xe-events` from `tools/events/expansion/sinnoh.ts` and `hoenn.ts`). They use the same quiet-node scheduler and event-density setting as Kanto. Sinnoh: The Clinic (the anchor), Fridays, Grunt No. 836, The Bench in Sunyshore, The Marsh Test, The Wall at Celestic, The Town That Slept, The Ribbon. Hoenn: Wet Cellar Dry Well (the anchor), The Institute, Stern and Cozmo, Two Hoods, The Arms Dealer, Lunch Break, A Letter for Steven, The Keepers.
  - **World-state tracks** (with the World tracks setting on). Sinnoh: step 2 Pokémon Centers restore 80% HP and night battles have 5% less accuracy for both sides; step 3 your Pokémon cannot land critical hits and battle friendship gains are halved; step 4 every shop has forgotten one item; step 5 a map banner. Hoenn: step 1 some nodes are rainy or sunny; step 2 the sky follows which team is ahead (the `aqua-ahead` and `magma-ahead` flags from the opening event); steps 3 to 4 the weather flips every two turns; step 5 rain and sun alternate every turn. The Notebook track page (key T) shows both.
  - **Cast and Dossier:** the people in those events and behind each track step are added to the Dossier.
  - **7 signature traits in battle** (Galactic: Remainder, Miasma, Relay; Aqua: Riptide, Siren; Magma: Old Faithful, Thermal). They only apply in their own region and to their own team's Specialists and up.
  - **Extended Cut (opt-in, off by default):** Sinnoh and Hoenn are now on the Extended Cut list. Phase 0b, the Siege Map, works for both ("The Mountain Under Siege": Oreburgh Gate, Hearthome Road, Celestic Path, Snowpoint Trail; "Sootopolis Divided": Harbor Tunnel, West and East Ledge, White Rock). Held, Contested or Lost comes from the raids and the event flags above (Hoenn's ledges follow the tilt). A Contested gate costs one battle. The Breather already works in any region. Blackout works too: Sinnoh's White Tunnel (hail, accuracy x0.9, Sird must fall; lifted if Friday was lent) and Hoenn's Crater Lake (weather flips every 2 turns, lifted if Forecast was saved, a lunch-break truce pre-beats one anchor). The Set piece is a generic battle phase and needs no extra work. Colossus skips itself for these regions.
- **Save:** none. New optional battle field `noPlayerCrit`; new trait ids only widen an enum, so old saves load.
- **Limits:** Standard Cut only. Not built yet for these two regions: Raids 1 to 5 in play (the loose-ends thread owns raids), the Extended Cut's Colossus (needs raid data for Stillhour/Farshore and Abyssal/Cinderheart) the Siege Map's lost-district effects on later phases, Siege Map, liaison and Data ROM, Intel and Operational Strength, the Spiritless line rule and Charon's Poltergeist Engine, Lovrina's Heart Lock. Sinnoh track: 3a (memory: one fewer node ahead), wild Pokémon fleeing less (step 4), bond gains halving and the stopped clocks (step 5) have no game effect yet; the three lakes are one step, so freeing one lake does not remove just its rule. Hoenn steps 4 (berry trees and ferries) has only the weather swing. The traits Empress, Verdict, Bulwark, Zero Kelvin, Absence, Friday, Warrant, Forecast, Mirage, Specimen Nine, Borehead, Flagship, Twin Pearls and Bosun are named in the Dossier only. Event 7th foes are not used (Cyrus has no 7th Pokémon in the script).
