# 1264 Anime Forms: Squirtle Squad and Champion Primeape

**Player gets:** the Squirtle Squad in Cerulean shows Kice's animated Anime Forms art (Lesser Squirtle Squad, Leader Wartortle, Leader Blastoise), and the Squirtle that joins is the Leader Squirtle Squad (it keeps the look as Wartortle, Blastoise and Mega Squad Blastoise). The Fighting Tournament champion Primeape shows the Champion Primeape art.

**Save:** none (optional `look` field on a Pokémon).

**Limits:** presentation only; no stat or move changes. Lesser Wartortle and Lesser Blastoise art is wired but only shows if a Lesser member ever joins (none does today). No icon art was supplied, so menu icons stay the base species'.
