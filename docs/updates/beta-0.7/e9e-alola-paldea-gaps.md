# E9e: Alola and Paldea gaps closed

**Player gets:**
- Paldea's world-state track now bites at steps 1 to 4: step 1 marks one shop per stage up, step 2 shuts the first healing stop at night, step 3 takes a quarter off the wild pool, step 4 allows roadblock scenes. Step 5 keeps its map line. Numbers come from the same balance entry as Kanto's track.
- Old Habits (needs the story-traits setting): the Old Hallway Peons hit 25% harder once your Pokémon is under half health.
- Alola's Trophy Room has a new "Wait for Guzma to come home" fight. Every third turn he drives your Pokémon off the field and you choose who comes in (a free switch). Works in any event fight through the new `forceSwitch` rule.

**Save:** no change (the rule and trait live in existing loose battle fields).

**Limits:** Old Habits in a one-on-one fight is a damage bonus against a weakened Pokémon, since there is no choice of target; it will need a real target pick in double battles. Forced switch does nothing when no healthy Pokémon is on the bench. The "bring Beasts below half" wins, the Paldea Star badge and expulsion systems, and Raids 4 and 5 (loose-ends thread) are still not built.
