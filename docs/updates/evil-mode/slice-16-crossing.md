# Evil Mode: cross the water

**A Standard run is now three regions: Kanto, Johto and Hoenn.**

- **The Crossing.** When a region's over, two boats wait at the quay. Pick a ticket; each one lists four true rules of the region it goes to.
- **Not everyone comes.** A third of your team stays behind, each for their own reason. You can ask one of them to come anyway, if they trust you enough.
- **Your base.** Hand it to the town, sell it, leave an Officer in it (it keeps paying a little), or burn it.
- **What carries.** Your money and your team's trust come with you. Heat halves. Regard and Dread halve too, unless your choice for the base kept one whole.
- **The Hero follows.** Where they land depends on how your last fight with them went.
- **Johto:** everybody knows everybody. Persuading works better; taking draws more attention. Look after its old kept places.
- **Hoenn:** the sea and the volcano. Archie or Maxie is your neighbour. Carry letters between them to stop the worst, or watch the weather turn.
