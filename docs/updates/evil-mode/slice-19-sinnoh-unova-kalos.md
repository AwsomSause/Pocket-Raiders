# Evil Mode: three more regions

**Sinnoh, Unova and Kalos are open. A run can now be six regions long.**

- **Run length.** In Settings, Evil Mode run length turns between Three and Six. A Six run crosses the water five times.
- **Sinnoh:** cold and quiet. People here don't say: trust moves half as far, both ways. Cyrus doesn't negotiate; you deal with Saturn in the canteen. If nobody stops what's going up the mountain, a second clock runs at the Reckoning. Three quiet lakes nobody can own.
- **Unova:** everyone's got an opinion. Regard and Dread move twice as far, losses too. Ghetsis comes himself; N is a child and can't be touched. Five bridges. The Town is a square: give the Speech there, and its Card shows one extra line, what you're leaving out.
- **Kalos:** money and taste. Fronts pay half as much again, and a coat with no symbol starts with a little more Regard. Lysandre has a list and a corner table. Seats at tables instead of shops. If the weapon isn't stopped, the light in the south is a second clock.
- **Twenty-four new Gym Leaders** to beat, buy, bend, replace or befriend, and twenty-four new locals.
