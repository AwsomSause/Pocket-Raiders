# Evil Mode: play Kanto as the bad guys

**Evil Mode is now on by default.** Pick it in Run Setup.

- **Found your team your way.** Besides a name, Creed, Line and Doctrine, you now pick your team's Look (colours, a symbol, a cut, headgear and a motto), a Patron who backs you, and your first region. Kanto is the one that's open; the others are coming.
- **Play the whole of Kanto.** You land in Pewter for a short Arrival, then take six districts in order, from Cerulean to Cinnabar, and finish with three days at Viridian (the Reckoning). Losing there doesn't end your run.
- **Kanto's rule: everything's owned.** Paying people off works a little better here, and taking from somebody who holds a deed draws a little more Heat.
- **The Road.** Between districts you see how the last one went, then choose whether to leave an Officer behind to keep it. A district you leave nobody in pays half and can slip away.
- **Towers.** Celadon's department store and Saffron's Silph Co. are taken a floor at a time, from the bottom up.
- **Eight new Kanto faces** to win over, including Mr. Fuji, who can't be leaned on by anyone.
