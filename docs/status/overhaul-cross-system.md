# Overhaul slice 2: cross-system check (no gameplay change)

- Overhaul slice 2 (THE OVERHAUL.md rule 5): `src/quality/crossSystem.ts` walks every species and form and checks that battle (stats, HP, moves, ability), evolution (target exists, `evolve()` works, not into a battle-only form), eggs (family, lay + hatch, the family evolves into it), trades (Trade Post offer and every Plaza trader pool), battle forms (Mega/Primal stones and orbs, Gigantamax) and sprites (front, icon, cry) all agree. `tests/unit/crossSystem.test.ts` runs it over all species, saves and reloads every ownable species in parties of six, and has boundary (no usable moves gets a stand-in attack) and failure cases (a broken evolution, ability or sprite is reported by species and system). Today it finds 0 problems. Limited: the check covers level 50 Pokémon and one trade seed; Tera, Z-Moves and Dynamax moves are covered by their own tests. No save change.
