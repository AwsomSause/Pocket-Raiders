# W4: Rival activity, rumors and race paces

New: `src/core/rivals/activity.ts`, data `src/data/rivalActivity.json`, tests `tests/unit/rivalActivity.test.ts`. One call added to `simulateRivalWorld` (rivals/world.ts). No save bump: five-plus optional fields on `rival.world` (`near, goal, condition, pace, heardOfYou, stuck, newsAt, news`).

- **Tracker (14.11):** each stage every live rival gets `near` (stage third), `goal`, `condition`, `pace`, `heardOfYou`.
- **Race (14.13):** pace fast (ambition 75+), slow (resilience 35 or less), else steady; nudged by 3+ net wins/defeats. If a stage didn't move the lead, a pace roll (60/40/20%) gives +1. Lead moves at most 1 per stage. A slow rival that lost twice off-screen is stuck 2 stages (no lead gain). Race lines: 12 voices x ahead/behind/even/champion x 3, shown on observed Progress and Champion news (`raceLine`).
- **News (14.12):** 10 update types x 3 templates. Max 2 per stage (3 in a League stage, counting the older rumor line), at most 1 observed, one per rival per 2 stages, no template repeat inside 4 stages. Mystery and Personal only for recurring-or-higher rivals. 25% of rumor-type news is wrong; a correction (observed) follows the next stage.
- **Rumors (14.23):** 24 in data. Only those the game can back today are live (`LIVE_RUMOR_PAYOFFS`): ru_01, 02, 08, 09, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 24. The rest (hidden cave, rift, tournament, lab, etc.) stay off until W2/W8 build what they point to. False ones get a correction.
- Known limits: Rival Interference (11.1: steal/block/assist at a stop) is not built here. Private updates are never shown (no reveal yet). `near` is a stage third, not a map node. Stage numbers for gaps are region*100+stage.
