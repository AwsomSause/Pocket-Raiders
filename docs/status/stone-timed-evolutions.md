- Stone options for timed evolutions: a matching stone in the Bag can now do a time-of-day or weather evolution (`stones` in `src/core/conditionEvolution.ts`, read by `itemEvolutionTargets`). Eevee: Sun Stone → Espeon, Moon/Dusk Stone → Umbreon, both need high friendship. Water Stone → Goodra, Tempestwing, Megawattrel at their minimum level. The natural night/rain routes still work. No save change. Also: Noctowl (Moon/Dusk Stone, skips the Heartbond need), Rockruff (Sun/Moon/Dusk Stone pick the Lycanroc form), Tyrunt, Amaura, Cosmoem, and Budew/Riolu/Chingling (friendship). Sylveon isn't timed and has none.
