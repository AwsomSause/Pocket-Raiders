# S7a: Impossible Feats and the Resolve Charge

- `core/story2/feats.ts`: the 16-Feat catalog, the Story Run partner's hidden **Resolve Charge** (0 to 100) and banked Feats, run caps (1 a battle, 2 an Act, 6 a run, the same Feat twice, once-a-run Feats once), and the **Dramatic Moments** gate (Off and Hardcore: no Charge, no Feats; Quiet/Full: Feats fire; Prompt: the player arms one with the new **Memory stirs** battle button). Numbers in `balance.json` `story.feats`.
- Charge sources wired: survive a super-effective hit at a quarter HP or less (25), take a hit for an ally (20), win a significant fight with the partner in it (15), come back after a story loss (30, once an Act), a beat of the partner's own `arc_<line>` (20; no such arcs in the data yet). At 100 it banks a Feat (two once Heartbond 9 is latched). The Keystone Memory banks one too (once a run).
- 8 Feats have battle hooks in `engine.ts` (`tryFeat`, no RNG, so no other roll moves): Not Yet, Refuse to Fall (Champion or fact `final_battle`, heals 20%), Through the Numbness, Faster Than Fear, Blind Strike, Read the Room, Against the Odds, Crack the Barrier. Only in boss, Gym, Elite, Champion, rival, Alpha, Totem or raid fights, from turn 3. Elite/Champion respect an `answer_lock` fact.
- A Feat takes the Moment Director's major slot and pays Heartbond as `feat` in the same ranked call as clutch/Hyper (one payer a turn). Its line plus "{pokemon} remembered {memory}." (the partner's most important memory) show as battle lines. The battle prompt shows "Memory stirs: a Feat is charged" as the text-only Resolve spark.
- State in `run.story.v2.feats` and `v2.dramatic` (copied from settings each turn): no save bump.
- Interpretation: Read the Room fires on a foe attack while the partner is at half HP or less (the doc's "one named foe move").
- Tests: `storyFeats.test.ts` (34, incl. a scenario suite forcing each built Feat in Gym, Elite and Champion fights: fires once, no softlock, valid save), `storyFeatsSim.test.ts` (one banked Feat in an even boss fight: about +3 win-rate points over the baseline, 160-seed run; the design target is under 6 per run).
- Limited (in the catalog, never offered yet): Between Them and It, Break the Rule, Unmask the Illusion, Spark Shared, and the 4 backstory Feats; the personality-choice Charge source; the 4-6 s Echo montage presentation (lines only for now). Not browser-checked.
- Move naming rules (core only): `core/story2/moveName.ts` checks a player's move name (3 to 20 characters; letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes; NFKC, look-alike and number-swap folding before the blocklist in `data/story/nameBlocklist.json`; whole-word list for short words so "Grass Pass" is fine; no official move or Pokémon names; no markup). Raw and display names are kept apart, one free rename a run, "keep default", three seeded-free suggestions. Tests: `storyMoveName.test.ts` (7).
- Still open in S7: the naming prompt screen, and Signature I/II and Keystone moves for the 74 lines (the 74 Remembrance lines are not in the data yet: 12 exist).
