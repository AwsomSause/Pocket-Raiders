- **Evil Mode slice 6, "the Line"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 3 section 7, Part 5 sections 4 and 6, Part 7 sections 6, 7, 9 and 11, Part 9 section 10): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) the team can do worse things, at a cost, and be refused by its own people. Lean, Take and Expose work at Dispatch; Heat moves by the day's loudest job and its lamps say what the law is doing; Dread has effects; the Line can be moved once a region at Night; the Roster says what the team really won't do; three more ways to leave.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/line.json` (validated in `schema.ts` `LineFileSchema`): Lean, Take and Expose numbers, their odds terms, Take's three things (stock, a deed, a ledger), the Heat ladder (Rumour to Raid) and its lamps, Dread's words and "comes over on its own" at 8, the three stops (what each allows and darkens), the words for moving the Line, leaving, proof, the Card and the Ledger. `district.json`: locals take the three new methods; three locals have a secret that's true (Expose), two are hard to frighten. `people.json`: Voice fits Expose, Hands fits Take. `hero.json`: how loud each is.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/line.ts`: `lineAllows` (checked in `jobProblem`, so `step` refuses a method darker than the Line, not only the screen), `heatFor` (what each job adds to the loudest, by how it went), `heatStep`/`dreadWord`, `heatForecast`, `lawTerms` (checkpoints at Heat 7: −20 unless Wheels leads), `darkTerms` (Lean: fear known +10, Dread 4+ +10 or 8+ +20, Regard 6+ −10, a hard local −20, the kid within three steps −20; Expose: Regard 4+ +10, Dread 4+ −10), `thingProblem`, `proofFor`/`exposeProblem` (a taken ledger, or a Warm local the team holds on the same street), `linePreview`/`moveLine`, `heartsGo`, `goWithHero`, `realLine`. Stream `evil-line` (new; no existing roll moves).
- The Evening (`dispatch.ts` `darkResult`): Lean (squad of three) that goes: yours, would turn, takings ×1.5, Dread +1, the local Sullen, each other local on the street colder at one in two; failed: Shut, the street one colder, the squad's Loyalty −1 (not a Heart's), Heat +3. Take: stock pays three days once; a deed holds (paper); a ledger is proof, or shows what the street earns; Dread +2; failed without Hands leading, the lead's face is known. Expose: the street one warmer, the target Shut to the team, Regard +1 only if the proof was a witness; the proof's spent; the squad's out two days. Taken: Lean 3, Take by the thing. Dread 8+: one holding a day comes over on its own (nearest first, no squad, no Heat). Heat 8+ (Warrants): known faces are arrested and can't be sent. A district's end: Heat −3, and Dread −1 if nothing was Leaned or Taken.
- Moving the Line (`step` action `moveLine`, at Night, once a region): darker is free; to Whatever it takes every Heart is named and they leave together at breakfast two days on. Lighter costs the hard cases (talent fits a method the new stop forbids): under Loyalty 5 they leave, otherwise they stay and watch (−1). Logged.
- Three more ways to leave (`LEAVE_WAYS`): the Line moved, with the Hero (a posted person beaten by the kid at Loyalty under 5: one in three, Doubts or Kind two in three), arrested.
- The Card: Takes, Gives, Moves (Heat, Dread, Loyalty for a Lean), Holds or turns, How it's written down, and The other way (ask them, with the odds) for each new method; "The other way" button swaps a Lean or a Take to a Persuade.
- Screens (New look only): Dispatch shows Take's three things, who won't (field 8, before the Card), and "Heat tonight: 5 → 7 (Lean, step 4). At this pace: a Raid in 2 days."; methods refused by the Line say so by name. The strip's Heat lamps are amber to 5 and red after, with a red rule from 6; tips give the ladder word and what the law's doing, and Dread's word. The Line page (N, or "Line" on the board): the three stops, what each allows and darkens, who a move costs by name, why it can't happen now, and "Hold to move the Line" (Enter or a press held two seconds; letting go early cancels). The Roster's foot adds "This team doesn't frighten people. Whatever the Line says." and "There's nobody here now who'd stop."

## Limits (slice 6, by plan or for later slices)
- No Raid: Heat 10 just sits there, lit (by plan). No detective, bribes or handing somebody over; Found (9) is a word only. Checkpoints apply to every job (there's no base to be between yet).
- No in-person Lean scene or Take pseudo-stage yet (going in person plays Outplay only). Take of a Pokémon waits for the Pen (slice 10); forgery, Shadow methods and Capture Rigs aren't built. Expose is against a local only (not the neighbour, the law, a Gym or the team itself), and lands at the first Evening (the squad's out two days).
- Moving the Line only at Night (no Crossing yet); the Night knocks to people whose line a darker stop permits breaking come with slice 7. Hearts' quests (which would keep them) aren't built. "Hard cases" are read off talents, not off who was hired under which Line.
- Arrested people stay gone: bail, lawyers and visits come later. Dread's ceiling on Loyalty, prices up and "children called indoors" are words, not rules yet (Lean +10 and Persuade −10 at 4+, and 8+ coming over, are rules).
