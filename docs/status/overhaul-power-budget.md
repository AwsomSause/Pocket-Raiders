# Overhaul slice 4: power budget (content gate)

- Overhaul slice 4 (THE OVERHAUL.md rule 2): `src/core/powerBudget.ts` scores every species and form as content: the Threat Rating (`core/threat.ts`) at level 50 with its default moves and ability. Each species falls in one power class (baby, basic, middle, final, legendary, mythical, form = Mega/Primal/Gigantamax/battle forms), each with a cap in `balance.json` `powerBudget.caps`; `powerScore().withForms` also shows the best battle form a species can reach. Learnable moves have a cap on effective power (power × accuracy × most hits, `moveCap` 250 = Explosion).
- The content gate has a new check, `over-power-budget` (baseline 0): a new form or move stronger than its class allows fails `npm run check`. Today's caps sit at today's strongest in each class (Garchomp 269 for final, Eternatus 330 for legendary, Primal Groudon 354 for forms), so nothing changes in game. `exempt` lists deliberate exceptions with a reason (only Eternamax Eternatus, a boss-only form).
- Tests: `tests/unit/powerBudget.test.ts` (everything fits, classes, forms in the score, at-cap boundary, an overpowered Mega and move are caught, exempt works, move power maths).
- Limited: abilities count only through the Threat Rating's strong-ability list; Tera and Z access are not scored yet (Z-Moves are made in battle from a move, Tera keeps the species' stats). The Mega signature moves thread checks each new move against `moveCap`. No save change.
