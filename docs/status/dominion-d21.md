A Papers, Please desk at the HQ: from Day 2, on every Day, 2 to 4 applicants queue in the rain. **I** on the HQ or the Crew File (or the APPLICATIONS chip on the HQ) opens it; **Esc** goes back.

## What the player can do
- Read the paperwork: ID card (name, age, home town, ID number), Trainer card (badges, latest badge), team, reference letter with its town stamp, and "Why join?".
- **Tab** = Inspect: ◀ ▶ pick a field (home town, ID number, latest badge, Pokémon, levels, letter stamp), Enter checks it. A planted mismatch gets a red ring, the lens shows what's wrong ("should be Lavender", "Vermilion stamps say VMN", "the Marsh Badge is the 6th badge", "Zigzagoon doesn't live in Kanto", "Lv. 55 won't obey 2 badges", "Lavender numbers end in -L"), the "N MISMATCHES" chip counts them and red string ties them together.
- Ask: three quick questions each (◀ ▶ and Enter, or a click), or **T** / Type… for a typed question. Each answer flashes a tell: none, NERVOUS, CONTRADICTION or OVERLY REHEARSED. With a Key-Card, Rotom speaks the applicant's reply (the ✦ Rotom chip says "Quick · tell: … · saved"); without one, the written answer stands. Questions and inspections each cost one of the applicant's 5 patience.
- Stamp: **A** Accept (they join the crew, if the HQ has room), **R** Reject, **D** Detain (to the basement; Heat +3).

## How it works
- Data `src/data/dominion/desk.json` (Zod shape in `schema.ts`, D21 section): truth weights, mismatch counts and kinds per truth (plants and spies by skill), tell odds (and card traits: Coward more nervous, Proud more rehearsed, Joker calmer), the six fields and their lens lines, Kanto's eight badges, town stamp codes, 42 local and 14 off-region species, 12 reference writers, 40 surnames, reasons, 7 quick questions with lines for all four tells, typed answers, effects, report lines, Rotom texts.
- Rules `src/core/dominion/desk.ts` on a new stream `dominion-desk` (no old roll moves): the queue is rolled the first time a Day is seen (`stepInRun`); each applicant's hidden truth is an **honest recruit** (30% of them great trainers: +2 on their best stat, +1 trait point, Loyalty 70), a **police plant** (more likely when Heat is high), a **rival-team spy** or a **glory-seeker**. Their card is D19's, rolled from their name (home town included), so an accepted applicant keeps it. An Accept goes in through D3's door (`admit`), so they are an ordinary member with one Team Type Pokémon.
- The night (World Turn, after the crew): unanswered applicants go home; the basement talks (honest: crew Morale −5; plant: Heat +5; spy: their team loses 10 rival Control in the HQ district; glory-seeker: nothing); an accepted plant adds Heat +2 a night and tips the police off on day 3-6 (Heat +15, gone); a spy leaks the HQ plan on day 2-5 (their team +15 rival Control in the HQ district, a `leaks` entry for D9/D22) and stays; a glory-seeker quits on day 4-7 (Morale −5 to the rest). Accepted members carry a `desk:<truth>` flag for D22 (spies on the crew).
- Rotom: `deskTalk` builds the call (the applicant's card, paperwork and hidden truth as context; the question, typed text wrapped as player text, and the tell the rules rolled). `askClaude` role `line` (Quick at Standard); typed questions follow the "Typed arguments" switch. Rotom answers `{reply, tell}` (Zod-checked); it is stored in `run.ai` under `desk:<day>:<id>:<n>` and replayed on reload. Rotom never decides anything: the truth, mismatches, results and effects are the rules'. `?aifake=1` has a canned desk answer.
- Screen `src/ui/redux/screens/dominion/desk.ts` ported from k5 (booth window, the queue in the rain, desk, paperwork, lens, red string, questions panel, bubble, tell, Rotom chip, stamps, the round-3 overlays and the round-4 `sig4`/`vl4` layers, whose CSS arrives with D26's v4 port). Controller `src/scenes/dominion/deskController.ts`; the Type box is `src/ui/typeBox.ts` (takes every key while open, so typing never stamps). Six sprites copied from the mockup (PROVENANCE.md).
