- **Save safety** (Kice 2026-11-17, "saves keep getting damaged and discarded"). Why saves were lost: (1) a profile saved by a newer build (a second tab or an offline copy of the old page after an update) was judged "incompatible", backed up out of sight and replaced by a NEW profile; newer settings were silently replaced by defaults; (2) one bad field anywhere failed the whole record; (3) a run that named a removed item, move or Pokémon was thrown away whole; (4) no backups of good saves, and fields an older build didn't know (e.g. the Evil Mode block) were dropped on the next save. What's in now (`src/save/safety.ts`, `src/save/saveManager.ts`): a checksum on every save; 3 rolling backups per record (`<key>.bak.1` = previous save, `.bak.2`/`.bak.3` at least 10 min older); writes go to `<key>.tmp`, are read back, then to the real key; a damaged main copy loads the newest good backup (or the temp copy) and is kept under `<key>.corrupt.<time>` (newest 3 kept); a save from a newer version, or with no upgrade path, is never written over (the game plays on and says so); a bad field is reset to its default instead of losing the record; unknown items/moves/Pokémon are dropped from a run (a Pokémon left with no moves gets Tackle); unknown fields are written back into the same run/profile; when storage is full, old backups are pruned and the save tried again; the title screen offers "Export damaged save" next to "Discard damaged save", and Export works on broken JSON. No save version change (the checksum field is optional; old builds ignore it). Tests: `tests/unit/saveSafety.test.ts` (19), `tests/e2e/milestone1.spec.ts` (damaged save). Limits: a save from a newer build stays read-only until the page is reloaded on the newer build; repairs reset a broken field rather than guess its value.
