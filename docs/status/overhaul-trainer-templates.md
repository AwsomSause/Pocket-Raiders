- `overhaul-trainer-templates.md`: Overhaul slice 16 (trainer teams): ordinary trainers' teams follow one of 17 templates (Balanced, Offensive, Defensive, Hyper-offensive, Stall, Weather, Terrain, Trick Room, Setup, Status, Hazard, Speed control, Monotype, Mixed-type specialist, Evolutionary line, Regional, Rare), in `src/data/teamTemplates.json` + `src/core/teamTemplates.ts`. Each species has battle roles (13: attackers, walls, sweepers, pivot, hazard, status, speed control...) read from its family's best form and learnset; a template only re-weights the existing theme pool, so eligibility is unchanged. Early stages (1-3) get only the simpler templates, mid (4-8) and late (9+) unlock the rest. A template used by the last 3 trainers is turned down (`tmpl:<id>` counters in `itemCounters`, no save bump). Template is picked from its own hash stream; `balance.teamTemplates.enabled` switches it off (old-seed test does). Gym Leaders, Elite Four, Champions and rivals keep their fixed teams. Limits: roles and templates shape species choice only (moves and held items still come from the tier system); species/line anti-repeat across trainers waits for the shared generator history (slice 3).
