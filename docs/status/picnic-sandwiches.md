- **Picnic sandwiches (S500, 2026-11-06):** a new camp kitchen action `sandwich` (`camp.json` `sandwich`, handler in `core/camp/index.ts`). Two berries make a sandwich named by their main flavour; it heals the team 15%, lifts mood and lures two types (spicy fire/fighting, dry ground/rock, sweet fairy/grass, bitter dark/poison, sour bug/electric) for the next 3 wild encounters (lure 3, +1 when both berries share a flavour). Once per stop, same berry picker as the Cooking Pot. No save change. Tests in `tests/unit/camp.test.ts`. Limited: berries only (no bread or toppings); the Cooking Pot is unchanged.
