- **Evil Mode slice 5, "the Hero walks"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 18): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) a kid with a starter sets out from the Town on day 1 and walks the district toward the Gym. The player can post somebody at a holding the kid is about to reach, fight the kid in the real battle screen with that person's kit, lose, watch the holding go, and read in the File why the kid is angry. Outplay can now be played in person as a real battle.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/hero.json` (validated in `schema.ts` `HeroFileSchema`): six kids (name, picture, who raised them; pictures from the director's trainer pack), the eight personalities with their steps a day (Part 18 section 7.2), Route 1 openers, a counter per Doctrine, how loud each job is, the sixteen reasons (2 and 7 built, the other fourteen `disabled` with the slice they wait for), the five tiers, battle levels as offsets from the stage's level, marks, the locals' and wild grounds' teams for an Outplay, the trainer classes the battles draw, the road's lines and the File's blanks. `"on": false` is the slice's undo: no Hero is made and slices 1 to 4 play as before.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/hero.ts`: `makeHero` (from the run's seed with its own generator, no gameplay stream: a starter from one of three regions, never the Boss's own; a Route 1 opener; a personality from the rival voice bank's eight; home on the Town's street, starting at the Town). `heroReason` (a pure function of the Log: the first entry with loud 2 or more in their home district; on their street reason 2, elsewhere reason 7; a quiet district that ends gives 7). `heroField` (the Card's field 10: "This is their street. Mr Onuoha's known them since they were born."). `walkHero` (beat 6, once a day, no roll: a holding that would turn turns as they pass and the File learns who told them; one that holds is a wave; a person posted stops the walk for a fight; the Gym gives the Badge and the district is "Taken"). `likelyRoad` (the Board's faint line). `post`/`unpost` (Hold, at Dispatch, no Card: one person, not a minor, at a holding that's yours and still ahead of the kid). `holdResult` (won by the person posted: the kid steps back a step, rests a day, two marks; lost: the holding turns, one mark; the kid is never removed).
- The Hero is a record in the rival system: `run.story.rivals`, id `evil-hero`, made once by `session.ts` `ensureHero` (state `recurring`, never `retired`). **Their party is read there** (`rivalFightTeam`), never copied into the block. The counter joins that record after the third battle with the team.
- Battles (`src/core/evilMode/fight.ts`, `fightEnd.ts`): `beginEvilFight` starts the battle the day is waiting on, in the battle engine as it is. Hold: the posted person's Doctrine kit (at A-1) fights for the player while the Boss's party waits in the box; the kid's party is the record's, cut to the tier's size, at A-2 at tier 0. Outplay in person (beat 3, G, once a day): the Boss's party against the local's team at A+0 (a wild ground's two at A-1, an Alpha at A+1). `run.ts` `takeTurn` hands an Evil Mode battle's end to `endEvilFight` before any main-game after-battle step: the party is given back and healed, phase `map`, never `over`, no prize. An Outplay played in person isn't rolled at Evening and takes no number from `evil-dispatch`.
- Day machine (`day.ts`): actions `post`, `unpost`, `goYourself`, `battleResult`; while a fight waits nothing else moves; `watchRoad` walks the kid first; nobody stays posted overnight; on the seventh day the kid takes the Badge on their way through.
- Screens (New look only): the Board shows the KID marker, their likely road tomorrow (a dotted line), their street (⌂) and who's posted (⚑); Dispatch has a Hold button on your own holdings (H); In person lists the Outplays the Boss can go to (G or a tap); the Morning shows what the kid did on the road. The File (F, or "File" on the board) in `src/ui/redux/screens/evilFile.ts` from `heroScreens.ts` `fileModel`: the Report (who, personality once met, the reason with its Stop/Ask/Find mark, temper, where), the Notebook (six slots that fill only when a Grunt has seen them, with where each came from; marks; eight Badges; what they've seen), the Dossier (what they've said about you; fires, letters and the other three say they come later). Blank lines are drawn blank. The battle screen reads the result's lines and returns to the day.

## Saves
The block's own version goes 4 to 5 (no run/settings bump; the block is optional). `migrateEvilBlock` adds no Hero, no fight, nobody posted and nobody gone in person; the session makes the Hero (and their rival record) on the next load from the run's seed, the same Hero a new run with that seed would get. Tested in `tests/unit/evilModeHero.test.ts` (save and reload, and a battle in progress saved and reloaded).

## Limits (slice 5, by plan or for later slices)
- One Hero; not the other three; no fires; no temper (by plan). The reason never changes once (the strike-through comes later). Only reasons 2 and 7 can be given: until Lean, any loud job on their street is reason 2, and reason 7 also stands for a loud job elsewhere.
- The road is the district's single line, so the personality decides only the pace, not which node (Protector "stops for everyone", Strategist's detours, the Idealist's "toward whatever the team did" are not built). No rest stops, Fronts, Works, rackets or Digs on the road yet.
- Their home street is the Town's four nearest locals; the house and the mother as a holding come later. The File shows the reason from the first loud job (the design says from District 2; there is only one district yet).
- Hold is one person at one holding, with no Card. A kit is one of the Doctrine's three, fixed by who carries it; people's own Pokémon and their kits growing with them come later. The kid's party is at A-2 by tier 0's row and grows only by the counter; candidates from the team's Cards come later.
- In person, only Outplay is played; Persuade and Pay in person, the conditions (doubles, one each, the clock...) and the winning lines by how it was won come later. "Meet them on the road", "Let them win", Mislead and Help are not built.
- Battle trainer classes: the kid is drawn as a Youngster in battle, locals as Workers, wild grounds and Alphas as Rangers (the save checks classes against `trainers.json`).
