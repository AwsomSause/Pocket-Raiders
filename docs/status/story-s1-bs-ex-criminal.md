# Story S1, backstory 1: Ex-Criminal (`bs_ex_criminal`) plus Professor Vance

- Data only, in `src/data/story/*.json` and `text/en.json` (no game behaviour yet; the S5 module loader plays it). 7 characters (Vance is shared by all five backstories), 1 intro + 8 listed events + Tally arc steps + 2 small arcs (`arc_tally`, `arc_pell_defection`, `arc_nell_rescue`), 3 echo events, a reception-modifier event, 4 ending scenes, 2 interactables, 3 Lore entries, 2 Remembrances (Sandile, Pawniard). Every speaking role has 3 variants; all 19 story gates stay at 0 (`tests/unit/storyBackstoryContent.test.ts`).
- Backstory manifest fields (`events`, `echoEvents`, `endingGrid`, ...) come from the Module Validator PR (#860); until it merges the schema drops them on load, so the test reads the raw JSON. After it merges, `validateModule(data, 'bs_ex_criminal')` should return no problems (checked locally on a merge of both).
- Authoring conventions for the other backstories: ids and effect/condition `kind` values are snake_case (the schema forbids camelCase); the prior-role input is the flag `bs.prior_role`; beats with a speaker point to a `dialogue.json` line; narration beats carry their own `textKey`.
- Limits: character art is stand-in (existing trainer sprites; Tally uses the Sandile front sprite); `item_old_pass`, `item_legal_pass`, `item_informant_token` and the two encounter pools (`enc_marauder_squad_ex`, `enc_abandoned_pawniard`) are named but not built; effects are data that the S5 loader will run; `prop_torn_patch` has no art yet.
