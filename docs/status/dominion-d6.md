Districts are now held and lost, every town keeps its own Regard and Dread, and Notoriety climbs a seven-step ladder that opens new actions.

## What's built
- **Data** `src/data/dominion/empire.json` (shape `EmpireFileSchema` in `schema.ts`): the Notoriety ladder (Nobody, Street Gang, Crew, Outfit, Syndicate, Team, Empire; what each step opens), Control rules, the Ledger's lines (which are clean money), and the district tasks' report lines.
- **Rules** in `src/core/dominion/`:
  - `book.ts`: every ₽ in or out lands on one line of this week's page (Rackets, the Boss's jobs, shows, odd jobs, crew jobs, hiring, vehicles, fines, wages, rent, other). Clean money is D5's (`hq.clean`, hqFx.ts): odd jobs and the starting money come through D5's `addClean`, and Street Theater's pay is added there by `afterStep`; spending takes dirty money first.
  - `empire.ts`: Notoriety (larger of Regard and Dread in every town + 2 per held town + 1 per member), the ladder (a step once reached stays open; Street Gang brings a press contact), the action gates, Control (Rackets +2 a week in their town; Heat 40+ wears 1 a night; a Held town nobody holds for a week loses 5), the week's close into history (last 8 weeks), and the crew's district tasks.
  - `ledgerScreens.ts`: the Ledger's three spreads.
  - The World Turn books each step's money (`worldTurn.ts`), and `addWorldEnd` runs D6's end-of-turn after all steps.
- **The Boss's actions**: 10 more played (81 of 150 with D4, D5 and D9): Skim the Till (half a Racket's weekly take), Marry In, Street Theater (clean money), Spray the Wall, Leak a Story (rival Control −6), Befriend the Mayor, Sponsor a Trainer, Silence a Reporter, Cooperate with Jenny, Secret Meeting. The list's old word "Known" maps to the ladder's Crew (50); "Press contact" opens at Street Gang. Gym Takeover moved to D10 (Growth builds it).
- **The crew's tasks**: all 18 that waited for D6 are live (81 of 152 now, with D4, D5 and D9): Patrol the District, Collect, Graffiti Run, Eavesdrop, Poll the Streets, Befriend Locals, Chant, Write the Songs, Street Preacher, Paint the Banners, Spin the Story, Spy on Public Mood, Host a Block Party, Make a Hero Out of a Grunt, Hold, Hold the Line, Keep the Peace, Be Your Face. Each is done in the member's town: on the Morning, P (or the "In …" row) picks the town. Made by `tools/dominion/lists-to-json.py` (ENGINE table). Formed traits Honest Accountant (Rackets +10%) and Heir Apparent (Hold below Admin) are live.
- **Holders**: the Boss working in a town that week, a member on Hold there, or Be Your Face.
- **Screens**: `src/ui/redux/screens/dominion/ledger.ts` (mockup 7: candle, brass corners, stamp, quill, index tabs, red-ink "best week yet!" when it is). Spreads: MONEY (Money | Empire), EMPIRE (Territory | Notoriety), CREW (Crew | Pokémon and stores). ◀ ▶ turn, the tabs jump, L or Esc close. The map's district file shows ♥ Regard and ✊ Dread. DominionScene takes the L key from the game's interaction log while a Dominion screen is up (Shift+L still opens the log). Art: `g-big-nugget`, `g-nugget`, `t-giovanni` (PROVENANCE.md).

## D6b: the hand-offs (`src/core/dominion/town.ts`, `src/data/dominion/town.json`)

- **Hold a Rally** (Boss action 44, needs the Radio Room): 3 new leads (5 from Lv 3), a hat passed round from Lv 2 (₽50 × Lv, clean), no police Heat from Lv 4, Regard +1 everywhere at Lv 5. The first rally opens **Chant Night** (action 95, Night): Regard and Dread, more Regard from Lv 2 and 4, a lead from Lv 3, Regard +1 everywhere at Lv 5.
- **Pay the Wages** (crew task 23): the member on it runs payday. Lv 1 pays the cheapest first (most people get paid), Lv 2 Loyalty +1 for the paid, Lv 3 wages −5%, Lv 4 a bonus for the loyal (Loyalty 70+: a tenth of their wage, Loyalty +3), Lv 5 no late-pay penalty. A failed day costs someone Loyalty −3 ("Mistakes anger crew").
- **15 buildings**: Regard/Dread every week in their district (the HQ's for a room): Barber and Uniform Shop, Trophy Hall (+5% recruit), Propaganda Studio, Press Office, Emblem Gallery, Banner Hall, Charm School (+5% recruit), Culture Wing, Fossil Gallery, Zoo of Stolen (+₽100 a day), Uniform Depot, Dress Code Wall, Standard Bearer's Hall (Morale), Newsstand (Intel). The **Ledger Office** makes the Card show exact odds (the Honest Card). The **Recruitment Center** brings one walk-in lead every 7th morning.
- **Street meetings** (outside-interactions.md sections A and J, 50 rows): after a Boss action in a district (no fight waiting), once you're a Street Gang on the Notoriety ladder (the town notices you once you're known), 20% by Day, 14% by Night, once a day, each row once a run (right time of day; the wanted poster needs Wanted 20+; rival rows need a rival there). The meeting waits on the Region Map as a panel: ▲ ▼ pick, Enter answers (or click). Effects per choice in `town.json` (Regard, Dread, Heat, money, Control, Wanted, Tension, the Hero's attention, Morale, a lead, scouting, the rival's plan); a fight choice starts a battle (win: the choice; loss: its cost, else Heat +2); a Charm or Stealth check uses your best member. Saved in `empire.meet` (optional, no bump). New RNG stream `dominion-town`.
- Limits: the panel has no mockup page; it reuses the map's vehicle-panel look. Choices whose words go beyond these effects (a favour owed, a curse, a story unlock, catching the runaway Pokémon) give the nearest effect (a lead, Morale, Regard) and nothing more. Notes on each partly-working building are in `buildings.json` (`note`).
