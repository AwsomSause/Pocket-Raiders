# Wide formats: Colossus (E4, 5 of 7)

- `src/core/battle/colossus.ts` (rules) + `battle.colossus` in the battle state (optional field, no save bump): an Apex with armor seals, each with a shield bar (`balance.colossus.shieldPct` of the Apex's max HP). Damage drains the first unbroken seal and never reaches HP while a seal remains (also guarded in `settleFaints`: nothing else can KO it). A broken seal removes one of the Apex's moves at the end of the turn (the named move if it knows it, else its strongest; always leaves one). A timer ticks each turn while a seal remains; at zero the Apex escapes: the battle ends as a lost fight with `colossus.escaped = true`.
- Engine touches (three small call sites, nothing near the variant-line hooks): `takeHp` (enemy absorb), `settleFaints` (guard), end of `resolveTurn` (moves/timer/notes).
- `src/core/formats/colossus.ts`: Kanto Phase 6, M2 / XD002 (Mewtwo +4 levels): four seals (Right Shoulder: Mind Vault, Left Shoulder: Null Pulse, Chest: Heart Eclipse, Crown: Obedience), timer `balance.colossus.timerM2` = 10, +1 with `rocket.x.madame`, first seal half broken with `rocket.x.seal-diagram`. Result written once to `run.story.flags['rocket.x.m2']`: `broken` (also plants `legend-cerulean-cave`), `taken` (timer ran out; the climax still counts), `lost` (the director's Second Shot applies). A lost fight is a story loss: party healed, run goes on.
- Screen: `ColossusPlateScene`, Kice's pick Colossus 5 "Blueprint", now compact (one short row per seal: name, percent, what its loss removes, a thin shield bar) and foldable: click the plate (or press C) to fold it to one line (seals left and timer) and click again to open it. It sits beside the battle and only reads the battle state.
- Not built: the full-screen big-sprite layout of the mock-up (the battle's own sprite is used), the Rig Room shutdown lowering M2 a tier, the trio's debt delaying Giovanni's order, the one idle turn after the armor falls, and the signature moves (Mind Vault, Null Pulse, Heart Eclipse are not real moves yet, so the seals take M2's strongest moves instead). The seal-by-seal aim from the mock-up is not a player choice: seals break in order.
- Tests: `tests/unit/colossus.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/wideFormats.spec.ts`.
