# Battle depth (redo of platform alignment)

Redo of #808 (reverted in #809: your Pokémon sat over the UI and the foe had no depth), matched to Kice's reference (2026-11-07). The foe now stands on its feet a little in front of its platform's middle (`feetOrigin` in `src/ui/sprites.ts`: median of each frame's lowest opaque pixel, so one wing-beat frame can't lift the whole animation); Flying types and Levitate hover 14 px above it. Your Pokémon stands low in the foreground (`PLAYER_POS` y 428, feet anchored too) and is clipped at the top of the message bar (`standMask`), so it is never drawn over the bar, even a see-through one; styles without a bar (Ring, Cinematic, Ink) show it whole. Doubles: both foes share the enemy platform, the left one further back. Trainer and sticker row keep their old spot (`TRAINER_POS`). Presentation only; no battle state, RNG or saves.
