- **Evil Mode slice 4, "people"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) the four nameless Grunts are gone. The Roster is the sixteen Founders and Kanto's twelve, each with a talent, a flaw, a line they won't cross, a rank, a wage and a Loyalty shown only as a face and a word. The player can hire from the notice board, ask one question, promote a Grunt to Lead in the Office, let someone go, and be told no in the yard.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/people.json`: seven talents (what they fit, how they say the team's name, what they say when they refuse and when made Lead, what the Board says when nobody has one), eight flaws (how they change the name, the note left by night), the thirty-six lines with the job tags each matches, five ranks with wages, the rules' numbers (Roster cap 8, applicants, Loyalty moves, odds terms), the four questions, and 28 people (16 Founders, 12 Kanto) from Part 10a, each with a status.
- Data `src/data/evilMode/faces.json`: who's drawn as whom. Named people are townsfolk and trainer-class pictures from the director's packs with an armband in the team's first colour (Kice's answer 4); minors are Schoolkid and Youngster pictures with the plain second border. One row a person, with provenance.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/people.ts`: lookups; `teamLoyalty` (the mean of everyone in, rounded down: worked out, never stored) and `bandOf`; `wageOf` by rank and Creed (Officers and Executives a 5% share); `jobTags`, `crossers`, `wontText` (the Card's field 8), `teamWont` ("What this team, as it stands, won't do."), `missingTalents`; `saysName`; the `evil-people` stream (`peopleRoll`, weights by Creed from Part 10a section 1.1, `deckFor`: Founders only in the first region, nobody twice a run); `foundingRoster` (the Creed's count, at least two talents, never a minor).
- Core `src/core/evilMode/roster.ts`: Morning applicants (one a day, three with the Town held; they wait two days), the one question ("Who sent you?" is always "Nobody."), hire (capped), saying no kindly or rudely (Regard −1), Grunt to Lead after three jobs that went (Loyalty +1; anyone at the rank with more jobs done is passed over, −1), let go kindly (a month's wage) or coldly (Heat +1), and leaving at Loyalty 0 by morning with a note by flaw.
- Asking anyway (`dispatch.ts` `askAnyway`, `answerRefusal`): a job whose squad has somebody across their line can't be plainly confirmed; the Card's button reads "Ask anyway" and field 12 adds "Asked X anyway.". The asking itself always costs: the line's heard by the whole Roster and the person's `asked` count goes up. In the yard the Boss withdraws (refuser +2, the others +1), sends someone else (the first free people whose lines are clear; refuser −1) or insists (they leave on the spot, the two nearest in rank −2, the job isn't done today). Nothing else in Dispatch moves until it's answered.
- Odds (`odds.ts` `peopleTerms`): the lead's talent fits, +20 ("Rolls on Voice: Sioned Prys leads"); a second fitting talent, +5; a Lead leading, +5; a Devoted squad +5, a Distant one −10, anyone Leaving −20. Eyes leading scouts two steps deeper; Nerve leading loses an Outplay without the day's rest.
- Evening: wages by person and rank; not paid, −1 each; a job that went counts toward Lead; a talent that fits, +1 once a district; paid on time a whole district, +1 at its end. A minor goes scouting and nowhere else (`jobProblem`, checked in `step`).
- Screens `src/ui/redux/screens/evilRoster.ts` (New look only): the Roster (R, or "Roster" on the board) with two tabs, The Roster and At the notice board; cards with face, armband, rank, talent, flaw, line (or "Hasn't said."), the band's word and face, wage and "why they've come"; leavers dimmed with how; the Office (promotion scene, two answers); the yard (refusal scene, three answers). Keys: R opens; Left/Right tabs (or the actions under a card); Up/Down cards; Enter opens actions or does; Esc backs out. Taps do the same.

## Limits (slice 4, by plan or for later slices)
- No quests and no letters when people leave (by plan). Three of the nine ways to leave: a line insisted on, Loyalty 0, let go. Flaws are shown at hiring but don't fire yet (they fire at the Night, slice 7); "may fire" isn't on Cards yet.
- Only Grunt to Lead is open: Specialist needs the base's rooms (slice 10), Officer the Night's disagreement (slice 7). No demotion. No kits yet (no person's Pokémon until battles use them).
- Lines match the four built methods only: Outplay against a local ("won't fight anyone who's not ready") and the seventh day ("won't work Sundays"). The other tags are in data for later methods. Line 36 (the unsaid line) and caught lines aren't built.
- The team's Loyalty band moves the strip only; its Dispatch odds (+5 / −5) and its effects (one leaves a district at 3-4, Mutiny at 0) wait for later slices. Applicants come by Creed only, not yet by yesterday's acts, Regard or Dread; an applicant met at the cap isn't told "when there's a bunk". Over-sixties don't come back each morning.
- Soo-ah's minor flag doesn't lift at District 4 (no districts after the first yet); her status is `limited`. The Mess (Beatriz's other allowed job) doesn't exist yet.
- The block keeps the key `crew` for the Roster (the design calls it `people`); renaming it would only churn slices 2 and 3.
