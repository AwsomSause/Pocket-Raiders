# Evil Mode slice 18: the voice

- **Evil Mode slice 18, "the voice"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 25a, Part 25b, Part 24b, Part 24c, Part 30 section 3.10): the team hears the Radio, reads the paper and gets post. Built on slice 16 (#1470).

## What's built
- **The Radio** (`src/core/evilMode/voice.ts`): each Morning one item from yesterday, in the order of Part 25a section 2.2: a neighbour's tick (or a tick stopped), a Badge with the Leader's words, a loud job (named only at standing 5+), a Work finished, a Front's advert, a police appeal at Heat 7+, a request (the Late Shift, Mum, G., the fishmonger; a birthday once the Night's `birthdays` flag exists), else the weather. Nine presenters; Radio lines for Giovanni's, Archer's, Archie's and Maxie's five ticks, and for each stopped. It never guesses. The set can be switched off at Night once a run; Hortense switches it back on. The Hero hears the same item (the File's "On the Radio").
- **The paper**: one headline a Morning. Below Regard and Dread 5 it prints a guess (waived bakery, unsigned Work, heist, Front, Dig, the Pen, a quiet week) and never the team's name; above, the standing bands; "BOTH." only with both at 5+ and the interview. The Hero's first and third Badge, a tick stopped, a neighbour's stance. A coldly let-go leaver's letter appears in the paper, never in the post. Headlines move nothing.
- **The post**: at most one piece a Morning, the older first; at Heat 7+ a day late and steamed. Forty Officer letters (seven talents × four, eight flaws once the flaw's found, four any Officer sends once), fourteen with three answers; the garrison (five, one answerable when there's a Pen); the cells (by how they were caught, then their flaw; the Kind's asks you to write to a stranger); leavers by way (line, line moved, the Hero, stayed, let go kindly); Tomasz's mother, Beatriz's aunt, Old Obadiah's daughter (one a region); the town by a held holding's mood; the empty envelope once a run at Dread 7+; Class 2, Erika (Befriended), the detective (a region under Heat 3); Giovanni's fruit-basket card by region number at Truce or better (none at War, a big one after); Archie's bottle and Maxie's memo; a Grunt's postcard. Replies move nothing by more than one (money aside) and are kept in the Log.
- **Barks**: the ten moments' pools (Loyalty band, mood, flaw, talent, Doctrine, Creed, the Line, the Hero's temper, Heat 8-10, the Crossing) and six lines for each of the sixteen founders. Picked by a hash of the seed (no gameplay stream), never saved, never at a fire or the last room, no line twice in a district, a Shut person never barks.
- **The Ledger's margin**: a Books at Loyalty 6+ writes one pencil note a district (Ngozi, Old Obadiah, Priya, any Books).
- **The endings**: the front page card (Part 25a section 3.6) and the detective notebook's last line by detective and caught or not (Part 25b section 10).
- **Knocks 41 to 130** (`src/data/evilMode/knocks2.json`): all ninety written in slice 7's knock shape, with 202 new flags and 64 new needs.
- **Screen (New look)**: the Morning shows the Radio, the paper and the envelope; the post page (P, or the Post tag): open, leave, answer, pin; the drawer; a bark's bubble over the board. The tag row scrolls sideways when it's too long.
