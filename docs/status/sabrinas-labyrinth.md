Scattered party, a Shuppet/Duskull/Drifloon/Gastly companion as the only usable Pokémon (levels 10 to 40, evolves on the way), an unwinnable first fight with the tombstone and ghost swarm, six rooms (your own body in a room you cannot enter, your party as ghosts plus a shade fight, doors to places from earlier regions, the villager who says one thing, the children's drawings, the small grave), then Sabrina and Alakazam. Text expanded and made more emotional; the companion copies you at first and leads by the end. If the ghost would faint, `ghostRescue` calls every Ghost-type and Alakazam goes down. Next-region return unchanged (jump scare, Mega Stone, Haunting). Haunting is now a Ghost special move, power 80, then **Haunted**: a fixed four-turn cycle (stat falls, 50% confusion, stat falls, 50% confusion), two turns for bosses, no re-apply for two turns after it ends (`vol.hauntedTurns`, `vol.hauntLock`). Loss resets the Labyrinth (nobody freed, first room); the new setting `labyrinthResume` (Gameplay and Accessibility, read at run start) resumes at the room reached (`canon.labResume`, `lab.room`). No save version bump (optional fields, battle volatile defaults). Limits: "see true" is automatic, not a button; the false slot is a function of turn and Pokémon (no gameplay roll); rooms are choices, not mini-games, except the shade fight and Sabrina.
