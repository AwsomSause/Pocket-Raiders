# Evolution scene (Kice 2026-11-17)

- **Evolution scene** (2026-11-17): every time one of your Pokémon evolves (after a battle, an evolution item from the Bag, a reward, an event choice, a Camp action) the classic sequence plays in both looks: "What? X is evolving!" (waits for a key or click), the Pokémon turns to a white silhouette that flickers between its old and new shape faster and faster with rising light, a flash, the new form in colour with the evolution jingle and its cry, then "Congratulations! Your X evolved into Y!" and one line per move learned (each waits for a key or click). A press during the flicker jumps to the reveal. Old UI draws it in Phaser (`cine-evolve`), New UI as a full-screen card. Timings, lines and "who evolved" are pure data in `src/core/presentation/evolutionScene.ts` (no RNG); drawing in `src/ui/evolutionScene.ts`. Follows Settings: Evolution animations / all animations off skips the flicker, reduced motion cross-fades without the flash, Animation speed scales it, low flash dims the flash. Tests: `tests/unit/evolutionScene.test.ts`, `milestone10.spec.ts` (Old UI), `battleNewUi.spec.ts` (New UI).
- **B stops it; full scene everywhere** (2026-11-18, follow-up): an evolution earned in battle (level, friendship, held item, a weather/move route, a Pokémon Moment) now waits as a decision (`pending` kind `evolve`, run save v46) instead of happening inside the battle's end. The Decision screen plays the evolution scene; B (X, Esc, Backspace) on the opening line or during the flicker stops it ("Huh? X stopped evolving!"), and it is offered again after a later battle, as in the classic games. The rules evolve the Pokémon only when the flicker ends (`resolveDecision({ kind: 'evolve', accept })`), so a reload during the scene replays it and nothing applies twice. A Pokémon past two thresholds gets a second evolve decision. Mid-battle evolutions (a foe's drama evolution, a story evolution in battle) now play the full scene over the battle too ("What? The foe's X is evolving!"), then the battle shows the new form. Tests: `tests/unit/milestone8_75.test.ts` (wait, accept, stop, gone, save v46), `milestone10.spec.ts` (evolve and B-stop, Old UI), `battleNewUi.spec.ts` (New UI).
- Limits: evolutions out of battle (an item from the Bag, a reward, a level-up Rare Candy, an event, Camp) still happen first and the scene only shows them, so B can't stop those (stones can't be stopped in the classic games either). A foe's evolution can't be stopped. Pocket Town actions (between runs) don't play the scene.
