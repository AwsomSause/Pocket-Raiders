## What's built
- **Incursions for a Boss** (`src/core/evilMode/tear.ts`). Ten Appetites fed by what the team does (in-person jobs, Outplay wins, districts with no failed job, Evenings over ₽20,000, quiet days, wins under 40%, Takes, the same squad three times, loud days, people who left). The Beasts, thresholds and Ultra Rules are the main game's (`core/ultra`).
- **Static**: the day after a tally crosses. Nothing on the Board; the Radio, a bark, a kit, the cat, the Night's knock (Kiele if she's on the Roster), and a line in the Hero's File.
- **A Tear** the next Morning over the held holding whose person is fondest of the team. Its Ultra Rule is in every battle in that district (and none outside it). The holding pays nothing, its street half. **Heat can't rise while it's open.** Who comes: the district, the detective, the neighbour (by Boss), the kid, Looker from the third region.
- **The Tear's Honest Card**: Takes, Gives and Not known read "We don't know."; CONFIRM still works. Four choices: Go through (In person: the Beast, wild, in its own rule; catchable), Hold the street (six people off every other job for three days; it comes out to an empty street), Leave it (the Incursion), and "(Go through together.)" only when the kid is Set or worse (a File line and a mark). Regard ±1 (2 in Unova) by the choice.
- **The Incursion**: after three days. It meets the first squad sent (nobody wins), or takes the next Outplay in person as the Beast.
- **A Beast in the building**: caught, it's Homesick 0; it rises a step a Morning when the team doesn't do what drew it and falls when it does; at 10 it leaves (out of the party/box, once). **Poipole** follows on Longing: a plate can be put out; it's never a person on a job.
- **The Shadow branch** (`src/core/evilMode/corruption.ts`, only reached through `edges.ts shadowOn`). Capture Rigs (Whatever it takes, at Dispatch, wild ground: six kits, Heat +4, Dread +2, Corruption +1 the first time, the ground stripped for the region; the Card's field 12 reads "Processed."). The Shadow Lab is now a real room (Whatever it takes only; its keeper is the lowest Loyalty, named; Corruption +1 built and +1 a size; 1/2/4 a district through, +1 each six; the keeper knocks twice). Shut, Relieve (Loyalty to 5 and no further), Purify (In person, Regard +1, the figure stays). Shadow orders answer a refusal by sending the Pokémon (Corruption +1 the first time). The ladder: rung 1 one person leaves in the doorway; ceilings 8/6/4; Heart applicants stop at 2; the Patron leaves at 8. Corruption never falls.
- **The setting** "Evil Mode Shadow branch" (`expansion.evilShadow`, Available by default) is copied into the block at founding. Removed means removed: no Lab on the build list, no Rig, no Corruption figure, line 7 reads without the word, the Line's third stop reads "Everything Grey allows.", no Corruption row at the Crossing.
- **Screen (New look)**: the Edges page, key **I** (U is the Charter house rule, slice 22; J is slice 17's Suspicions) (its tab shows in the board strip only while something is at the edges or the Shadow branch can be used, since the strip is full on a phone; it reads "A Tear: open, N days" while one's open, and the chip is on the frame). Left: what's off and the Card; right: CONFIRM and the rows. The yard gets a fourth answer, "(A Shadow order.)", with the branch in.

## Limits
- The home world's law (Double Battle, no switching) isn't applied to the Tear fight (the main game's own limit for most laws); the Tear fight is a single wild battle.
- The Lab's kits (+2 levels, Shadow moves) and the Rig's six kits are counted, not yet given to a Grunt's Hold kit. A Shadow Pokémon's battle tray and purifying by the main game's means aren't wired; Purify is a job that counts.
- The "together" mark is a File line and a mark; the last room doesn't yet say its line. "Tell the Hero" at a fire and the Lab's last scene at Corruption 10 are not built.
- The Meteor's stone (`stoneBack`) exists as a rule but no Dig calls it yet. Aether, Hokulani and the crater are later regions (switched off).
- Undo: set `"on": false` under `incursions` and/or `shadow` in `src/data/evilMode/edges.json`.
