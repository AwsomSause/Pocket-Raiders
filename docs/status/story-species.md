# Story Overhaul S15: ten new species

- Built: Tuftin (baby, evolves into Eevee at level 12), Oracowl (Noctowl, level 42), Tempestwing (Swellow, level 46), Megawattrel (Kilowattrel, level 52), and six Legends: Equinar, Hollow Shepherd, Brinecrown, Veinheart (legendary), Lumeria, Verdelis (mythical). Types, stats and levels follow Design v2 Part II. Source: `tools/build-story-species.ts` (`npm run data:story-species`) writes `src/data/storySpecies.json`, merged in `src/data/index.ts` (`patchSpecies` also makes Eevee evolve from Tuftin). Legends are not in the wild pool.
- Limits (status: limited): art is stand-in, recoloured PokeRogue sprites made by `tools/make-story-sprites.py` (no PixelLab access), so real art should replace `custom/<id>` files. Evolutions are level-only; Heartbond level, night and storm conditions are not in the engine yet. Signature abilities (Wise Eye, Eye of the Gale, Grid Surfer) are not built; existing abilities stand in. No Dex text, poses, cries of their own (they reuse the base species' cry and icon), or Legend signature moves/events yet.
- Tests: Story old-seed fingerprint test now leaves these species out of its content (like other late additions).
