- **DOMINION slice D22, "spies on the crew"** (Rotom slices, 2026-11-19; build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode-rework/BUILD_SLICES.md` row D22 and its Follow-ups line, design `/mnt/project-files/plans/claude-api/CLAUDE_API_DRAFT.md` §5.3 C, mockup a6 = `plans/evil-mode-rework/mockups-v4/index.html` #k6): Team Rocket's spies are now real members of your crew. Clues pile up night by night; at Suspicion 40+ you can take the member into the back room and **Confront** them (**F** on the Crew File). The truth was fixed when the rival bought them; the rules pick how it ends.

## What the player can do
- See **Suspicion** on a member's Crew File (orange from 1, red from 70) and a Morning alert when it reaches 40 ("F on the Crew File to confront them").
- **F** on the Crew File opens **Confront** (mockup a6): the swinging bulb, the member at the table, their photo next to the rival Boss's, the intercepted note, red string, the Suspicion panel (big red number, HIGH/WATCH tag, the newest three clues), "The talk", the tell stamped over their head, and the four endings "picked by the rules".
- Ask up to **four** things: Evidence, Where were you?, Offer ₽300 (once), Threaten (−3 Loyalty), or **type your own line** (any letter starts typing; Enter says it; Esc leaves the box). Each answer can show a **tell**: NERVOUS, CONTRADICTION or OVERLY REHEARSED. An innocent member can only look nervous.
- **Accuse**: an innocent member is cleared (Loyalty −15, Morale −10, everyone else −2 Morale, Suspicion 0). A spy's ending is rolled: **Confess** (they name their contact: this week's rival plan is shown, the rival loses a spy and 10 intel), **Double agent** (they stay and feed fake plans: −2 rival intel a night, and they count as your spy inside), **They run** (member gone, +15 rival intel) or **Battle** (their own team against yours; win and they're thrown out, lose and they get away).
- **Let it go** (or Esc): Suspicion −15. Walking out before asking anything costs nothing. One confront a day.
- **Plant a Mole** (Boss action 54, night, from the Follow-ups line): sends your best Charm + Stealth member of rank 2 or more inside the rival team (the plan becomes visible, raids on you hold better); a big success reads this week's plan at once. Closed with a reason when nobody can go.

## How it works
- **Data** `src/data/dominion/spies.json` (Zod in `schema.ts`, D22 section): Suspicion numbers, clue chances and gains, who a rival buys (trait weights: Loyal never, Greedy most), 15 clue texts shared by moles and red herrings, the five questions with mole/innocent lines, the tells, the four endings and their trait changes, all lines and the screen words, the Rotom system text.
- **Rules** `src/core/dominion/spies.ts`, block `spies` (optional, no block bump): `syncMoles` keeps the moles in step with D9's spy count (a new spy buys a member; a counter-spy taking the count down **exposes** the most suspected mole at Suspicion 100); `spyStep` (World Turn, after the rival step): clues, red herrings, cooling after 3 quiet nights, Gossips leaking (+2 intel a night), double agents lying; `startConfront`/`askSpy`/`accuse`/`dropTalk`; `endWeights` (pressure, Loyalty, the bribe ×2 for a Greedy member, traits; no Pokémon = no battle); the spy battle's settler; `plantMole`. Streams: `dominion-spy` (pick, clues), `dominion-spy-talk` (replies, tells), `dominion-spy-end`. No old stream moves.
- **D19 effects now live**: Loyal "a rival's bribe won't turn them", Greedy "a bribe can turn them", Gossip "tells spies your plans" (`spy` field on the trait effect; no "(from D22)" on the card any more).
- **Rotom**: a typed line asks Rotom to voice the member's reply (`askClaude`, role line, use "typed", id `spy:<member>:<day>:<turn>`), given the fixed truth, the clues and the tell the rules already rolled; the reply is stored in `run.ai` and replaces the written reply on screen with the "✦ Quick · tell: …" chip. With no Key-Card (or Rotom off) nothing is sent and the written reply stays. Rotom never changes a result.
- **Screen** `src/ui/redux/screens/dominion/spy.ts` (v3 classes + k6's overlays), model `src/core/dominion/spyScreens.ts`, controller `src/scenes/dominion/spyController.ts`. The text box is a real input: the game's keys ignore it while it has the focus.

## Saves
