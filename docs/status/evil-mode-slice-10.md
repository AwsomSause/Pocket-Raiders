- **Evil Mode slice 10, "the base"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 16, Part 17, Part 5's costs, Part 29's New look): the player can build somewhere to be found, defend it fairly, walk through it as the kid would, and be raided.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/base.json` (validated in `schema.ts` `BaseFileSchema`): six sites and Kanto's own (the lodge), each a grid with a front door, a Common Room and an Office the game puts furthest from the door; the nine other regions' sites written and disabled with what they wait on. Twelve rooms at up to three sizes (Part 5's costs), by Creed and Line; the eighteen adjacency pairs; fourteen Defend pieces; puzzle layouts (ice, spin, conveyor, warp, bins); quiz facts; the walk, Night, Listening Post and Raid texts; the detective.
- Core: `baseGrid.ts` (the grid, puzzle solvers, the fairness check, the intruder's route, the Hero's delay), `baseFx.ts` (what working rooms and pairs do; Roster cap), `base.ts` (site offer and pick, build/enlarge/move/sell, staffing = the Lead-to-Specialist promotion, Defend pieces and posts, Walk the base, the window, Evening and district effects, Listening Post notes), `walk.ts` (Walk it), `raid.ts` (the warning at 9, the Raid at 10, wing by wing, the Office, the cells, bail and visits), `baseScreens.ts` (the page model).
- Rule hook: the Garage's scout reach and fourth squad go through `regionRule.ts`; the Barracks cap and applicants through the Roster; the Reckoning waits for the base's delay (at most two days a region).
- Battles: Walk it lends a Hero-built party against the base's own people (a Devoted Grunt twice, the second at one Pokémon on 1 HP); the Office battle is the detective's team at A+1 against the Boss's party. Both go through the real battle screen.
- Screen (New look): the **Base page (B)**. No base: three sites. A base: the grid (rooms, posts, pieces, the route), Build · Staff · Defend (T), the fairness light and its sentence, the failing square in red, adjacency notes, Walk the base / the window / visit / bail, WALK IT, the Pen's latch. Walk it shows the route as stops over the site's background. The warning shows the four choices; the Raid shows the Siege Map (wings, orders, the back way's order, the turn, the door, the Office). The Listening Post's notes show at Morning.

## Limits
- No Pen unless the Line allows it (Grey or Whatever); no job takes a Pokémon yet, so the Pen stays empty. No Lab at all yet (by plan).
- Limited in data, with what they wait on: gadgets, relics, the Wall, getaways, rackets, the forecast; the Infirmary's Small and Medium; "Walk into the station" (endings, slice 15); the Lawyer, Jailbreak and Leave cards.
- A lock's key on the page is always in the Common Room, and a quiz door asks the first three facts (the rules take any key square and any facts).
- Undo: switch `base.on` off in `base.json`: the base is one room again.
