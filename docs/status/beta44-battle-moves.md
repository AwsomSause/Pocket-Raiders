- **Beta 4.4 follow-up (moves found in battle)**: When your Pokémon shows a move it had been working on mid-battle (bond "New Move" at low HP), the move no longer replaces one of its moves. It goes in the extra Hyper slot for that battle (+20 power, 5 PP), and after a win or catch the learn-move screen asks whether to keep it, even if that Pokémon fainted later. A Pokémon with fewer than four moves can now learn into its free slot from that screen ("Learn X") without forgetting anything. Rival Pokémon still swap a weak move for the new one. Files: `bond/index.ts revealMove`, `battle/hyperMove.ts endHyperMoves`, `run.ts resolveDecision`, `DecisionScene.buildLearnMove`. Tests: bond, hyperMove.
