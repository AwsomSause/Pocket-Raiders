- Growth slice 8 (ideas S52): Regional Form Shifts. Camp menu > Camp > "Form shift": swap a living party Pokémon with its regional variant (Arcanine <-> Hisuian Arcanine; any base with a `regional` form in the species data) for one Moon Stone (the ritual item; `FORM_SHIFT.item`). Reversible. Level, moves, nature, bond and the HP share stay; the ability slot resets to the first if the new form lacks it; the new form is marked caught in the dex. Code `src/core/formShift.ts`; no save change. Tests `tests/unit/formShift.test.ts`. Design choice: Moon Stone is an existing shop item, so no new item or art. Limited: moves keep their old list even if the new form would not normally learn them.
