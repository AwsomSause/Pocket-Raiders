- Raids & bosses slice 3 (`s-raids-bosses`): **S391 Raid Roles**. In a raid each of your Pokémon fights as a Tank (HP/Def/Sp. Def average tops its best attack by 5), Striker (best attack beats bulk by 15) or Support (the rest), from base stats (`raidRole`). Striker hits ×1.1, Tank takes ×0.9, a Support on the field heals 1/16 max HP each turn (not in no-healing raids). Numbers in `raids.json` `roles` (default if missing). Role is derived, nothing saved, no version bump. Shown in the den's mechanics line. Limited: no party-slot picking and no role badge in the battle UI yet. Tests `tests/unit/raidRoles.test.ts`.
