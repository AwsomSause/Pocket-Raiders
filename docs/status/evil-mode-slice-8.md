- **Evil Mode slice 8, "the Gym"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 8a, Part 8b's Kanto, Part 29 section 12.2): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) the team can deal with the district's Gym Leader five ways: Beat, Buy, Bend, Replace, Befriend. The player can buy Koga, or try (he has no price, and the door says who shut it).

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/gym.json` (validated in `schema.ts` `GymFileSchema`): Kanto's eight Leaders, first to eighth (Brock and Giovanni untouchable; Misty, Lt. Surge, Erika, Koga, Sabrina, Blaine in full), plus Janine as Koga's heir (young-Leader flag). Each has the Gym's rule (with a status), a Buy answer (only Blaine has a price: ₽13,500, the formula's for district 6, with the other way), what bends them (kind, two to four steps, the lights-off scene), the Creeds they respect, where they go if replaced, what they say beaten, to the Hero by way, at the gate and walking in. Numbers for each way; the Card for each door; the shut words. All text from the design parts above. `"on": false` is the undo: the Gym's a holding like any other.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/gym.ts`: `gymDoors` (all five, every day; a shut door always names who shut it: "Shut by your Line", "Shut by him", "Shut by her age", "Not scouted.", "Shut: no Officer", "Hasn't heard of you." on the first Gym, "The Hero's there." on the eighth). `gymAct` (at the In person beat, the Boss's one outing; refused in `step` unless the door's open): Beat and Replace start a battle; Buy pays once and is logged as a bribe (each Heart −1); Bend does one step a day (Sabrina's is checked against the Ledger: a Lean or Take in her district fails it; Koga's gets him the Elite Four hearing, two in three he gets the seat and the Gym's Janine's); Befriend is three exchanges by Creed. `gymResult` (Beat won: Sullen, Dread +1, the Town's street colder at one in two; lost: the party's healed, the Leader leads differently next time. Challenge won: the Officer holds the chair and can't be sent; lost: they're +1 and the way shuts). `gymEvening` (Bought: found out at one in ten each Evening; Beaten: a Lean on the Town's street Shuts them; Befriended: a note, then they walk in, Regard −2). Heat from the Gym counts toward the day's loudest. `heroAtGym` (a Bent Leader beats the Hero first, two or three tries; the Badge is half a tier off a Beaten or Bought Leader; the File learns by way; a Gym that's dealt with no longer ends the district when the kid wins). Stream `evil-gym` (no existing roll moves).
- Battles (`fight.ts` `leaderTeam`): the Leader's own main-game Gym team for that Gym (species, moves, training), every member at A+2, boss gear (`gearTeam`, `giveBossItems` from a hashed generator), gimmicks by `gimmickSlots` (none before Stage 5 of Region 1, Blaine and Sabrina get one), class `gym-leader`. The Officer's challenge fights with their Doctrine kit while the Boss's party waits in the box.
- Scouting: the Gym can be scouted from Dispatch (a talk with the caretaker); Buy and Bend need depth 2.
- Screens (New look only): the Board's right rail on the Gym (the five, open or shut with a word why; the Leader and their standing as a word). The Gym page (Y, or "Gym (Y)" on the board): the five doors, the chosen door's Card, the standing and what last moved it, "Do it" (Enter). Title tags now shrink so every page tag fits.

## Limits (slice 8, by plan or for later slices)
- One region's Leaders (by plan). Only Cerulean is playable until slice 9 lays Kanto's other districts; the others are tested by setting the district.
- Gym rules are written on the door but only singles are fought: the wet floor, rooms (bins, pads, quiz, invisible walls), berries-only and the clock wait for the engine and slice 10's defences (each rule's status is `limited`).
- Bend's steps are done in person, one a day, not as the Work/war/Escort jobs the design names (those cards come with slices 11 and 13). Befriend at Grey needs a Work (slice 11), so it's shut by the Line there for now.
- Replace has no design screen yet (type, room, three Gym trainers, Badge, the sixteen door rules, Saturdays): the Gym runs as it was. The Disgraced Leader's return isn't built. The Hero at a Replaced Gym takes a full Badge.
- The Hero's Gym fight is told, not played (who wins and how many tries). The Strategist's lowball question and a Talks Grunt finding out a Buy aren't built. "What the Hero learns" from a Beat is a line, not the Boss's six by name.
- The district still ends Held at eleven; "the Gym: never asked" is a line at the end, not yet a Board mark.
