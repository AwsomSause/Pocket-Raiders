# Premier Balls replace Hyper Potion rewards

Every reward that gave a Hyper Potion now gives a Premier Ball: reward drafts (the reward card and the Medic Gamble deal), events, legendary events, minigame prizes, raids, dungeon chests, totems, expeditions, pseudo-stages, camp mix, plaza prize, guild cook, legacy parcel.

Not changed on purpose: the Hyper Potion item itself, the shop, the crafting recipe, merchant purchases, the overlevel XP item in balance.json, trainer battle potion tiers, and shop price modifiers.

Tests updated: camp.test.ts, draftPersonality.test.ts. No save version change (item ids only).
