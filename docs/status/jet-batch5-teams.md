- Beta 0.7 Jet batch 5 in the teams (2026-11-16): new Jet Pokémon are worked into fixed boss teams (1,869 of about 11,800 gym/Elite/champion team slots) and 54 Evil Team rank slots. `node tools/jet/add-new-to-teams.ts` writes `src/data/jetTeamSwaps.json`, laid over `bossTeams.json`/`evilTeams.json` when content loads (`applyTeamSwaps` in `src/data/index.ts`), so the generated files are untouched and old-seed tests (story.test) leave the swaps out (`jetTeamSwaps: {}`). A new Pokémon only replaces a member it fits (evolves from it, is its Alolan/Galarian/Paldean form, or shares a type at similar power and stage); typed bosses only take their own type or swap an off-type member; aces, Mega/Tera/Z/Dynamax gimmick mons, legendaries and power-exempt species are never touched; no species used more than 25 times. Moves come from the species' own learnset. Wild/ordinary trainers: the pre-evolutions that pushed Farfetch'd, Kangaskhan, Scyther, Pinsir, Tauros, Lapras, Shuckle, Heracross and Miltank off the first-stage list are now Kanto/Johto `wildSpecies`; Alolan Trapinch/Dratini (Alola) and Paldean Golett (Paldea) roam their region. Limit: re-run the script after changing the Jet species or the boss builder.
