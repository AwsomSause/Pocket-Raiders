- Raids & bosses slice 7 (`s-raids-bosses`, no save version bump: new optional raid-fight field `coop`): **S410 Team Co-op Raids**. At an ordinary den, "Before you go in" has a "Co-op: ON/OFF" row (2 players, one device). With it on, your party is split by slot (1st, 3rd, 5th = Player 1; 2nd, 4th, 6th = Player 2), the boss has 40% more HP, the crowd meter grows 4 faster each turn, the prize is x1.25 with 1 more draft choice, and each player has 3 cheers of their own (`raids.json` `coop`). The command menu says "PLAYER n: you're up! Pass the controller." for whoever owns the Pokémon in front; the role badge shows P1/P2; the status line shows both players' cheers; a win reports each player's damage. Needs two Pokémon able to fight; works with one card, bot or talk, not at story raids or practice. Limited: hot-seat on one screen (no second controller or online play); both players share the one bag and the draft picks. Tests `tests/unit/raidCoop.test.ts`, e2e in `tests/e2e/raids.spec.ts`.
