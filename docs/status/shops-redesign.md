- Shops redesign, slice 1 (Kice 2026-11-02; mock-ups in project files `redesign/shops/`): Kice picked six looks as six kinds of shop, each to get its own stock and a perk. Every shop stop now has a type (`core/shopTypes.ts`, `shopTypeAt`): Poké Mart, Aero Boutique, Kecleon's Bazaar, Supply Depot, PokéNet Shop, or Night Market (after dark, or while Giovanni's Black Market runs). The type comes from a hash of the run seed and the stop's place, so no gameplay roll changes. Built: **Poké Mart** (Shop Channel: pinstripes, category capsules, big rounded tiles that puff up and wobble, a "downloading" bar and a BOUGHT stamp on buy) and **Aero Boutique** (Aero Carousel: goods on a glass turntable in a bubbly sky, Left/Right spins the ring, Up/Down jumps between categories, buying pops a bubble). On both, party icons glow with "CAN USE" for stones and form items, the money counts down, and a buy refreshes the screen in place (no more restart flicker). Mouse: hover focuses, click buys. Framework: `ui/shopStyles/` (one file per look; `ShopScene` keeps state, input and buying), view model `shopView` in `ui/shopLook.ts`. Types not built yet draw as the Poké Mart. Stock and prices are unchanged in this slice. Limited: Sell still uses the old list. Tests `tests/unit/shopTypes.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/shopStyles.spec.ts`.
- Shops redesign, slice 2 (Kice chose "own stock and a perk per type"): entering a shop stop fixes its kind on the run (`run.shop`, optional, no save bump; cleared on leaving) and each kind sells its own goods (`typedStock`, numbers in `balance.json` `shopTypes`). **Poké Mart**: the usual stock. **Aero Boutique**: only held, training, evolution and form goods at +10%, plus the nearest 3 locked rare ones early. **Kecleon's Bazaar**: 8 of the Mart's goods (different at every stall) and one haggle per visit (35% + 0.5% per Charisma point, max 80%: a win cuts that item 30%, a loss adds 15%; fixed per stall and item, so reloading can't reroll it; `haggleItem`). **Supply Depot**: only balls and medicine; buying 5+ of one item at once takes 10% off (`buyItems`; its basket screen comes in slice 3). **PokéNet**: the Mart stock plus the next locked item early, +5%, and one "deal of the visit" at 20% off. **Night Market**: the Mart stock plus 2 mystery rare goods no Mart lists (₽1800 rare, ₽3600 epic). New looks: **Kecleon's Bazaar** (striped awning over the town backdrop, Kecleon bobs and changes colour to the focused good and comments on it, a carpet with hand-written price tags, a friendly-price meter with the haggle odds, a Buy / Haggle / Never mind prompt on the first pick, a happy hop or offended shake) and **Night Market** (swaying lanterns over the night-town backdrop, Meowth and Persian, goods on a rug, mystery and black-market goods "under the counter" behind a purple curtain, Meowth flips a coin on each buy). Depot and PokéNet still draw as the Poké Mart. Tests `tests/unit/shopTypes.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/shopStyles.spec.ts` (all four built looks).
- Shops redesign, slice 3 (last): all six kinds now have their own look. **Supply Depot** (Basket & Register): metal warehouse shelves under a hazard-striped header. Picking a good puts it in the basket on the clipboard (nothing is paid yet). Each line shows its bulk saving (5+ of one item = 10% off). **Checkout** rings everything up at once with one bulk buy per line (`buyItems`): "KA-CHING!", and a receipt slides out. Back (or "Empty basket") first empties the basket, and a second Back leaves. Key items, Mega Stones and Z-Crystals go in the basket once at most (`oneOnly`). The basket can't cost more than your money. **PokéNet Shop** (Web Store): a glossy mid-2000s browser window on an Aero desktop, with an address bar, a cart that counts this visit's buys, a scrolling marquee with the day's deal, category tabs that light up for the focused good (click one to jump there), a Deal of the Day banner, and product cards with "Buy now". A buy flies the item into the cart. Buying still happens straight away. Limited: the basket isn't saved, so leaving or reloading the shop empties it. Tests: `tests/unit/shopTypes.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/shopStyles.spec.ts` (all six looks, plus a Depot checkout).
