# Story S10: the seven story rivals

- **Data** (`src/data/story/rivals.json`, `rivalThemes.json`, `text/rivals.en.json`; 7 characters added to `characters.json`): Dara, Hale, Ines, Pip, Soren, Kael, Marisol (`rv_*` ids). Each has six Act teams (2,3,4,5,6,6 Pokémon, one Ace, a counter slot), six battle lines, a friendly and a hostile line, likes and dislikes, Secret Packs (Hale: three tone variants; Kael: three faith-breaking events), endings and four key scenes (beats 2, 4, 6, 8) as 28 story events (`ev_rv_<name>_b<N>`, three choices each, three variants per speaking role). Kael and Soren also have the six-clue fair-play ledgers (type, mandatory, cover, retro line, eye icon) and a three-clue compressed Secondary arc.
- **Roles** (`core/story2/storyRivals.ts`): `rolesForTheme` returns the design's theme table (all 10 themes) as `[Primary, Secondary, Friendly]` (`rivalListOrder`, rule R1); Champion Heir and Ex-Criminal may swap Primary and Secondary, never into an Avoid slot. Primary plays beats 2/4/6/8, Secondary 2/4/6 (my reading of "3 scenes"; the doc's "beats 1, 4, 7" do not match the key scene numbers), Friendly beat 2 plus three spars (Acts 2, 4, 6). Soren, Kael and Marisol can never be Friendly.
- **Teams**: `teamFor` builds a team from the Act bands (`balance.story.rivalBands`, Ace at the top of the band, others at the midpoint, Friendly minus 0.03). `counterFor`: Primary any, Secondary once, Friendly never. `pipDraw` draws Pip's extra slots on the named stream `rival_pip_draw` and re-draws a full team already used. Marisol's Bastion is Ace only when freed (else Dusclops leads and slot 6 is Gothorita).
- **Audit**: new gate `story-rival-audit` (`rivalAudit`) checks references, teams, ledger rules (5+ clues, 3+ Acts, 3+ mandatory, all five types, an innocent reading each), the compressed arc and the theme table. `revealAllowed`: 4 fired clues (3 compressed).
- **Safe**: the scene events carry only rival tags (`rv_story`, `rv_<name>`, `rv_beat<N>`), so the Director's slot fill (generic Stop tags) is unchanged. No save change (profile 20, run 40, settings 40).
- **Wired (follow-up PR)**: `core/story2/rivalPlan.ts`. A Story Run's stage-2 first rival is the Primary with its written Act 1 team (never weaker than the scripted +2 ace); the new record is tagged `Rival.storyRival` (optional field, no save bump) and named after the character. Returning story rivals fight the written team of the current Act (`storyRivalPlan`, then the usual adaptation pipeline), Pip's draws are kept in a run flag, Marisol's Bastion needs the `bastion_freed` flag. The first two sparked rivals become the Secondary and Friendly (list order, R1); extra sparks stay ordinary. Intro/rematch lines show as the rival note. Without a theme row the roles are dealt on stream `story_rival_roles`.
- **Limits**: the written key scenes (28 events) still wait for the Stop-event runner (S5/S19); the written counter slots are not used (the engine's own counter logic still applies); battle mid/win/lose lines are text only. Marisol has no clue ledger. Theme D's Marisol pack is my reading. Poses are placeholder sprites.
