- **Evil Mode slice 13, "a neighbour"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 15a sections 1 to 7, Part 30 section 3.9): Kanto's neighbour is Giovanni's Team Rocket. The player can send back a fruit basket, sit down with him, take his Front or draw a line, and take his chair (or lose it and work for him).

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/neighbour.json` (validated in `schema.ts` `NeighbourFileSchema`): the seven stances and what each means; the ladder; Giovanni's card (holdings per district, Dread 6, a Pen of twenty, then forty-three), his five ticks pinned to days, the fruit basket, his four rules, the first and second Sit-Downs (Ledger slot, the Front Card, "why are you doing this", the Pen's forty-three, his son), the thing he asks (three), the Chair's line, Proton's Betrayal, Mewtwo, what's left of Rocket (Giovanni leaves, Archer to Johto, Ariana joins, Proton never, Petrel, twenty-six Grunts), the Vault Card. All text from Part 15a. `"on": false` is the undo: no neighbour, nothing of theirs on the Board.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/neighbour.ts`: Rocket's holdings tagged on the Board when a district's laid (own generator `evil-turf`; no other roll moves). The ladder (a war job steps toward War; a quiet district steps back toward Wary, never past it). The clock: each tick fires at the Morning its day comes (or the first Morning after), whatever the player does, and is marked with its day, so never twice; a stopped tick never fires and the next is two days late; a war being won slows the next tick four days, once. Tick effects: a holding behind goes Shut; Dread +2 and the Pen to 43, and his invitation for tomorrow (refused: a step toward War); two more holdings in Saffron; a cold day; the fall to the Hero (the Betrayal on the Radio, the race to the cave if allied, the Boss's kept answer read to the Hero). Messages in his own format (basket, the thing he asks, his Pen, the Vault Card), answered once each. The Sit-Down (In person, once a district unless he called it): exchange 1 quotes a real Ledger Lean (or the warm opening), exchange 2 the Front Card, exchange 3 "why" (kept), then the table (line, person, a job not done, money, a fact, the promise, his Pen; three at most; a minor is refused by the slot). The Understanding only when every held local's known by name. A war job across a Truce or Front: the Card's twelfth field and the Ledger read "Broke our word.", stance Rivals, opinion down two. Front: Heat −1 a day to 2, a tenth every seventh day, the thing he asks on each district's first day (`evil-neighbour`, one draw). The Chair (from District 3, after a Sit-Down or a winning War, never a team marked minors): won, Rocket falls to the team (holdings the team's at Wary, Ariana joins if there's a bed, Grunts take free beds, Proton refused by `hire` on any path, the Pen opened at Clean hands or the team's to decide, the Scheme stamped four times not clean, the Hero's reason rewritten, the rest of the clock stopped); lost, a soft loss: the team's kept and works for him, one order a district, one more try next district. The Legend (Mewtwo, wild at A+3, catchable once a region); caught goes into the party (or box); the Vault Card: keep (Dread +3, Heat +4 floor 5, Kind −1) or open the ball (Regard +3, it leaves the party, locals one warmer).
- Battles (`fight.ts`): the Chair is Giovanni's own Gym team at A+2 with boss gear and the region's gimmicks; the cave is a wild battle so a ball can be thrown. `fightEnd.ts` puts a caught Legend in the party.
- Screens (New look only): the neighbour page (K, or "Rocket (K)" on the title row): stance and what it means, Allies/Rivals, "Yours N. Theirs M.", opinion, Dread, Pen, the five ticks, his messages, and the Sit-Down with its choices and the table; rows picked with Up/Down, Enter does it; a Sit-Down can't be closed, only got up from. The Morning shows his news and asks for answers; a holding of theirs says "Rocket's".

## Limits (by plan, or for later slices)
- One neighbour, Kanto's (by plan). No third team. Opinion doesn't travel yet (one region until slice 16).
- War jobs are ordinary jobs against Rocket's holdings; Part 13's six war jobs, Raids and their answers wait. "Turn them out" of Silph isn't built; tick 3 only puts their people in Saffron.
- The table's "line" is the whole Truce (no holding-by-holding redraw); "a person" hands over one person (no poached Grunt or mole yet). Truce's border takings and the "lying" Loyalty bump, Front's Belonging, War's Loyalty by talent aren't built. Their Executive breaking a Truce isn't built.
- The Pen: the team has no Pen of its own yet, so "Open your Pen" always takes the empty-Pen answer (he opens eleven). Reading three names (depth-3 scouting of his base) waits for the base slice.
- The thing he asks is a message (do it / say no) with a Ledger line, not a Board job with a full Card. Under him (Chair lost) the order is the same list.
- The Legend has no gimmick; the Hero's Legend at the Reckoning isn't shown. The Scheme is stored, not playable (slice 14). Petrel's three visits and the Café jobs are lines only.
