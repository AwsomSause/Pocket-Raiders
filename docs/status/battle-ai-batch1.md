- Battle/AI batch 1 (rundown §9, approved ideas; numbers in `balance.json` `typeRules` and `zBond`, defaults in `core/battle/typeRulesConfig.ts`; rules in `core/battle/typeRules.ts`):
  - **S169 Immunity Breakers:** 11 rare moves ignore one immunity (Thousand Arrows hits Flying, Plasma Fists/Bolt Strike hit Ground, Sacred Sword hits Ghost, Photon Geyser/Psystrike hit Dark, Moongeist Beam/Spectral Thief hit Normal, Dragon Ascent/Roar of Time hit Fairy, Gunk Shot hits Steel), with a battle line. The AI knows it.
  - **S156 Regional type charts:** Kanto uses its old rules (Ghost can't touch Psychic, Bug and Poison hit each other super effectively, Fire doesn't resist Ice); Johto's Steel resists Ghost and Dark. Applied through `battleContent(content, rules, regionId)`; the Map shows the rules top right (`region-type-rules`).
  - **S161 Status stacking:** a second burn or plain poison on a Pokémon that has it stacks (up to x3, +50% chip per stack; `status.stacks`, optional, no save bump). Toxic, sleep, paralysis and freeze don't stack.
  - **S162 Elemental reactions:** a hit marks the target with its type (`vol.element`); a hit of a reacting type sets off a reaction: Electro-charged (Water+Electric, 1/8 chip), Melt (Fire+Ice, 1/8), Wildfire (Fire+Grass, burn), Steam (Fire+Water, accuracy -1), Frostbite (Water+Ice, Speed -1), Bloom (Grass+Water, attacker heals 1/16), Overload (Electric+Steel, Defense -1).
  - **S170 Counter types:** sound moves are Songs, pulse moves Auras, binding moves and hazards Traps; Song beats Aura beats Trap beats Song for +20% against a foe whose last move lost. Shown in the move info text.
  - **S174 Z-Crystal Bonding:** a Pokémon needs bond 30 (trust + affection) before it can use a Z-Move; the menu says why. Enemy bosses are unchanged.
  - **S275 Elite Duelist:** new run modifier: every battle roll is the middle value (`Rng.steady`): no crits, no lucky misses or <50% side effects, damage roll 93%, moves under 50% accuracy miss. Catching stays random.
  - **S43 Pace Controls:** four Auto-Battle rules in Settings > Gameplay: Super-effective first, Heal under 30%, Save items, Never switch (`settings.autoRules`, settings v32 with migration).
  - Already built before this batch (no new work): S154 Move Mastery (`core/moveMastery.ts`), S266 Rival Learning (`rivals/adapt.ts`, `rivals/major.ts`), S270 Weather-Aware AI (AAI `fieldPlay`, `fieldDamage`), S277 Dynamic Team Scaling (`encounters.ts` `withPartyStrength`).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/typeRules.test.ts`, `tests/unit/battleAiBatch1.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/battleAiIdeas.spec.ts`. The story fingerprint test switches these rules off (no re-pin). Limited: reactions and counters are not shown as icons, only battle lines; regional rules exist for Kanto and Johto only.
