- **Beta 4.4 bug list, part 5a (Hyper move slot)**: When a player Pokémon has a Hyper moment in a battle, it gets a bonus move it does not know (the first of its strongest usable moves) in an extra slot, like the Z-move slot. The slot is marked with a star, has 5 PP and hits with +20 power (`balance.hyperMove`; `engine.ts resolveMove`; `battle/hyperMove.ts`). Once per Pokémon per battle, singles only. When the battle ends the slot is removed; after a win or catch (not raid practice) the player gets the normal learn-move choice to keep the move at its normal power or skip it. Move menu fits a 5th row; info panels show the boosted power. Tests: hyperMove (grant once, power bonus, removal and keep offer). Limits: doubles don't grant the slot; the bonus move is picked automatically, not chosen.
