# Beta 0.7: Galarian lines, Magmuk, Neocleon, Hoopno (Jet batch 3)

39 new Pokémon from Kice's `jet-pokedex-data.zip` (62 in the pack; the first 23 were already in) with art from `Galarian.zip`. Built by `node tools/jet/build-jet-species.ts` and `python3 tools/jet/make-jet-sprites.py` from `tools/jet/source/` (pack data refreshed there). Sprite sizes: `tools/build-sprite-scales.py`.

**Pokémon:** Galarian Grimer, Muk, Magmuk; Galarian Spearow, Fearow; Galarian Azurill, Marill, Azumarill; Galarian Trubbish, Garbodor; Galarian Lotad, Lombre, Ludicolo; Neocleon; Galarian Magnemite, Magneton, Magnezone; Galarian Tangela, Tangrowth; Galarian Chikorita, Bayleef, Meganium; Galarian Cyndaquil, Quilava, Typhlosion; Galarian Totodile, Croconaw, Feraligatr; Galarian Larvitar, Pupitar, Tyranitar; Galarian Onix, Steelix; Galarian Slugma, Magcargo; Galarian Snivy, Servine, Serperior; Hoopno.

**Evolutions:** level ups in the species data; Fire Stone (Muk to Magmuk, Lombre to Ludicolo), Leaf Stone (Magneton to Magnezone), Thunder Stone (Kecleon to Neocleon). `core/conditionEvolution.ts`: Galarian Azurill with high friendship, Galarian Tangela knowing Discharge, Galarian Onix holding a Metal Coat, Hypno holding a Muscle Band.

**New engine pieces:** 16 signature moves; move effects `healSelf` (Pitaya Burst) and `drowsy` (Ring Toss, falls asleep at the end of the next turn); 15 abilities (Basalt Shell, Scavenger, Parcel Guard, Landfill, Kitchen Heat incl. no Berries for burned foes, Neon Camouflage, Overgrown Magnet, Live Wire, Ripe Bloom, Thunderhead, Venom Jaws, Ashfall, Forge Body, Foam Shell, Royal Disdain, Hoop Focus). Volatile flags `parcelUsed`, `camoUsed`, `thunderheadUsed` and held state `berryLock` default in the schemas, so no save bump. Tests: `tests/unit/galarianPokemon.test.ts`.

**Limits:** spread moves (Paella Pan Toss, Pitaya Burst) hit one target; the game has one poison status, so "badly poisoned" is plain poison; Ripe Bloom heals only the holder (no ally yet); Thunderhead sets terrain after its first landed Electric move; Galarian Ludicolo's Paella Style sprite is kept in `tools/jet/source/galarian-ludicolo-paella.png` but not shown in battle; 10 lines (Larvitar, Pupitar, Tyranitar, Onix, Steelix, Slugma, Magcargo, Snivy, Servine, Serperior) have no shiny art, so shiny uses the normal art; Pokédex icons borrow the base species; the zip's custom icons are unused.
