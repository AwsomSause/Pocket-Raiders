# Story Overhaul S9b: Fork Gate, passing a town and Rematch Day

- **Fork Gate**: on a Story Run, stepping onto a gym node first opens a `circuit` decision (`core/campaign/circuitGate.ts`, shown by `DecisionScene` in a wider menu): the town's two Leaders (name, type, persona, ace, one hook line for authored Leaders) and "Pass this town". The walk does not happen until a door is chosen; the gym node is then chosen again. Entering a door sets `circuit.chosen`, swaps the stage's Leader and marks both Leaders met (the other may invite you to Rematch Day). Unanswered (any non-circuit choice, as the auto-play bots send): the first door.
- **Pass**: at most 2 per region. The town's gym is skipped, both Leaders are passed over and unmet, no badge; the stage moves on (`advanceAfterGym` in `run.ts`).
- **Six of eight**: after the last town, a region with fewer than 6 counted badges replays the first town without one (make-up fight; its choice and pass are cleared, so the Fork asks again). Make-up fights use the earlier town's stage number, so the Leader's team is the one that town always had.
- **Rematch Day** (after 6 badges, before the League or the next region): met, passed-over Leaders invite you; pick one Guest Match or "Move on". A Guest Match replays that Leader's town stage with them as the gym Leader; a win records a Guest Badge (not counted) and +1 Circuit Merit. Unmet passed-over Leaders write a journal letter. An auto-player's unanswered Rematch choice is "Move on".
- **No save bump**: the decision kind is new data inside run v40 (`pending`); old saves have none.
- **S9c**: a Guest Match is harder: the Leader's rotating slots (remixed team) and `balance.story.guestMatchBonus` (3) extra enemy levels. The Fork Gate and Rematch Day menus show each Leader's portrait.
- **Limits**: a Guest Match replays the whole town stage, not a single battle. Meet-the-Leader Stops, Closed Doors and the Charter Seal art are not wired.
- Tests: `tests/unit/circuitCast.test.ts` (fork, pass, make-up, Rematch Day, auto-player), `tests/e2e/circuitFork.spec.ts`; `tests/unit/storyDirector.test.ts` now answers the Fork and gives six badges before the region-end checks.
