- **Evil Mode slice 11, "the economy"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 12 sections 1 and 2, Part 13 sections 1 and 2, Part 5 sections 2.2 to 2.4): the player can run Rackets, Fronts and Works, each chosen on an Honest Card of thirteen fields; pay upkeep; and go bankrupt for three days (Short, Broke, Bust) without the run ending. They can run a café at a loss because the street likes it.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/economy.json` (validated in `schema.ts` `EconomyFileSchema`): fifteen hand-written Kanto Cards. Six rackets (the Dock at Vermilion, the Corner at Celadon, Protection, the Book, the Ball Monopoly at Cerulean and Fuchsia, the Tithe for Cause and Belonging), each with its honest version; five Fronts (Café, Day-Care, Charity, Garage, Allotments); four Works (Mend the Bridge at Cerulean, Light the Town, Feed the District, the Clinic). Every Card names somebody in field 3, and an empty "other way" fails the data check (so the build). The bankrupt Cards: One Last Job, Sell a Front, the Whip-Round. All numbers are the design's. `"on": false` is the undo: holdings pay flat takings.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/economy.ts`:
  - **Rackets** (Part 12 section 2): started at Dispatch on a street the team holds something on (a squad's day; whoever's line it crosses won't go, by name). Not on a Warm street, not at Clean hands, Belonging keeps only the Tithe, only Cause and Belonging keep a Tithe. Dread +1 and Heat +2 that day; a running racket is a floor under Heat. Patience by the street's mood (Easy 7, Wary 5, Sullen 3; Power's Protection +2), down one an Evening, faster with a Lean on the street or the Hero within three steps; at Dread 6+ it doesn't run down until somebody's had enough alone (one in ten, stream `evil-economy`). At two days the Board says "Sullen"; at none it's **Shut**: Heat +2, what would turn on it turns. Waive a day (Morning: no takings, a day's patience), end it (early: the street's warmer), or **make it honest** (the price less a tenth; pays less, for good, never turns; field 12 changes). Profit +10%, a Books leading +25%. Weekly moves (the Dock's Regard −1, Protection's Dread +1). A racket ends with its district.
  - **Fronts** (Part 13 section 1): open in a holding had by deed, lease or share (Paid or Persuaded), with someone of its talent, who then runs it and does nothing else. Pays clean every Evening, wherever the team is. The back room (open, shut; the Day-Care has none). At a district's end: run well (talent fits, Loyalty 5+) Regard +1 each, +2 at most; **Going Straight** counts a Front in profit with its back room shut for a whole district.
  - **Works** (Part 13 section 2): paid up front, built over their days, then Regard, Heat −2 for the first in a district, Loyalty +1 to Heart, Doubts and Kind, Given +1 (the Kindness Ledger from the other chair). Feed the District runs: ₽500 a day upkeep, Loyalty +1 for everyone at the start, Regard +1 a week. Once a district each.
  - **Upkeep**: an Evening line, from running Works now (rooms add theirs with the base).
  - **Bankrupt** (Part 5 section 2.4): an Evening that can't pay counts a day: 1 Short (paid late, nothing falls), 2 Broke (the lowest rank −1; One Last Job appears: ₽9,000, Heat +4, Dread +2, Taken 40, once), 3+ Bust (everyone −1 a day; sell a Front for half; the Whip-Round from Regard 4, once a region: a day's wages, Loyalty +1, Given by name). Paying in full again ends it. Nothing ends the run.
- Screens (New look only): the **Trade page** (T, or "Trade (T)" on the board): tabs Rackets, Fronts, Works, Running; the chosen row's Honest Card (thirteen fields, red where not known) with the buttons under field 13; the street or holding (P or "Next"); on Running, Waive (V), End (E), Make it honest (M), the back room (B), and the ways out when bankrupt. Evening lines for rackets, Fronts, upkeep, Shut, Sullen and the bankrupt day.
- Simulator: the autoplayer trades too (`policyTrade`); `npm run sim:evil` reports rackets, Fronts, Works, bankrupt Evenings and the lowest Funds. Table `docs/reports/evil-sim-kanto.md`; **read against Part 5's model in `docs/reports/evil-sim-economy-vs-part5.md`** (seven differences written down, none tuned).

## Limits
- Heists and Digs wait for slice 14 (by plan). One Last Job is one press with its Card, not a heist's plan and rooms.
- No inspection scene (once a region), no Front's person changing over a district/region/run, no Works "why" question from Tobi at Night (the Night is slice 7's), no Offer (Profit, Region 2). The Day-Care's no-Pen rule waits for the Pen (slice 10).
- The squad for a Card is picked for the player (the best fit, free, whose line it doesn't cross); there's no squad picker on the Trade page yet.
- Rackets 7 to 12 (Weighbridge, Pump, Rents, Tickets, Remedy, the Toll) and Fronts 5 to 12 aren't written for Kanto yet; the Cannery and the Mutual exist as the Dock's and the Book's honest versions.
- Funds still stop at ₽0; a short Evening counts a bankrupt day instead of going negative. Wages aren't halved in an Arrival.
- Rooms aren't sold (there are none yet); "Sell a room" is "Sell a Front" until the base lands.
