# Catalog Overhaul Pack: evil-hoenn-sinnoh-unova (row 9)

Source: `HOENN_TEAMS.md` (parts 0 to 5, 8), `TEAM_GALACTIC_SINNOH_FULL_1-3`, `TEAM_PLASMA_UNOVA_FULL_1-3`, and the Hoenn, Sinnoh and Unova parts of `EVIL_TEAMS_VOLUME_3_A-D`. Built on row 8's inside-route engine (`core/evil/inside.ts`, PR #1336). Save: no bump (story state lives in run flags; Team Sky's management is the optional `run.expansion.teamSky` block, absent in older runs). No ledger ids are owned by this category.

## What's built
- **Sinnoh, Team Galactic** (`evil-hoenn-sinnoh-unova-1/2/3`): the Offer (Saturn, after The Files; yes / yes and tell Looker / no), Acts 1 to 5 (Act 3 is the `ps_gx_lab` map), six Operations, Lights Out, Inspection, Seen, Next of Kin, the four officers alone, the loyalty test (Suspicion 7) and found out (10), the Final Choice at the Spear Pillar, four endings (`ps_gx_plan/turn/charon/own`), **Team Lantern** (founding, three Rounds, Three Hundred Lanterns), and twelve scenes for any player.
- **Unova, Team Plasma** (`-4/5/6`): N's Offer (for himself), Acts 1 to 5 (Act 3 is the Dreamyard map `ps_px_dreamyard`; Act 4 needs Rank 3 and night), the officers alone (Zinzolin, Colress with his sparring match, the Shadow Triad, Ghetsis), the loyalty test and found out, **the Coronation** with its four endings (`ps_px_asking/turn/ghetsis/own`; the third is warned and confirmed twice), **Team Open Hand** (founding, three Inspections, All Night), the Watch, Collections, the King's Room, the Table, six Operations, and U1 to U12 for any player.
- **Hoenn, Magma and Aqua** (`-7/8`): both recruiters on the road (hooked to The Files of whichever team the run drew). Taking the other uniform makes it the region's team. Acts 1 to 4 written once per team (`mg-*`, `aq-*`): Initiation, the Bunkmate (Ottoway or Marisca), the Errand, three missions, the Rival, Norman, the Counter-Offer (cross over one rank lower, double agent, report, no), the Weather Institute, the Squad, Mt. Pyre, the Leader Confides (40 Standing, Rank 6), the Bunkmate's Request. The Direct Question (Suspicion 6), Marked (9), handing in the uniform, six new Operations (Volume III C), and the Seafloor Cavern's four endings (`ps_mg_*`, `ps_aq_*`).
- **Team Sky** (`-9`): the Draconid at the fire, the Founding (emblem, creed, the first three), Acts S2 to S4 played in person (Mt. Pyre is `ps_sky_pyre`), the Zenith, the Sky's Choice and its four endings (`ps_sky_choice`).
- **Engine pieces**: route fields in `evilTeams.json` (`steps`, `max`, `test`, `out`: Hoenn ranks rise at Standing 3/8/15/25, max 60, the Direct Question at 6, Marked at 9); `routeOf()`; `evil` effect `side` (+ `demote`) moves the player to the region's other team (`crossInside`, its running Operations carried over one for one) and `leave` (hand in the uniform: the inside meters go and the outside story resumes). Standing condition max is 60.
- **Placement**: `placement/evil-hoenn-sinnoh-unova.json` for the scenes any player meets (Galactic S1 to S12, Plasma U1 to U12). The Acts, Operations and endings follow the team's beats, as row 8's do.
- **Team Sky, run by the player** (HOENN_TEAMS part 6, screens 7.9 to 7.13): `core/evil/teamSky.ts`, data `src/data/catalogOverhaul/sky.json`. Renown (story flag + missions) sets the grunt, squad and Admin caps; Funds (own chest); Heat with Magma and Aqua (decays a point a stage; at full the base is raided for a third of the chest); Loyalty per grunt. Each stage: 3 to 5 candidates (origin, trait, flaw, Skill, Loyalty, fee, their reason) and 3 to 6 missions (Rescue, Sabotage, Watch, Supply, Raid, Parley). Hire (with the one interview question), promote by the rank table (Fledgling, Flyer, Wingleader, Admin; Admins choose an ace from three and take standing orders), talk (the flaw decides which line works), dismiss, give a Pokémon from the Box and ask for it back, set the doctrine (three types or one of eight archetypes, with a live species preview and matchups; free the first time, then ₽2,000), build the six base rooms and the Workshop's gadgets, and dispatch squads Quiet, Bold or Kind with the success chance shown as a number and who moved it. Squads come back after their nodes with a report in the grunt's voice. Glory-Hound, Homesick, Greedy, Loud, Hot-Headed and Old Loyalties all act; a cracked feather shows a stage before a grunt leaves (or sells your routes). The roster's Loyalty 8+ count (`sky-loyal-line`) decides who stands in the line in Ending S-Three.
- **Team Sky screens** (`src/ui/skyMenu.ts`): a "Team Sky" button on the map menu once Sky is founded opens one glass list with six tabs (Hub, Roster, Hire, Dispatch, Doctrine, Base) and the sub-screens for a candidate, a grunt, a mission, types and archetypes. Every change goes through `GameSession.skyAct`.
- **Barks** (`core/evil/barks.ts`, `src/data/catalogOverhaul/barks/evil-hsu.json`): both FULL_3 part D tables. The squad (Greer and Lior, Yara and Emil) by Standing and Suspicion, the canteen and dormitory, grunts in the field and the Crew on the Vigil by the story's flags, and Cyrus to the Witness, N to the King's Friend and Ghetsis to the Seventh. Rest lines show in the map's notice line (about half the nodes); win lines after a won battle; faint lines when you send in the next Pokémon; heal lines at a heal stop. Hoenn's **Radio** (7.5) plays team chatter and tells of each Operation you refused, done by somebody else.
- **Team Hub** (7.1, `src/ui/insideMenu.ts`): a "Team" button on the map menu while you're inside any team: rank written out with chevrons and the leader's unfillable seventh place, the Standing bar, Suspicion as five eyes with its word (trusted, watched, doubted, marked), the running Operation as Orders; for Magma and Aqua also the crew (bunkmate's hearts, the leader locked until Act 4), pay, and Hand In Uniform with a confirm step (`GameSession.handInUniform`).
- **Hoenn effects made real**: the uniform pays each stage by rank (₽100 to ₽600, `pay` on the route); the double agent's fourth way, **Tip Them Off**, on every Operation; the wrong orb is noticed 40% of the time (Maxie or Archie swaps it back, Suspicion +3).
- Tests: `tests/unit/evilInsideHsu.test.ts`, `tests/unit/teamSkyBarks.test.ts`.

## Known limits
- Galactic's Lights Out whispers and some officers' answers that the docs leave unwritten are short fill-ins.
- Keepsakes are cut (Kice): the brass tag, the urn's tea, the Schedule, Three Names, the July Snowball and the like are run flags.
- World state, region weather (Desolate Land, Primordial Sea), the redrawn Hoenn map, Reputation by town, the team shop and the uniform's costs outside are said in the text and kept as flags; they don't change other systems yet.
- "Take it all" ends each team story as allied; Evil Mode is on hold, so its decisions are flags only (`evil-start-<team>`).
- Team Sky's management fill-ins (the docs give none): names, interview answers, talk lines, most report lines, Zinnia's window lines, the Admins' orders, the success formula's weights and the pay ladder. Gadgets help missions, not your own map (a glider shortens a mission instead of skipping one of your nodes). Grunt teams aren't fought anywhere yet (the doctrine sets the preview and the ace). The Radio can't be muted. The hub is a list, not the kite-and-bars drawing of the mock-up. The Final Choice screens' ring of faces and hold-to-confirm (7.7, 7.14) and the Rank-Up, Briefing, Result and Leader's Table screens (7.2 to 7.4, 7.6) aren't built: those moments play as events, with the numbers on the buttons. "Seen both teams' damage" reads as the region's story four beats in. "Two Rayquaza Signs" isn't read for "Call it for everyone" (the promise is).
- Quiet missions roll their shown notice chance (20 to 30%). Not built: the other team's Suspicion starting at 1 after the Offer (only the joined team keeps meters), and the "later runs" carry-overs (run memory stays within a run).
- The W6 King's Errand Shadow row isn't read (no condition for an unpurified lead).
- Cast stand-ins: Saturn, Jupiter, Charon, Padraig, Sister Ianthe and other new faces use NPC sprites; Plasma Sages share one Sage sprite.
- Built on #1336's branch, which isn't merged into ui-mockups yet.
