- **Evil Mode slice 3, "the Card and the Ledger"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) no job goes without its Honest Card. Before a job is queued the player reads thirteen fields: what it takes and from whom, what it gives, how the five numbers move, the odds, who goes, how it'll be written down and the other way. CONFIRM files the Card and writes its twelfth field into the Ledger. The Ledger (L, the Funds slot, or "Ledger" on the board) reads the run back as sentences.

## What's built
- Core `src/core/evilMode/card.ts`: `cardFor` assembles the thirteen fields from a job (Part 12 section 1.1 order). Red ink for every field that's a guess (an unscouted local); field 11 lists them; field 6 is `oddsText(oddsFor(...))`, the same figure the Evening rolls; field 12 is the Ledger's sentence; field 13 is "This is it." on a clean Card, or the other way (a Pay under the price offers the fair price, with a swap button). The gate: `openGate`, `gateKey`, `gateSeen`, `confirmLit`.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/ledger.ts`: the Log (append only; `appendLog` is the only writer), the grammar ("Day 3. Grunt 1, Grunt 2. Asked Mrs. Chiamaka Eze, 61, runs the ferry, Nugget Bridge, to pay ₽300 a day. They said yes."), Today's two pages, The run, By person, Given and Taken (counters, and sums over the Log). Waive (no takings from the next Evening; Given +1), Halve, Ask about (one exchange, once a district per line: tells what they want and scouts them fully).
- Day machine: `confirmCard` (same as `queueJob`: every queued job files its Card and a `card` Log line), `waive`, `halve`, `askAbout` at any beat. Replacing or clearing a queued job writes "Called off." under its Card; the Evening writes "It went." or "It didn't go." and a wages line (one to Given a head when paid in full).
- Data `src/data/evilMode/ledger.json`: field names, the Ledger's verbs by method, the Card's lines, Given/Taken counts, Ask-about lines, tab names.
- Screens `src/ui/redux/screens/evilLedger.ts` (New look only): the Card overlay takes the whole stage (the frame falls away), CONFIRM sits under field 13 in the flow, the edge is plain on a clean Card; the Ledger shares the frame with four tabs (Today, The run, By person, Given and Taken). After CONFIRM the filed sentence shows in the Ledger's corner. A kept Card opens read-only from The run.
- Keys: How → Enter opens the Card; Up/Down scroll; Enter confirms (a held Enter only scrolls; confirm wants a fresh press after a release); Esc back. L opens the Ledger; Left/Right tabs; Up/Down lines; Enter opens a line's Waive · Halve · Ask about (Left/Right, Enter) or a kept Card; Esc closes. Taps do the same.

## Saves
The block's own version goes 2 to 3 (no run/settings bump; the block is optional). `migrateEvilBlock` chains v1 → v2 → v3 and adds an empty Log, no Cards, Given and Taken at 0 and no rates; nothing is removed, and a job queued before slice 3 still rolls (without a Card line). Tested in `tests/unit/evilModeCard.test.ts`.

## Limits (slice 3, by plan or for later slices)
- Every Card is assembled from data; none is hand-written yet (by plan). Field 8 "Who won't" is always "Nobody." (named people, slice 4) and field 10 "The Hero" is "Nobody's on the road yet." (slice 5).
- Taken stays 0: nothing in this build takes (Lean, Take, rackets come later). Given counts wages paid in full and waivers.
- Names are always in the Ledger (Night knock 3, which can take them out, is slice 7). No Books' notes tab (needs the Books talent, slice 4). Lines aren't "crossed" or "kept" yet (people's lines, slice 4).
- A waiver lasts until the district ends; there is no "undo" line yet. No debug flag bypasses the Card (none was needed: the browser tests read and confirm it).
- Two field lists exist in the design (Part 12 and Part 29 section 6.1); this build uses Part 12's, which the Ledger and courtroom parts refer to.
