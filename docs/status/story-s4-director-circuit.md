# Story Overhaul S4: Orrery Circuit and the Narrative Director

- **Campaign** `orrery-circuit` (`balance.json` campaigns, `mode: "story"`): 3 story-only regions in a fixed order (Cairnmoor, Tidewrack Reaches, Orrery Basin; `regions.json`, `story: true`), 8 stages each, 12 columns, one League after region 3 (`leagueRegions: [2]`). The first two regions' last gym clears the region and goes straight on. Story regions are filtered out before the Classic region shuffle and from Classic pickers, so no Classic roll moves. Starting a run on this campaign makes it a Story Run (`run.story.v2`). Reachable today with `?campaign=orrery-circuit` or the testing campaign list (`countsForProgress: false`); the real Story Run entry is S2.
- **Evil override** (Appendix S.7 A, code-change option): `campaign.evil` `beatEvery 4`, `firstStage 2`, `hideoutAtLeague` (`core/campaign/circuit.ts`, read by `core/evil/teams.ts`). Beats 1 to 6 come due on stages 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22; after beat 6 `nextAt` waits (`HIDEOUT_AT_LEAGUE`) and League entry makes beat 7 due and puts the hideout event on the corridor's first step. Classic keeps `beatEvery 3`.
- **Director** (`core/campaign/storyDirector.ts`): the 44-Stop table (Acts 1 to 6 = 8, 8, 8, 7, 6, 4, Finale 2, Epilogue 1), `storyActOf`, a two-pass blueprint (pass 1 frame: theme, antagonist = the run's evil team, rivals; pass 2: Stop columns, side lanes, slot events) on its own `story_director` hash stream, `validateBlueprint` (placement rules 1 to 9, Act budgets, beat stages, intensity runs, slots, locks, checksum), `replanStops` (next 6 unlocked Stops only), and map placement. Stops sit on nodes (`MapNode.storyStops`, optional); a main Stop takes the whole column, a side Stop one lane; the evil beat's forced stop is moved onto its Stop's node (never a second stop). Region Gates play after the stage's gym, Finale and Epilogue after the Champion. Reaching a Stop plays it once: a map line, a chronicle moment, `v2.stops.done`. The map shows a ★ on nodes with an unplayed Stop and names the category on hover.
- **Knobs**: `balance.story.*` (stopsTotal, actEndStages, actBudgets, per-stage limits, column gap, rule 5/8/9 columns, jitter, replan window, intensity run, repeat rules).
- **Saves**: none bumped. Blueprint (`stops`, `checksum`) and `MapNode.storyStops` are optional fields inside run v40. A damaged or missing blueprint is rebuilt from the seed ("The story was re-told." in the journal).
- **Table changes from the design doc** so its own rules hold: stage 5 relationship Stop at column 4 (rule 5 only applies to stages with 2+ Stops; with 1 Stop per stage the old "first Stop at column 2 to 4" cannot hold), stage 14 midpoint reveal at column 7 (rule 8), Champion-stage intensities 3/4/3/5 (no more than 2 high in a row). Rule 7 counts a Region Gate as downtime (Acts 2 and 4 have no other rest Stop).
- **Limits (later slices)**: slots are filled by tag from the story events on main (with the repeat rules), but a reached Stop still plays its category line, not the event's scenes; running the slot's event is S5. Pacing rules that need content (named characters, downtime cadence, arcs, twists, returns) are left to S19. Six-of-eight badges, passing towns, the two-leader Gym Fork, authored leaders, Rematch Day and town names are S9. `cinematic` marks a request only; the Moment Director has the final say when scenes exist.
- Tests: `tests/unit/storyDirector.test.ts` (10,000 blueprints valid, same seed same blueprint, slot fill, replan, Acts, Story Run start and reload, re-told blueprint, Stop played on the map, League only after region 3, Classic untouched, evil schedule and hideout).
