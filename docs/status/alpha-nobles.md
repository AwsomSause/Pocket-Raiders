- **Alpha Nobles (S498, 2026-11-06):** 8 rare map events (`alpha-noble-<species>`, group "Alpha Nobles": Arcanine, Electrode, Kleavor, Lilligant, Avalugg, Wyrdeer, Ursaluna, Braviary; from stage 3 on, likelier with a party Pokémon of its type). A frenzied noble blocks the way. Data-driven: `tools/events/nobles.ts` + `tools/build-nobles.ts` (`npm run data:nobles`) append to `events.json`; no save change. Tests `tests/unit/alphaNobles.test.ts`. Limited: the "alpha" is a level bonus, not the larger sprite or extra stats of the random Alpha wild encounters (M78).
