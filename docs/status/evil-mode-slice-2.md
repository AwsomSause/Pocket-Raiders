- **Evil Mode slice 2, "a district"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) the day now has a Board. Kanto District 1 (Cerulean) has twenty steps and eight locals with wants, fears and five moods. At Dispatch you send squads of up to three from four unnamed Grunts to Scout, Persuade, Pay or Outplay. The Evening rolls each job once, pays takings and wages, and the district ends at eleven held or on day seven.

## What's built
- Core `src/core/evilMode/`: `board.ts` (lays the district from the run's seed with its own generator, so no gameplay stream moves; first moods; the Town's street; the count to eleven with the Town double and Outplay double under Power; fair prices; takings), `odds.ts` (one function, a base and named terms, clamp 5 to 95; the screen and the roll both call it; an unscouted mood shows as a band), `dispatch.ts` (who can go and why not, queue, Send fixes the odds, the Evening), `boardScreens.ts` (view models). `day.ts` adds `queueJob` and `clearJob` at Dispatch; `send` fixes odds; going into the Evening rolls.
- Stream `evil-dispatch`: roll n is made from the seed and n; the count is stored in `draws`, so a reload rolls the same Evening. One draw per job, in queue order, even for certain jobs.
- Data `src/data/evilMode/district.json`: the district, the layout (8 locals, 5 wild, 1 Alpha, 2 safehouses, 2 opportunities, the Town, the Gym), rules, four streets, five moods, the seven holding kinds and what can be done at each, method numbers, odds terms, voices, and the twenty written locals (Part 6 section 11). Ines (11) is a minor: Persuade and Scout only, "She's eleven."
- Screen `src/ui/redux/screens/evilBoard.ts` inside the day's frame, New look only: the road of twenty (rings: scouted, holds, would turn; mood dots; a pin when queued), the holding panel (blank ruled lines for what isn't known), Overnight and Evening sheets, the tally when the district ends. Dispatch: Where list with "Send them", Who, How (the seven methods always in the same order; Lean, Take, Expose shown and refused with why), Pay offers (100/75/50%), the odds line with every term, the queue with Clear.
- Keys: Board arrows move along the road. Dispatch: Where (arrows, Enter), Who (Up/Down, Enter toggles, "How" row), How (Left/Right method, Up/Down offer, Enter queues), Esc back a step. Taps do the same.

## Saves
The block's own version goes 1 to 2 (no run/settings bump; the block is optional). `migrateEvilBlock` turns a slice-1 block into v2 with nothing removed, four Grunts, an empty queue, and `district: null`; the session lays the district from the run's seed when the run is next opened. Tested: v1 block saved, loaded, migrated, laid the same as a fresh v2. A queue saved mid-Dispatch reloads and rolls the same Evening.

## Limits (slice 2, by plan or for later slices)
- No Hero: the seventh day is the only clock. Outplay is a roll, not a battle; there are no kits or conditions yet, so its odds are base, Alpha, rematch, scouted and squad size.
- Every job takes one day; Persuade's two-day option, Settle on wild ground and the four-person squad aren't built. All holdings are in reach (no safehouse reach yet).
- Favours (the teacher, the clerk, the doctor...) give nothing yet; wild ground gives no kits; opportunities and the Town can only be scouted; the Gym can't be worked (slice 8).
- Moods don't leak along a street, Shut doesn't spread, wants can't be got by schemes, and nothing turns a holding (no Hero). No talents, ranks or Loyalty per Grunt (slice 4); no Honest Card or Ledger (slice 3); wages are flat; when Funds run out wages are paid short and the line says so (no debts or bankruptcy yet).
- Results show all at once at the Evening, not one press each. After a district ends the tally shows and "Leave" saves; the next district comes with slice 9.
