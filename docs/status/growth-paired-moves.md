- Growth slice 4 (Bond Levels follow-up): paired moves. At Bond Level 2+, when a Pokémon faints and its partner comes in, the pair's damaging moves can form a paired move by type (Fire+Water Steam Burst, Fire+Grass Wildfire, Water+Electric Storm Surge, Water+Grass Rain Bloom, Ice+Fire Thaw Strike, Fighting+Psychic Mind and Body, Ghost+Dark Night Pact, Rock+Ground Earthbreaker, Electric+Steel Magnet Rush; `BOND_RULES.pairedMoves`, `pairedMoveName`). The hand-over then gains one more stage and the battle line names the paired move. No new engine rules and no save change. Tests in `tests/unit/bond.test.ts`. Limited: it is a hand-over bonus named after the pair, not a move you pick from the menu.
