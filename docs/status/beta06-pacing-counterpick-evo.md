# Beta 0.6 slice: stage pacing (B6), E4 counter-pick (B7), Evolution Encounters (B9)

Settings (all in `settings.expansion`, default on, read once when a run starts and stored in `run.expansion`, so a run never changes rules mid-way): `stagePacing`, `leagueCounterPick`, `evolutionEncounters`. Runs started without them (old saves, tests that call `createRun` with no options) play exactly as before.

- **B6** `generateStageMap(..., pacing)` in `src/core/map.ts`; numbers in `balance.json` `stage.pacing`. 4–5 trainer columns, 1–2 skippable (a wild node beside the trainer, `map.optionalTrainerColumns`), so every route meets at least 3 trainers. The last forced trainer column is the ace trainer (`node.ace`: +3 levels on its strongest, +1 on the rest). From region 2 (`doublesFromRegion: 1`) one forced trainer column is always a doubles battle (`node.doubles`). EXP x0.8 for the whole run. "One fewer empty node": the extra trainer columns replace wild columns (column count stays 20).
- **B7** `src/core/campaign/counterPick.ts` + `leagueDoor.ts`. Entering the first Elite Four node shows a League Door decision: your party is registered (strongest 6), the League names the Pokémon it will answer most, and you may swap that one for a boxed Pokémon (the swapped-in one is unseen). Each Elite Four member swaps at most 4 non-ace slots (`cap`) for Pokémon from that boss's own authored rosters that hit a registered Pokémon super effectively; the Champion answers only your top 2 by level. No RNG.
- **B9** New effect `evoEncounter` (`stone`: a party Pokémon that uses a stone finds it; `nudge`: a Pokémon within 8 levels of a plain level evolution evolves now) and condition `evoEncounters`. Three events in `src/data/events/evolution-encounters.json`: The Eevee Who Couldn't Choose, The Pokémon That Wouldn't Evolve, Pidgey's Resolve. Risky Evolutions are not built and stay off.

Follow-up (this PR): the three settings now have rows on the Settings screen (Gameplay: Busier stages, League counter-pick, Evolution Encounters; they apply to runs started after changing them). Wild pre-evolutions meeting their stone: `src/core/evoEncounter.ts`; a wild Pokémon that evolves by a plain stone stands beside it with `balance.evolutionEncounter.wildStoneChance` (15%, own hash, no RNG stream); win or catch it to take the stone (`battle.evoStone`). The dev-campaign pacing fallback test came with the build lead's fix.

Known limits: the doubles trainer needs two Pokémon able to fight, else it is a single battle; the League door only appears when the box has a Pokémon to swap in.
