# Story Overhaul S3: quiz and three-pick

- `src/data/story/quiz.json` (+ `text/en.json`): the 12 design-doc questions and 6 quick/alternate questions (5 answers each, seed tags). The 12 were **reweighted** (14 of the 60 weights nudged, found by a small search from the doc's weights) because the doc's weights failed its own balance rule (best-case score per trait ranged 11 to 16). Now every trait totals 18 +/- 1 over all answers and the best-case score per trait is within 10 percent (14 to 15). The 6 alternate questions are new writing for the topics the doc lists; they feed Quick Start / Short Intake / the straight-lining fix and are not part of the balance gate.
- `src/core/story2/quiz.ts`: `scoreQuiz` (clamp(100 * raw / 14, 5, 95), seed tags), `answerHash`, `straightLined` (8 of the same position), `quizEchoes` (top two traits at 65+), `pickThree` (Match, Complement with no shared primary trait, Surprise from ranks 4-12 seeded by the answer hash with a named `::quiz-surprise` stream; diversity penalties and the hard constraints), `quizStarterOverride` (species ids for createRun's existing `starterOverride`, the "quiz_three" route).
- Gate `story-quiz-balance` now checks the rule above (was 18 +/- 1 plus a per-question 14 +/- 1 that the doc's own scoring section does not support).
- `RemembranceSchema` gained `role`, `generation`, `branching`, `baby`, `quizEligible` (for the diversity rules).
- Tests (`tests/unit/storyQuiz.test.ts`): 100,000 fuzzed answer sets always give 3 distinct valid picks with the hard constraints; same answers same result; Match is best compat; small pools; gate passes on shipped questions.
- **Not in S3:** the 74 Remembrance lines (only a synthetic pool in tests; the real pool comes from the Remembrance data slice), the quiz screens/Vance lines/Retake button and Quick Start (S2/S16), and the `pool_remembrance` pool kind UI (Starter Sets are archived, so the Story Run feeds picks straight into `starterOverride`).
