# Battle screen fixes (Kice, 2026-11-16)

Four items from Kice's list, all in the battle screen. No rules, rolls or saves changed.

1. **Battle animations.** Cause: 480 moves played an EBDX "scripted" effect first, and 411 of the 420 scripts were auto-drafted by `tools/gen-ebdx-scripts.py` and never looked at. Many drew wrong: Flamethrower drew a whole flame sheet as one particle (a wall of fire), Hydro Pump, Surf, Earthquake and Psychic drew nothing but a tint. Fix: a script has a `draft` flag (`core/presentation/scriptAnim.ts`); `playableScript` gives only hand-checked scripts to the player (`battleFx.readyScript`), so a drafted move now plays its Essentials or PokeRogue animation (both checked by eye for the moves above). The 9 hand-ported scripts (tackle, ember, water-gun, thunderbolt, absorb, mega-drain, giga-drain, growl, comet-punch) still play. The generator writes `draft: true` on new drafts. **Status of the 411 drafts: disabled.** To bring one back: fix its timeline (most need a `cell` size for their sheet), look at it in the game, remove the flag.
2. **Sprite cut off (New look).** The canvas cuts your Pokémon at y 396 (`MESSAGE_Y`), but the mock-up text boxes started lower, so the cut showed. Layouts 0 (boxes) and 1 (tray): the text box and menu now reach up to the cut (`screens/battle.ts` `FRONT_EDGE`, `trayHeight`). Layouts 2 (top bar) and 3 (side menu) have no bar there: your Pokémon is no longer cut (`BattleScene.standClipped`). Old look and picked battle styles are unchanged.
3. **Battle text above the battle UI (New look).** A Trainer's spoken line is drawn on the canvas, under the HUD layer, so HUD pieces sat over it. The HUD now steps aside while a line is up (`BattleScene.speak`). The recap line after a skipped opening sat under the text box; in New it shows above it. (Full-screen Pokémon Moments were fixed by the UI thread in #1404.)
4. **Enemy Trainer on the versus card.** 559 of 2,114 event Trainer battles named nobody to draw (no class, no cast picture), so the card and the battle showed no Trainer. `core/presentation/foeArt.ts` `fallbackFoeArt` picks a stand-in from the Trainer's name: a character or Pokémon of that name, a Trainer class named in it, the run's Evil Team grunt for grunt/executive names, else an everyday Trainer chosen by the letters of the name (no gameplay RNG). Used by `BattleScene.trainerArt`, so Old and New both get it.

Tests: `foeArt.test.ts`, `scriptAnim.test.ts` (drafts never play), `reduxBattle.test.ts` (text box reaches the cut). Shots: `/mnt/project-files/shots/battle-fixes-nov16/`.

Known limits: stand-ins are generic (an event that wants a specific face should name a `sprite` or cast picture); Jessie and James and other missing cast art still fall to a stand-in. Layout 0's player box sits 25 px higher than before.
