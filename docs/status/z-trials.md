- **Z-Trials (S502, 2026-11-06):** 18 rare map events, one per type (`island-trial-<type>`, group "Z-Trials", from stage 3 on, likelier with a party Pokémon of that type). A trial captain offers a totem challenge: with a Z-Ring in the bag you fight the type's Totem (3 Pokémon) and win that type's Z-Crystal plus 6 research points; without one you can watch and learn (2 research points) or walk on. Data-driven: `tools/events/ztrials.ts` + `tools/build-z-trials.ts` (`npm run data:z-trials`) append to `events.json`; no save change. Tests `tests/unit/zTrials.test.ts`. Limited: the Totem is a normal 3-Pokémon trainer fight, not a multi-phase boss; the existing Z-Form trial stays separate.
