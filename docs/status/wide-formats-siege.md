# Wide formats: Siege Map (E4, 4 of 7)

- `src/core/formats/siege.ts`: Extended Cut Phase 0b, Kanto ("The Sealed City"). Each district is Held, Contested or Lost, worked out once at setup and kept in the state. Held: North Gate = rival in the Help variant (`cut.rivalRole === 'help'`, written by the climax x rival slice); East = Raid 3 Triumph or `rocket.x.wire`; South = Raid 4 Success or better or `rocket.x.surge-down`; West = Raid 5 Success or better. Lost: the raid was failed or declined (North: the rival beat you, `rivalRole === 'beat'`). Otherwise Contested (including a raid never reached). The Center is Contested by design: Sabrina holds it.
- The player picks a gate: Held = free entry; Contested or Lost = one battle (`launchFormatBattle`, a Rocket Cordon of 3; a lost fight heals the party and the player may try again). Entering writes `run.expansion.siege.{lost, contested, entered}` (existing `levers`/`flags` keys from the raid slice are kept) and checkpoints. Every Lost district's effect applies (the Breather reads `siege.lost`: West Gate heals 35%); the other effects (rival battle added to Phase 1, reinforcements sooner in Phase 4, Major Surge as a third duel) are for the Extended Cut director (E5) to read from `siege.lost`.
- Screen: `src/scenes/SiegeScene.ts`, Kice's pick Siege Map 7 "Rocket Ledger" (ledger rows with stamps, sticky notes for allies, footer). `MapScene` launches it through `formats/glue.ts` `sceneForFormat`.
- Also in this PR: `canStart` on formats (a region without content cannot start), `beginCut(run)` (the director calls it at the start of each climax so one-shot formats can run once per region), Convoy reads Raid 4 through `raidRecord`.
- Not built: the mock-up's "Retake left (Rival Assist)" counter (no rule for it in the pack data yet), ally sprites on the sticky notes, other regions' maps, the `siege-fall-order` roll (Stand With Them Contested districts falling during Phase 1).
- Tests: `tests/unit/formats.test.ts` (Siege block), `tests/e2e/wideFormats.spec.ts`.
