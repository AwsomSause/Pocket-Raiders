- Beta 0.5 Mentor / Protégé rework (doc §2, M3-M5), built on `core/story/mentorship.ts` (not replaced). Run save v42 adds `story.mentoring` (bond per mentorship, defeated foes, returns); migration `MIGRATIONS.run[41]`, tests in `tests/unit/mentorRework.test.ts`. Numbers in `src/data/mentorship.json`.
  - **Types and chemistry** (`core/story/mentorBond.ts`): ten mentorship types (Veteran, Rival, Protector, Specialist, Family Figure, Survivor, Former Enemy, Prodigy, Unlikely Pair, Shared Experience) worked out from Pokémon histories (a pair can have several); twelve chemistry traits; chemistry level 1-5 grows with each won battle together. Offers now use any type, not only veteran + boss win.
  - **Stat drift**: each win nudges the protégé's EVs toward the mentor's top two stats (skips stats the protégé is already naturally better at; capped at 60 per bond; uses the normal EV caps).
  - **Move inheritance**: Camp > Mentor bonds > Learn. Mentor teaches only moves it knows; power tiers need mentorship level 1-5 (signature-level power also needs 90 chemistry); moves outside the protégé's coverage need level 4; Z/Max/Mega/story signature moves never transfer; at most 2 per protégé.
  - **Events**: eight chemistry events (refuses to let it give up, imitation, disappointment, surprise, recognition, disagreement, protection, won't leave a mentor behind) as story moments, one per pair per 2 battles.
  - **Mentor's Last Resort** (`core/battle/lastResort.ts`): once per battle, singles, protégé at or below 50% HP, mentor alive in the party and chemistry 20+. Battle menu button "Mentor's help" lends the mentor's strongest known move in a temporary slot (♥, 3 PP, real move properties), removed at battle end.
  - **Graduation spar** (`core/story/graduation.ts`): when the protégé catches up (or evolves) the pair becomes ready (no longer takes a mentorship place); Camp > Mentor bonds > Graduation spar runs a simulated match from real stats (stream derived from the run seed, no HP/PP changes). Protégé wins: title, EVs, mentor's best move, equal ("respect") relationship. Mentor wins: +10 chemistry, retry after 3 battles. Mutual respect: chemistry 85+ and a close match, both get EVs by mentorship type.
  - **Returning protégés** (`core/story/returnProtege.ts`): knocked-out foes are kept (no second tracker, uses the knockout events). 3+ battles later, 35% (derived stream), cooldown 6, max 3 per run, never twice for a pair, rare Pokémon need a mentor with 3+ boss wins. Unskippable four-beat scene in `DecisionScene`; the mentor's bond decides acceptance. Joins as the mentor's protégé with Former Enemy type and remembered moves.
  - Limits: enemy trainers do not use mentors yet (the pure helpers `lastResortEligible`/`borrowedMove` are ready for the trainer-parity thread); signature-inspired techniques are not inherited; Last Resort is singles only; presentation is text beats plus a flash (cinematics framework later).
  - Follow-up (presentation): Last Resort also works in doubles (the commanded Pokémon is the protégé, any able teammate can be the mentor); the "Mentor ♥" battle button plays a cinematic moment (`mentorAssist`) with the new mentor stage (`ui/battleFx/mentorScene.ts`: mentor lends light to the protégé, heart burst) through `planMoment`/`playMoment`; the graduation spar plays `mentorGraduation`; a returning Pokémon plays the mandatory (unskippable) `protegeReturn` moment before its dialogue. New moment kinds live in `core/cinematic/moments.ts`, specs in `core/story/mentorMoments.ts`.
  - Art/audio and signatures (follow-up): the mentor scenes use the Deluxe Battle Kit pack art already in the repo (`pack.dbk.particle/pulse/burst`) and the game's own sounds (sparkle, hit_strong, ui_ability, pb_rel, jingles evo_success and pkmn_get). Signature-inspired inheritance: a Mega signature move never transfers, but a mentor whose species has one (`megaMoves.json`) can hand down an echo (the strongest weaker same-type, same-category move its species can learn) at mentorship level 5 and 90 chemistry, once per protégé (`signatureEchoes` in `core/story/mentorBond.ts`). New art/audio made for this is still open.
