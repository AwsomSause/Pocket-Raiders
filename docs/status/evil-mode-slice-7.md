- **Evil Mode slice 7, "the Night"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3): in an Evil Mode run (setting still off by default) the Night is now the Office. Somebody knocks at the end of the day; the Boss answers from three, or "(Not tonight.)"; their reply, then what moved, as one line. Then one action: hear someone out, or sleep. The Wall fills from the answers, and anything taken down leaves its gap.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/night.json`: the picker's numbers, the needs (live ones read the block; the rest name the slice they wait on), 76 echo flags (each says where it's read later), nine Wall and desk things, the screen's text, the four Wall states, Hear-out lines (all 28 Founders and Kanto people by name, seven by talent, seven Warm extras), and the first forty knocks from Part 24a, each with its title, ☼, who says it, its needs, whether it can be shut out, and three answers with their echoes.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/night.ts`: `drawKnock` (the eleven rules in order; rules 4 "asked across their line today" and 6 "passed over yesterday" send that person; rule 11 draws from the deck), `deckTonight` (no knock twice, nobody two Nights running unless sent, the quiet come first at double weight, the first Night only knock 1 or 2, the seventh day only ☼, Corruption 2+ takes one ☼ a district), `answerKnock` (one Log line per answer, every answer kept, echoes: a mood step, one person's Loyalty, Regard/Dread/Heat by one, a Wall thing, a flag, "won't knock this region", leaving with a handshake or the coat), "(Not tonight.)" (returns once, then gone; slept-through counted; their mood a step down if it was their own business), `nightAction` (Hear someone out with the topic and the five mood wrappers, or Sleep; one a Night, after the door), `takeDown` and `wallState`.
- Day machine (`day.ts`): the knock is drawn on the way into the Night (one `evil-people` draw every Night, even when nobody comes). Lights out with someone at the door is "(Not tonight.)"; the summons can't be skipped that way. `promote` records who was passed over.
- Screen `src/ui/redux/screens/evilNight.ts` (New look only), in the board's place at Night: the door (their picture, the knock's title, what they say, three answers and "(Not tonight.)"), their reply, then what moved, then they go; the desk (Hear someone out, Sleep, The Wall, Lights out); the Hear list (faces and a mood word); the Wall (pinned cards, a pale gap where something came down, its state). Keys: Up/Down, Enter, Esc back to the desk (Esc at the desk leaves, as on the Board). Taps do the same.
