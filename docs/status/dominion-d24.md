## What the player can do
- **Get caught.** A failed act whose stat is Stealth may end with the Boss grabbed (own stream `dominion-caught::<act count>`, chance 12% + 0.3 × Heat); a lost police fight or a lost raid always does; at Heat 60+ the police may pick the Boss up at dawn (`dominion-pickup::<day>`, 8% + 0.6 per point over 60, at most once in 5 days; Hide in the Warehouse stops it). A crew member arrested overnight is questioned in the morning.
- **The interview room** (mockup a8) takes over the screen, whatever page you were on: the officer (Officer Pike the rookie, Officer Jenny by the book, Officer Dalton the tough detective; picked by Heat), Looker behind the mirror, the Belief meter (0-100, FREE 60+, BAIL 30+, HELD below; last answer's red chunk), "What they know" (up to four papers from the run's evidence log; with three or more, one lies face down), the officer's line, "Your last answer" (consistent, fits the evidence, persuasive pips, Belief change) and the CONTRADICTS EVIDENCE tell with the red string to the paper.
- **Answer** five times: **1** Alibi, **2** Deny, **3** Blame Rocket, **4** Lawyer up (₽300, ends it, never better than bail), **5** Bribe (₽500 if taken; Jenny never takes one), **T** your own words (Enter sends, Esc closes the box), **Esc** stay silent. Contradicting a paper drops the meter hard (more if it was face down: it turns over); changing your story and repeating yourself cost too.
- **The release** (mockup a9): RELEASED / ON BAIL / HELD stamp, "What it cost" with the weight (×1-4 from how low the meter went; Held adds one), the crew and HQ rows, the cell line and the officer's parting words. **Enter** goes back to the HQ.
- **Always some consequences** (Kice): Morale for everyone and the site watched (Heat can't fall below 20 there for 7 days) even on a full release, and the Boss loses the rest of the day and the night (Held: one more day). Weight 2+: an informant in the crew (a statement and +3 Heat a night until they're gone), one Pokémon held as evidence (weight + 1 days; from the Boss's team after the lead, else the Reserve, else a member's spare), a raid (one HQ room taped off for weight days), ₽ seized, a Heat floor (5 + 10 × weight, 7 days). Weight 3+: Loyalty down for everyone. Weight 4: a member walks out. On bail: bail ₽400 × weight. A questioned member: one night in the cell, or two more when Held.

## How it works
- Data `src/data/dominion/police.json` (Zod `PoliceFileSchema` in `schema.ts`, D24 section): bands, start Belief, catch odds, the answers, judge weights, typed-word keywords, three officers (sheet + 15 lines each), evidence kinds and what each contradicts, release numbers, every line.
- Rules `src/core/dominion/police.ts`: the evidence log (`noteAct` after each act that raised Heat, stream `dominion-evidence::<rolls>`; statements from caught/arrested members, Racket ledgers weekly, the informant), `openQ`, the caught hook (`addCaughtHook` in field.ts, called on a failed act, a lost police fight and a lost raid), world steps (police: statements, pick-up; world: watched sites, Heat floor, raid tape), `answerQ` (stream `dominion-q::<n>::<turn>` for the noise and the bribe), `releaseQ` (`dominion-release::<n>`), `returnHeld` (session, every step). Models `policeScreens.ts`; view `src/ui/redux/screens/dominion/police.ts`; controller `src/scenes/dominion/policeController.ts`.
- **Typing:** T opens the shared typing box (`src/ui/typeBox.ts`, from D21; every key is its own while open, Enter sends, Esc cancels, limit `judge.maxText`).
- **Rotom:** a typed answer asks `askClaude` (role judge, use "Typed arguments", id `police:q<n>:t<turn>`) with the officer's sheet, the case (hidden papers marked) and the typed words wrapped as `<player_text>`. Rotom returns `{stance, contradicts, consistent, persuasive, reply}`; the rules check it (an evidence id not in the case is replaced by the rules' own reading; a bad shape falls back to the rules) and decide the Belief change with their own stream. Rotom's reply becomes the officer's line and the chip says "✦ Rotom · checked vs N known facts". Without a Key-Card nothing is sent and the rules judge (keywords for the stance, length and detail words for persuasiveness).
- The Boss in the room holds the clock (every other action is refused); a member's questioning waits on its screen without stopping the core day.
- Look: the round-4 Nova livery for these two pages only, in `police.css` (ported with `tools/dominion/port-mockup-css.py` from v4's "interval 2" and scoped under `.st.d24`), plus the v4 overlays and the dusk skyline (`paint.ts` dusk palette). D26 ports v4 for every screen and can drop `police.css`. Art: `t-off2`, `t-looker`, `g-tape`, `g-policekey` (PROVENANCE.md, D24).
