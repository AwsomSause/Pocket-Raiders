- Item sprites P6 (balls and Tera Jewels): **25 Nature Balls** (catch like a Great Ball, x1.2; the caught Pokémon has that nature), **6 Training Balls** (+5% in one stat for the caught Pokémon, like a vitamin) and **3 Tera Jewels** (Radiant = Tera Type becomes its first type, Master = its second type, Mystery = a random type). Balls are Bag items (`items.json`, library pictures) and each has a "x3" catalog pack so it can turn up in drafts. Code: `ball.nature` / `ball.stat` in `core/run.ts` (on catch), catalog effect `teraType` in `core/catalog.ts`. Built by `tools/drive/wire-balls.py` (ids in `tools/drive/ball-items.json`). Tests: `tests/unit/ballsP6.test.ts`. No save change.
- Not built, on purpose: **species Z-Crystals** (Pikanium Z and friends need species-specific Z-Move selection and their signature moves are still `supported: false`), and the **fan and "Fallen" Mega Stones** (no Mega forms exist for those Pokémon; disabled, not claimed). Stellar Tera: the Master Tera Jewel does not make Stellar. Balls use the library pictures for the Bag and throw; a custom throw animation from the trainer-animation work (P3) can replace them later.
