- Overhaul slice 20, environments and conditions: each trainer and wild battle gets a seeded environment (60%: 70 catalog entries, one ±10% type power each, with a banner) and rarely a battlefield condition (mid 15%, late 25%, boss 35%). Supported conditions: 14 power changes by move type and 6 that start a weather; they last their catalog turns and announce when they end. Total power change from environment + condition is capped at ±30% (`balance.overhaulField.powerCap`). Rolled by hash of the run seed, so no RNG stream moves; stored on `battle.overhaulField` (optional, no save bump). Code: `core/overhaul/field.ts`, hooks in `battle/engine.ts` (`applyTypeModifiers`, `startBattle`, end of turn) and `run.ts` (`assignField`). Limited: the other 34 conditions (accuracy, end-of-turn damage, speed, priority/boost blocks) and evil-team conditions are not rolled yet; weather conditions start with the battle, not at the end of the next turn; trainers' own boss/evil-team battle sites other than the main trainer and wild nodes (raids, totems, fishing, expeditions) get no field.
