- **Evil Mode slice 14, "the Scheme"** (2026-11-17, build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode/31_THE_BUILD_PLAN.md` section 3; design Part 22, Part 5 section 7, Part 12 section 3, Part 13 section 3, Part 26a section 2.4, Part 29 section 10.2, Part 30 section 3.6): the player can work toward the thing the team was founded for, part by part, clean or not, and see how on the Scheme page. Built on slices 11 (#1454) and 13 (#1455), merged together on this branch.

## What's built
- Data `src/data/evilMode/scheme.json` (validated by `SchemeFileSchema`): the **six Grand Schemes** (the Monopoly, the Law, the Chair, the Waking, the Home, the Fall), each with four parts (Means, Place, Key, Moment), a clean way, a dirty way and a dearer second source, every way with the text its Honest Card needs; the Creed's groundwork sources; the sentence that gets plainer as the number rises; the fainter clean drawing. **Kanto's own scheme, the Ledger** (four steps, each clean or not; the books supply a Profit or Cause Key or a Spite Means). **Eleven heists** (the twelfth, the Payroll, is slice 11's One Last Job) with the plan's slots, six room kinds, the thing's room and the paper's headline. **Twelve Digs**, with the notes (by an Eyes, a Hands or a Doubts) and dig tags read from a holding's name. `"on": false` is the undo: the page is hidden and nothing's stamped.
- Core `src/core/evilMode/scheme.ts`:
  - **The percentage is never stored**: four twenties for the parts in hand and the groundwork (to 20). Groundwork alone stops at 20; 100 needs all four.
  - **Pace**: with only Kanto written, the three-region order runs across its districts: the Means from District 1, the Place and Key from District 3, the Moment in District 6. (With three regions written, Region 1/2/3, as the design says.)
  - **A part's way** is taken from its Card (Dispatch; in person ones at the In person beat): money and days, a squad busy on it (Dispatch and trade see them as busy), then stamped at the Evening its last day ends. **A stamp is fixed when it's got and never edited.** Power's Means comes on its own the Evening the fourth Gym is dealt with (clean only if all four were Bent, Replaced or Befriended). Belonging's Key is claimed when the count reaches a thousand (it can't be lost).
  - **A part lost** (`losePart`) can only be found again by its second source, and is stamped by that way.
  - **Groundwork** by Creed, counted once per source at each Evening (honest rackets and Going Straight for Profit; every Persuade that held for Cause; every Gym dealt with for Power; Works for Belonging; Digs for everyone, doubled under Wonder; true things found for Spite). The Hero's Badge in a district where a Scheme job ran: −5. The Reckoning lost: −10, once a region.
  - **The Moment**: its day is set when District 6 begins (day 4 of it). On its morning, at 80% or more counting it, it's stamped (clean if waited for; not clean if caused: "Cause it" makes it come the next morning) and the team's ready. Under 80% it passes and nothing's stamped. **Nothing else happens yet: the Finales are slice 15.**
  - **Heists**: Grey or darker, once a district. Planned on the page (a way in, a way out, a watcher, an inside word), played at the In person beat: the way in rolled (or certain), four to six rooms drawn by the seed, each with its choices (the guard, the dog and the other crew can be **real battles**, fight kind `heist`; win in three turns or it's loud), a noise count of three, the watcher's warning, the thing's room, the way out (a crew member caught at one in two). The take, the Heat, the paper, danger pay, Loyalty +1 for a clean night, give it back at Night within three days (Heat −2). A heist for a part stamps it **not clean**.
  - **Digs**: on a held holding whose name carries the tag (a cave, a quarry, the tide, the Town), three days, a note each Evening, then what's found and the choice: **keep it open, or put it back** (Regard +1; anyone whose line is the land or a shrine, Loyalty +1). The Site asks if you're sure.
  - **Wonder's eye** (open, narrowed, shut) moves only by the listed acts, from Region 2.
  - **A fallen neighbour's Scheme** (slice 13) can be taken up: four parts already stamped not clean, its other way "Don't. It was never a good idea." The team's own is set aside, not dropped.
  - Stream `evil-scheme` (new; no existing roll moves).
- Screen (New look only): the **Scheme page** (C, or "Scheme (C)" on the board): the drawing in four pieces (dashed when empty, a stamp when in hand, struck through when lost), the clean version fainter behind it, groundwork ticks, the bar with its notch at 80 and the sentence, the Moment's day, Wonder's eye, Belonging's count with its names; rows for every way, the Ledger, heists, Digs, give-backs and taking up a fallen Scheme, with the chosen row's Honest Card on the right (or the Scheme's own Card, growing). A heist's rooms keep the page open until they're done.

## Limits
- The Moment completes and nothing happens: the Finales (and Kalos's switch, Olympia's check, a Legend in the Vault, the Lab at ten) are slice 15.
- Some clean ways are one job standing in for a longer road (marked `limited` in the data): the port's bid isn't a Pay slider; Qualify is eight Gyms dealt with, not eight in-person fights; the Signs' trials and the charter's form/hearing/signature are one in-person or three-day job.
- Heists: the Hero's room, the clock without a watcher, and the detective's file (Part 17) aren't in; the Vault and the Evidence Room are `limited`. Digs' Fossil, Cistern and Meteor give what's listed but not their imprint, heist slot or restless kits.
- No Raid yet (slice 10), so nothing loses a part in play except the function itself. The Profit thing (Balls, healing, ferries, power), the Cause sentence and the Spite Target aren't chosen at founding yet; the Wonder Legend is drawn from Kanto's three birds by the seed.
- The simulator doesn't work on the Scheme.
