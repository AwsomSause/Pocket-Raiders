Built by `node tools/jet/build-jet-species.ts` and `python3 tools/jet/make-jet-sprites.py` from `tools/jet/source/`. Evolutions: plain level ups are in the species data; Sableye + Dusk Stone = Maliceye; two Sableye in the party = Gemgoyle; Dartrix = Decidueye by day from 34 and Decidueye (Assassian) at night from 36 (the plain Dartrix to Decidueye step moved to `core/conditionEvolution.ts`, `removeEvolutions` in speciesFixes.json); Krookodile at 55 holding a Hard Stone = Dreadodile; Benovolant Beak holding a King's Rock = Tyrant King (else Fallen Liege at 33). Alolan Flygonite (reward draft, Mega Ring) gives Mega Alolan Flygon (`alolan-flygon-mega`). Battle: 10 of the 12 new abilities run (Dread Jaws, Storm Talons, Clinging Dust, Sovereign Plumage, Coral Veil, Eyespot Glare also on Mega Evolution, Dynamo Down, Gem Orbit, Tailwind Rider, Rune Sigil); 17 new moves (Quake Jaw hits Flying types and Levitate at neutral damage; Bastion Fist breaks Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil first; power rules `rainBoost`, `tailwindBoost`; Hypnospot has a Mega version). **Starter scouts**: `core/story2/starterScouts.ts`; five townsfolk wander Pocket Town (Opal gives Sableye, Reed Promised Prince, Dune Alolan Trapinch, Rune Paldean Golett, Coral Alolan Dratini). Where each wanders is a hash of the save's profile id, so it differs per save. Talking unlocks that one line in the Remembrance Archive (unlock `event` `scout_<name>`); 5 new Remembrance lines (84 in all) with signature moves and starter arcs. No save bump (the Archive record holds any line id; new battle fields have defaults, tested). Tests `tests/unit/betaPokemon.test.ts`; provenance `docs/quality/provenance/beta-4-4-pokemon.md`. Limits: Twin Maw (biting moves strike twice) and Silent Cloak (+1 priority first move, skips charge) are listed as pending abilities and do nothing yet; Thornstrum and Feedback Shred hit one foe; Dusk Volley has no Arrow-Shot charge-turn picture; Reef Tempest sets rain for 5 turns (the Damp Rock bonus is the engine's); Mega Hypnospot sleep chance is 30% via a Mega-only move version; Dartrix evolves after the next win, not with a Rare Candy; Pokédex texts, EV yields and heights are not in the game; no shiny art; icons and cries borrow the base Pokémon's; Alolan Flygonite uses Garchompite's icon; Rowlet (Decidueye Assassian) and Sandile (Dreadodile) were already starter lines, so they have no scout.
