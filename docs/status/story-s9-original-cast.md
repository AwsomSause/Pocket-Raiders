# Story Overhaul S9: original cast data and Circuit rules

- **Data** (`src/data/story/circuitCast.json`, Zod-validated at load in `core/campaign/circuitCast.ts`): the 24-town table, 18 original Gym Leaders (scene, choices, pre/mid/win/lose lines, likes, badge name and icon, persona, ace, sprite), Elite Four (Isadora, Kofi, Brigid, Aurelio), Champion Selene, Cornelius Ashdown, 6 teams, 5 team leaders, 10 lieutenants, 48 grunt lines, 6 Old Region overlays. Built once from the design doc by `tools/story/build-circuit-cast.mjs`.
- **Bosses**: the 18 leaders, 4 Elite Four and Selene are `story: true` bosses in `bosses.json` (ids `orig-<name>`) with generated teams in `bossTeams.json` (`tools/story/add-circuit-bosses.mjs`; no existing team changed). Story bosses are filtered out of the Classic role pool, `pickGymLeader`, `pickLeague` and Club battles, so no Classic roll moves. Sprites come from the Drive packs; five are nearest matches (Wick, Rhodri, Bettina use similar Drive figures).
- **Circuit roster** (`core/campaign/circuitCast.ts`): 8 towns per region, 2 leaders each. The 9 authored towns hold the 18 leaders; the 15 pool towns draw two different-type pool leaders (not rivals or the evil team leader) from hash stream `circuit_cast`; door order from `circuit_fork`. Stored in `run.story.v2.circuit` (inside run v40, no bump), rebuilt if a stored door no longer resolves.
- **Rules**: 6 counted badges leave a region, 18 (6 per region) open the League gate, max 2 passes per region, Charter Seal once per region, Rematch Day (met passed-over leaders invite, one Guest Match per region, others write letters), Guest Badges never count (+1 Merit).
- **Wired**: a Story Run's gym stage is run by the stage's first door; a gym win records the badge (`noteGymWin`); the Story Run League is the authored Elite Four plus Selene.
- **Limits**: no Fork Gate, pass button or Rematch Day screen yet (rules and state exist and are tested; the stage always takes the first door and the player fights every town, so 8 of 8 badges). Elite Four are authored defaults, not drawn from the role pool. Lark's ace "Tempestwing" in the doc is not a species; Altaria is used. Meet-the-Leader scenes, Ashdown and the team lieutenants are data only (their scenes are S5/S10/S19). Leader and Elite tokens/trophy icons are P7.
- Tests: `tests/unit/circuitCast.test.ts`.
