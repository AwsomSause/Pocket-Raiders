- Beta 0.5 M1/M2 foundation (trainer parity, part 1): every trainer battle now builds a record in `run.story.trainers` (optional, no save bump; older saves load without it) via `core/trainerWorld/trainerWorld.ts`. Trainers' Pokémon get friendship (balance `trainerWorld`; bosses and rivals higher, it grows per meeting), a bond between the two friendliest teammates (`PokemonInstance.bondWith`), mastered moves (`PokemonInstance.mastered`; a boss's ace starts with its strongest attack mastered, anyone else earns it by using a move `masteryUses` times across meetings), a specialization (offensive, defensive, status, weather, setup, trickRoom, balanced, improviser) and a wealth level. After each trainer battle the record keeps meetings, wins, losses, reputation (-100..100), ace, bond and a short note list. Own hash streams only; no gameplay RNG moved. Tests: `tests/unit/trainerWorld.test.ts`. Limits: mastered moves, bond and wealth do not change battles yet (part 2: engine effects, items, relationship moments); records are per run.
- Beta 0.5 M1/M2 part 2-3 (same PR): the data now matters in battle. A move in `mon.mastered` hits `balance.trainerWorld.masteryPower` (10%) harder, for trainers and for you (your Pokémon master a move after `masteryUses` uses of it in one run, max `maxMastered`). A trainer's bonded Pokémon can hold on at 1 HP through a fatal hit (Last Stand rule, friendship on the 0-100 bond scale, own once-per-battle cap, `spectacle.enemyStands`). A trainer's potions follow wealth (`battle.trainerWealth`: 0, 1, 1, 2 potions; 30-60% heal); battles without it keep the old fixed rule. A returning trainer's intro line is read from its record (`trainerHistoryLine`: beat you before, lost and trained, famous). Tests in `tests/unit/trainerWorld.test.ts`. Limits: wealth only changes potions (other items, held-item shop budgets and gimmicks per trainer come with the cinematics/Galar threads); specialization is stored and shown but does not steer the AI yet; reputation does not change levels; no teammate-protect or refuse-to-recall moments yet.
- Beta 0.5 M1 part 4 (builds on the mentor rework, branch `claude/project-thread-y0yoyk` / #1070): trainers use Mentor's Last Resort. A trainer's bonded pair counts as mentor and protégé when one is the higher level; once per battle, when the protégé on the field is at or below the mentor rework's HP share and the mentor can still fight, the mentor lends its strongest move in a temporary slot (`enemyLastResort` in `battle/lastResort.ts`, called from `trainerAiTurn`; `battle.enemyLastResort` marks it used; slots are removed when the battle ends). Singles only, trainer battles only (not raids, Totems or wild). No save bump. Tests in `tests/unit/trainerWorld.test.ts`. Limit: trainers have no chemistry levels, so the pair needs no minimum chemistry.
