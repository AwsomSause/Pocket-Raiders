- Beta 0.5 M1/M2 foundation (trainer parity, part 1): every trainer battle now builds a record in `run.story.trainers` (optional, no save bump; older saves load without it) via `core/trainerWorld/trainerWorld.ts`. Trainers' Pokémon get friendship (balance `trainerWorld`; bosses and rivals higher, it grows per meeting), a bond between the two friendliest teammates (`PokemonInstance.bondWith`), mastered moves (`PokemonInstance.mastered`; a boss's ace starts with its strongest attack mastered, anyone else earns it by using a move `masteryUses` times across meetings), a specialization (offensive, defensive, status, weather, setup, trickRoom, balanced, improviser) and a wealth level. After each trainer battle the record keeps meetings, wins, losses, reputation (-100..100), ace, bond and a short note list. Own hash streams only; no gameplay RNG moved. Tests: `tests/unit/trainerWorld.test.ts`. Limits: mastered moves, bond and wealth do not change battles yet (part 2: engine effects, items, relationship moments); records are per run.
