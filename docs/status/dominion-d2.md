- **DOMINION slice D2, "the Boss's hands"** (Evil Mode rework, 2026-11-19; build plan `/mnt/project-files/plans/evil-mode-rework/BUILD_SLICES.md`, design Part 1 §3, Part 3 §2, Part 4 §4, Part 9 A20, list `lists/player-actions.md` 1-150): from the HQ, tab 2 (or key 2) opens the Region Map (mockup 3). Pick a district, a place and an action; Enter shows the Card (mockup 4) with the odds, Heat, who's hurt, who might see and the chance of a fight; Do it spends a Day action (or the Night/Evening slot). Fights go through the game's own battle screen and come back to the map; a loss never ends the run.

## What's built
- Data: `src/data/dominion/actions.json` (all 150 actions, built from the list by `tools/dominion/build-actions.py`; run `python3 -I tools/dominion/build-actions.py <path to player-actions.md>`), `src/data/dominion/field.json` (Kanto's ten districts at the mockup's positions, three places each with owner and trait, routes and water, how each played action works, odds, Edge, mastery, district control, police ladder, travel, vehicles, every line of text). Shapes in `schema.ts`.
- Core `src/core/dominion/`: `field.ts` (the Card, travel, the act roll on stream `dominion-act::<n>`, Edge stats + Infamy XP, mastery Rookie..Legend, district Heat and status, rackets, Wanted = average Heat of districts with Heat; police World Turn step on stream `dominion-police::<day>`: patrol at Noticed, checkpoint at Wanted, Looker weekly at Hunted with a racket, raid at Most Wanted; the night world step cools 3 per district and pays rackets on bill day), `fight.ts` (builds the foe: wild by district theme on a derived `dominion-foe` stream, owner, police, Looker, raid, ghost, spar), `fightEnd.ts` (result back into the block; run.ts hands the battle end to it), `fieldScreens.ts` (map and Card view models), `migrate.ts` (block v1 to v2).
- 32 actions are played: eight of the starting nine (Extort, Take, Steal, Thieve, Attack, Scout, Catch, Lay Low; the ninth, Recruit, shows locked until D3) and 24 more (Collect a Debt, Parking Fees, Shake the Jar, Train Your Pokémon, Plan the Week, Smash and Grab, Loot the Dock, Street Duel, Ambush a Patrol, Tail a Target, Count the Guards, Listen at the Pokémon Center, Read the Newspaper, Change Clothes, Pay the Fine, Spar, Workout, Meditate, Practice Smooth Talk, Practice Sneaking, Pamper the Team, Graveyard Shift, Cave Run, Hide in the Warehouse). Rest stays D1's (HQ Night panel).
- Night actions on the map (one per night, travel one hop); Evening actions once each per evening.
- Vehicles (A20): key V on the map; Bike ₽1,000, Van, Boat, Truck, Airship (HQ level 5). Fly needs a Pokémon with Fly and HQ level 3.
- Screens: `src/ui/redux/screens/dominion/fieldViews.ts` (map and Card), `paint.ts` (day skyline), DominionScene screens `map` and `card`. Keys: arrows move between districts / the 3x3 grid, T next place, [ ] action pages, Enter Card, B bring crew, Esc back, V vehicles, digits for tabs. Art added to `public/assets/images/dominion/` (PROVENANCE.md, D2 section).
