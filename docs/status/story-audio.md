# Story Overhaul S17: audio

- Built: `src/data/audio/story_music.json` (npm run data:story-audio) lists all 105 story music pieces and stems (48 leitmotifs, 18 team themes, 9 region tracks, 14 battle themes, 12 stingers, opening montage, 3 quiz stems) and 34 story sound effects from Design v2 Part AD. `src/core/storyAudio.ts` gives the schema, `storyAssetId` (real file once made, else the reuse sound), bar-line cross-fade planning (2 s) and the audio budget gate `audioBudgetProblems`. Tests: `tests/unit/storyAudio.test.ts`. No save change.
- Limits (status: limited): nothing is composed. Every id points at an existing catalog sound (hub, event, map, battle and raid tracks, me/ui sounds), so the same music repeats across characters. Nothing in the game plays these ids yet; story slices call `storyAssetId`. The 10 new cries reuse the base species' cries (S15). Vocal barks and the other ~26 sound effects in the plan are not listed. Real tracks need composer and licence in the data (the gate requires them with loop points).
