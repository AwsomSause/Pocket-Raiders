Pocket Raiders · Evil Mode (Dominion) · Round 4 · interval 5 of 5

Dominion

Round 4 of the Evil Mode mockups: 25 of 25 passes done. Every pass asked five questions: is it more extravagant, how is it better than last time, does an action need its own page, how do a Menu and Settings fit, and does it feel like an evil Pokémon game? The answers are in Reviews below. New pages come first, then all 27 screens. Mock data only; the game rules are unchanged.

Build notes: all styles sit in the one style block so tools/dominion/port-mockup-css.py can port them. Every animation stops under reduced motion. The battle screen is not part of this set and gets no new styles.

Round 4 · the five questions, asked every pass

Interval 1 · passes 1–5 · New pages

Big change: Four new pages: a Boss Menu, Settings, an Assign-a-task page and a District file. Menu and Settings did not exist before; the other two pull clutter out of Morning and the Map.

Pass 1 · Boss Menu
More extravagant?
Slanted gold blades, the Boss lit like a poster, live empire summary.
Better than last time?
There was no menu at all; now every screen has one place to pause, save and check the run.
More pages?
Yes: Save, Settings and Rotom leave the main screens and live here.
Menu and Settings?
This is the Menu. It opens on Esc from every Dominion screen.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: the Boss stands over his crew list with a crest and a criminal's quote.
Pass 2 · Settings · Dominion
More extravagant?
Segmented gold switches, live preview tiles for each effects level.
Better than last time?
Rules that were only in docs (run length, pace, betrayals) are now player choices.
More pages?
Yes: settings get their own page with tabs instead of hiding in Rotom's screen.
Menu and Settings?
Settings opens from the Boss Menu, with a Dominion tab and an Effects & motion tab.
Evil Pokémon game?
Partly: "Day pace: Ruthless" and "Betrayals" make the player think like a crime boss.
Pass 3 · Assign a task
More extravagant?
Big task cards with huge odds numbers and a Best fit ribbon with an orbiting spark.
Better than last time?
Morning had six tiny rows; now one member and six readable cards fill the screen.
More pages?
Yes: assigning moved out of the Morning screen.
Menu and Settings?
Esc still opens the Menu; the tabs stay at the bottom.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: the jobs are extortion, theft and recruiting, all with Heat shown up front.
Pass 4 · District file
More extravagant?
Three dials, target cards with portraits, a red Hero warning banner.
Better than last time?
The map no longer carries a cramped side panel; actions get a full page.
More pages?
Yes: the district and its nine actions are now their own page.
Menu and Settings?
Esc goes back to the map, then to the Menu.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: Money, Muscle and Shadow read like a crime syndicate's playbook.
Pass 5 · Wiring the new pages
More extravagant?
Each new page keeps the round-3 frame, haze and glow, so it sits with the old screens.
Better than last time?
The set went from 27 single screens to a connected game with a menu, settings and drill-down pages.
More pages?
Checked every screen: Morning and the Map were the cluttered ones; both now hand off to their own pages.
Menu and Settings?
Esc opens the Boss Menu from every screen (noted under each screen); Settings sit inside the Menu.
Evil Pokémon game?
The Menu, the task list and the district page all speak as the villain.
Interval 2 · passes 6–10 · The Nova livery

Big change: Every screen, old and new, now wears one villain look instead of the friendly blue-and-gold: black lacquer, crimson seams, the Nova eye and sigil, and a red lock-on ring on the thing you are choosing.

Pass 6 · Lacquer and crimson
More extravagant?
Top bar, tabs and panel edges turn black lacquer and violet with crimson seams, and every panel gets a thin crimson inner line; the open tab glows red.
Better than last time?
Round 3 was gold on friendly blue, which read like a hero's menu. Now the colours say "criminal organisation" at a glance.
More pages?
No new page; this pass is about the look every page shares.
Menu and Settings?
The Boss Menu and Settings take the same livery, so they feel part of the game.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: red and black is the classic villain team palette.
Pass 7 · The Nova eye
More extravagant?
The plain white diamond on every title becomes a gold-and-crimson slit eye that blinks now and then.
Better than last time?
Every screen title now has the team's mark instead of a generic shape.
More pages?
No; titles stay one line so pages stay calm.
Menu and Settings?
The Menu title gets the same eye, so it reads as the Boss's own menu.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: the team is always watching.
Pass 8 · Blood-gold buttons
More extravagant?
Main buttons get a crimson ring, a pink rim and two fang marks; chosen options get a crimson ring too.
Better than last time?
Buttons were plain yellow; now they look like a gang's seal.
More pages?
No; the button count is unchanged.
Menu and Settings?
Settings switches stay gold so they read as safe; only actions turn blood-gold.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: every "do it" button feels like committing a crime.
Pass 9 · Villain light and the sigil
More extravagant?
A crimson underlight rises from the floor, a red-and-violet siren seam runs along the top edge, and the Nova sigil (a hex crest with an eye) slowly turns behind the UI.
Better than last time?
Round 3 lit each screen in its own colour; now every screen also shares one sinister light.
More pages?
No; the sigil sits behind open space, never behind text.
Menu and Settings?
The Menu's darkened HQ gets the same underlight, so pausing still feels like the hideout.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: it looks lit by an alarm, not by daylight.
Pass 10 · Lock-on
More extravagant?
Whatever you are choosing gets a red ring that tightens like a target lock.
Better than last time?
Selection was only a gold fill; now it also says "target".
More pages?
Checked: the ring sits outside each card, so nothing gets more crowded.
Menu and Settings?
Menu blades and Settings categories lock on the same way, so the cursor feels the same everywhere.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: choosing a task or district feels like picking a victim.
Interval 3 · passes 11–15 · Getting around

Big change: The screens now work as one connected game: a map of how every page links, a hold-Q command wheel that jumps anywhere, "you are here" breadcrumbs, one shared Settings list, and a crimson shutter that plays between pages.

Pass 11 · The Boss's board
More extravagant?
A cork planning board with pinned photos and red string, which is how a crime boss plans.
Better than last time?
Before, nothing showed how the 31 screens connect; now one page shows every route.
More pages?
Yes: one new page, and it doubles as in-game help.
Menu and Settings?
The Menu is pinned at the top centre with gold string down to every main page; Settings hangs off it.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: red string on a cork board is the classic villain's plan.
Pass 12 · Command wheel
More extravagant?
A glowing ring of eight places around the Nova eye, with a crimson wedge on the one you point at.
Better than last time?
Jumping between pages took Esc, then the menu, then a choice; now it is one hold and flick.
More pages?
It removes clicks instead of adding pages; last and pinned pages sit on its sides.
Menu and Settings?
Boss Menu is one of the eight spots, so Settings is two steps away from anywhere.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: the Boss's orders come from the centre of the wheel.
Pass 13 · Breadcrumbs
More extravagant?
"You are here" chips with a crimson arrow.
Better than last time?
Drill-down pages could get lost; now each one says where you came from.
More pages?
Checked: the breadcrumb sits in the top bar or key strip, so no page got more crowded.
Menu and Settings?
Esc always goes one step back; from the top level it opens the Menu.
Evil Pokémon game?
Neutral; this pass is about clarity.
Pass 14 · One Settings
More extravagant?
The Rotom tab and the Dominion tab now share one category list.
Better than last time?
There were two different Settings lists; now there is one.
More pages?
No new page: two half-pages became one page.
Menu and Settings?
Settings opens from the Menu with the same nine tabs every time.
Evil Pokémon game?
Partly: Dominion rules sit at the top of the list.
Pass 15 · The crimson shutter
More extravagant?
A crimson blade with the Nova eye wipes across between pages, with the new page's name flashing on it.
Better than last time?
Pages used to just swap; now every change has a short villain sting.
More pages?
No; it is a moment, not a page. Lighter turns it into a fade and Calm turns it off.
Menu and Settings?
The Menu opens with the same shutter, so pausing feels part of the show.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: every page change looks like a team takeover.
Interval 4 · passes 16–20 · Action moments

Big change: An action is now a little show instead of a log line: the Boss gives the order in a cut-in, then the result gets its own page (Job done, Job botched, or the Hero showing up), each with clear numbers and a choice.

Pass 16 · Boss order cut-in
More extravagant?
Two slashing portrait bands, speed lines, and the order stamped in huge gold letters.
Better than last time?
Pressing Do it used to cut straight to a number; now the Boss visibly gives the order.
More pages?
It is a short moment, not a page; Enter skips it.
Menu and Settings?
The Cut-ins switch in Settings, Effects & motion, turns it off.
Evil Pokémon game?
Very: the Boss orders a crime and his grunt says "Consider it done".
Pass 17 · Job done
More extravagant?
A slamming PAID IN FULL stamp, gold rays, coins spilling and a huge count-up.
Better than last time?
Results used to be a row in the Morning report; now every change shows its before and after.
More pages?
Yes: results got their own page so the map stays clean.
Menu and Settings?
Esc from here goes back to the map like any other page.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: you just shook down a shop owner, and he is cowering in the corner.
Pass 18 · Job botched
More extravagant?
Red and blue police strobes, rolling police tape and a BOTCHED stamp.
Better than last time?
Failures were the same grey line as wins; now failing feels dangerous and forces a choice.
More pages?
Yes: the "what to do about Rosa" choice lives here, not on the map.
Menu and Settings?
Settings "Flashes: Soft" makes the strobes gentle; "Off" stops them.
Evil Pokémon game?
Very: bail, a jailbreak, or leaving her to the police.
Pass 19 · The Hero shows up
More extravagant?
The screen cracks like glass, splits into crimson and blue, and a giant VS pulses in the middle.
Better than last time?
The Hero used to be a warning banner; now he can actually walk in on a job.
More pages?
Yes: an interrupt page, so the Card does not need a "what if the Hero comes" footnote.
Menu and Settings?
The Hero's Threat number matches the one on the Boss Menu.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: in an evil game the hero is the boss fight.
Pass 20 · Wiring the moments
More extravagant?
Each moment shares the cut-in look, so a run feels like a TV crime show.
Better than last time?
The Card (screen 4) now leads somewhere: order, then result. Botched leads into police questioning (Rotom screen 8).
More pages?
Checked every action path: each ends on one result page with one main button.
Menu and Settings?
Every moment page skips with Enter and obeys the Effects & motion settings.
Evil Pokémon game?
Yes: crimes have consequences you can see.
Interval 5 · passes 21–25 · Villain flavour and polishlatest

Big change: The game now talks and acts like a villain: the morning paper reports your crimes, the Boss has a throne and a cat, menus say "Back to work" and "Go into hiding", grunts talk back, and taking a town ends with the Boss hijacking every TV.

Better than last time?
Results were numbers; now the world reacts to your crimes in its own words.
More pages?
Yes: news gets its own page, so the Morning report keeps only your crew's news.
Menu and Settings?
Esc folds the paper; it sits in the board between Title and Morning.
Evil Pokémon game?
Very: you read about yourself as the bad guy and your bounty goes up.
Pass 22 · The throne

More pages?
No new page.

Evil Pokémon game?

Evil Pokémon game?

Pass 24 · Audit
More extravagant?
Checked every page for clashes and fixed what turned up.
Better than last time?
Found and fixed: Quit was on Q, which is now the wheel (moved to End); the Crew File paged with Q and E (now the arrow keys); the paper now has the same name as the one on the Evening screen; long button text wrapped (shortened); the board's note covered a photo (moved).

Evil Pokémon game?
