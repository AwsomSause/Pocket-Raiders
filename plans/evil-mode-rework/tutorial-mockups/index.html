Pocket Raiders · Evil Mode (Dominion) · Tutorial and opening

Dominion · the first week

Mockups for a Dominion tutorial and opening, one page per screen or interaction. It runs from the title, through a short cold open and the built setup, to the first evening, the first week and the Founding. Rotom (the Boss's phone) teaches fifteen short lessons, one at a time, each pointing at one thing. Same look as round 4 (Nova livery, New UI).

Rules kept: text stays until a key is pressed, and every reward card appears only after its text is closed (marked ✓). Canon characters only (Red as the Boss, Giovanni, Officer Jenny); Rook is the design's default Grunt and is male. The helper is always called Rotom; tutorial lines are written ahead of time, so they cost nothing. No move animations are touched. Build note: a "Tutorial" row (Full, Tips only, Off) goes in Settings, Dominion tab, which means a settings save bump with a migration test.
