# New animations

Moments in the main game that have no animation of their own today: a line of text, a number, a hard cut, or nothing. 94 entries. Evil Mode and Dominion are left out.

Each entry says what happens today, what the new animation is, and where to watch it in the mockups. 66 entries have a mockup clip; 28 are described only. None of these changes a battle result: they only show what the game already decided. The one exception is marked with a note (Raid Barrier), because it would need a new rule.

## Battle basics

### 1. Idle Styles
- **Today:** Every Pokémon plays its sprite loop and nothing else; they all stand the same way.
- **New animation:** Six ways of standing, chosen by body type and layered on top of the sprite loop: fliers hover over a shrinking shadow, ghosts drift and flicker, heavy ones breathe slow and deep, electric ones twitch and hop, long ones sway, the rest stand steady.
- **See it:** pass 11, clip "Idle Styles"

### 2. Light and Heavy
- **Today:** Every Pokémon lands the same way.
- **New animation:** Weight is a character trait. A small Pokémon lands, bounces twice and squeaks out a little cry. A heavy one comes down like a dropped piano: the screen jolts, dust goes both ways, the HP plates rattle and the Pokémon across the field is bounced off its feet.
- **See it:** pass 11, clip "Light and Heavy"

### 3. Worn Out
- **Today:** The HP bar turns red and pulses. The Pokémon stands exactly as before.
- **New animation:** Below a fifth of its HP the Pokémon itself shows it: it slumps, breathes fast and shallow, and sweat flicks off. Heal it and it straightens up with a shake and goes back to its normal stance.
- **See it:** pass 11, clip "Worn Out"

### 4. Toughed It Out
- **Today:** A text line says it held on; the HP bar stops at 1.
- **New animation:** The bar drains to a single point and catches there with a spark. The Pokémon skids back, drops to one knee, shakes, then plants its feet and glows for a heartbeat. A HUNG ON stamp lands and a heart floats up toward you.
- **See it:** pass 11, improved up to pass 19, clip "Toughed It Out"

### 5. Flinch
- **Today:** A text line: "flinched and couldn't move".
- **New animation:** The Pokémon starts its lunge, jerks to a stop, pulls its head down and backs up two steps with a sweat drop. The move never comes out, and you can see why.
- **See it:** pass 11, clip "Flinch"

### 6. Waiting on You
- **Today:** Nothing happens while you think.
- **New animation:** Take more than a few seconds over your move and your Pokémon notices: it glances round with a "?", bounces on its feet twice, and settles again. It never repeats within a turn and never blocks input.
- **See it:** pass 11, clip "Waiting on You"

### 7. Forced out (Roar, Whirlwind)
- **Today:** No motion of its own was found in the survey.
- **New animation:** The target is dragged backwards into its ball. The next Pokémon is thrown out, not sent.
- **See it:** no mockup yet, described only

### 8. Hit and run (U-turn, Volt Switch)
- **Today:** No motion of its own was found in the survey.
- **New animation:** The user's dash ends inside its own ball, so there is no separate recall.
- **See it:** no mockup yet, described only

### 9. Form change in battle
- **Today:** No motion of its own was found in the survey.
- **New animation:** One beat: a snap, a ring in the type colour, and the plate updates. Used for a broken disguise and other mid-battle form changes.
- **See it:** no mockup yet, described only

### 10. Transform
- **Today:** No motion of its own was found in the survey.
- **New animation:** The user's outline wipes across into the shape of the target.
- **See it:** no mockup yet, described only

### 11. Running away
- **Today:** No motion of its own was found in the survey.
- **New animation:** The Pokémon is recalled (pass 1 Recall), the trainer turns, and the battle slides away with dust. A failed run is a stumble and a shake of the head.
- **See it:** no mockup yet, described only

### 12. Battle menu buttons
- **Today:** No motion of its own was found in the survey.
- **New animation:** Buttons press down two pixels and pop back. The four moves fan out in their type colours. No new look, motion only.
- **See it:** no mockup yet, described only


## After battle

### 13. Winner's Moment
- **Today:** After a knock-out the winner keeps idling until the EXP bar moves.
- **New animation:** The winner gets a second to itself. It hops twice, spins once on the spot, a note pops out, and it leans toward you. Different body types celebrate differently: fliers loop, heavy ones stamp once.
- **See it:** pass 11, improved up to pass 16, clip "Winner's Moment"


## Moves

### 14. Multi-Hit
- **Today:** The move animation plays once and the damage is shown as separate numbers.
- **New animation:** Each hit is a quick jab with its own spark and its own notch off the HP bar, and a combo counter in the corner ticks up and grows with every one. It ends by slamming the total.
- **See it:** pass 12, clip "Multi-Hit"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the counter, the stamp and the target shaking a little more on each hit. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the effect of each hit.

### 15. Recoil
- **Today:** A number on the user and a text line.
- **New animation:** The force comes back. A crack of the move's own colour runs from the target back along the ground to the user, who winces, flashes and loses its bit of HP. You see why it hurt.
- **See it:** pass 12, clip "Recoil"

### 16. Drain
- **Today:** Two numbers: damage on the target, healing on the user.
- **New animation:** After the hit, what was taken travels: green lights lift off the target one by one, cross the field in an arc and sink into the user, whose HP bar steps up as each arrives.
- **See it:** pass 12, clip "Drain"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the healing glow on the user and the bar filling. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the tether between the two Pokémon.

### 17. One-Hit KO
- **Today:** A normal hit animation and the bar empties.
- **New animation:** The ground opens. A crack races from the user to the target, the target's base splits and it drops straight down out of sight, and the bar empties in one frame under a ONE-HIT KO stamp.
- **See it:** pass 12, improved up to pass 23, clip "One-Hit KO"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the silence, the shake of the arena and the stamp. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the crack drawn across the ground.

### 18. Recharge Turn
- **Today:** A text line: "must recharge!".
- **New animation:** After the biggest moves the user is spent, and looks it: steam pours off it, it sags with a grey tint and a RECHARGE chip hangs over it while the foe takes a free turn. When the turn ends the chip pops and it stands back up.
- **See it:** pass 12, improved up to pass 18, clip "Recharge Turn"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the brace before it and the recoil of the Pokémon after it. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the beam.

### 19. Contact Punish
- **Today:** A number on the attacker and a text line (Rocky Helmet, Rough Skin).
- **New animation:** Touching the wrong thing bites back at the moment of contact: spikes jab out of the target, the attacker bounces off further than it came in with a star on its nose, and the item or ability flag slides out to say why.
- **See it:** pass 12, clip "Contact Punish"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the dash of the attacker and the knock-back of the target. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the contact effect of the move.

### 20. Priority Move
- **Today:** Plays like any other move; only the turn order tells you it went first.
- **New animation:** A FIRST! tag snaps onto the user before anything else on the turn can start. It does not wind up: it blurs, leaves three after-images across the field, and the hit lands before the tag has faded.
- **See it:** pass 12, clip "Priority Move"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the Pokémon blurring forward first. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the effect of the move.

### 21. Entry Hazards
- **Today:** The move animation plays; nothing stays on the field and the switch-in damage is a number.
- **New animation:** Hazards stay where you can see them. Stealth Rock throws five stones that plant themselves around your side and hover there with a ROCKS chip. When your next Pokémon comes in they all stab inward at once.
- **See it:** pass 12, clip "Entry Hazards"
- **Build:** Kept from the redesign: the marks the hazard leaves on the arena. Not built, by your rule that specific move animations are not added or changed: the throw of the hazard move.


## Conditions

### 22. End-of-turn Damage
- **Today:** Burn, poison, Leech Seed, recoil and weather damage only show a number. Nothing plays on the Pokémon.
- **New animation:** Pass 3: A small version of the type impact, a flinch and a short drain. Fast, because it happens every turn: 0.5s each. Pass 8: Residual damage now has a look for each cause; see Burn, Poison and End of Turn.
- **See it:** pass 3, improved up to pass 8, clip "End-of-turn Damage"

### 23. Weather Clears

- **New animation:** The chip counts its last turn and pops. The rain thins out, one beam of light breaks through the clouds and travels across the field, and the colours come back.

### 24. Terrain
- **Today:** Only the move animation plays. Nothing stays on the ground, so you cannot see that a terrain is active.
- **New animation:** A terrain is a carpet that rolls out from the user's feet and stays under both Pokémon: a humming grid for Electric, growing flowers for Grassy, slow rings for Psychic, drifting mist for Misty. Each has its own chip and turn count.
- **See it:** pass 8, clip "Terrain"

### 25. End of Turn

- **New animation:** The end of a turn becomes a short parade: a row of chips shows what is about to happen, each lights up as its tick plays on the field, and then a line sweeps the screen and the turn counter flips over.
- **See it:** pass 8, clip "End of Turn"

## Galar

### 41. Gym Leader Entrance
- **Today:** The same slide-in as any trainer, then the battle starts.
- **New animation:** The leader walks out of the tunnel as a shadow against the light, their number fills the screen, the stadium lights snap on in their colour and their League Card flips over to show the team size. Then both sides take the pitch.
- **See it:** pass 7, clip "Gym Leader Entrance"

### 42. Gigantamax
- **Today:** Plays the Dynamax scene; the form just swaps.
- **New animation:** Its own show. The swollen ball bursts in the sky, the Pokémon's shadow grows up through the storm past the top of the screen, the camera pulls back to find the new shape, and its special feature (here the burning wings) ignites last. The G-Max mark stamps the name bar.
- **See it:** pass 7, clip "Gigantamax"

### 43. G-Max Move
- **Today:** Max and G-Max moves share one generic animation.
- **New animation:** The giant fires across the whole pitch: the title slams in, a wall of the move's type rolls from one side to the other, and what the move leaves behind stays on the field (here G-Max Wildfire's flames, with a 4-turn counter).
- **See it:** pass 7, clip "G-Max Move"

### 44. Max Raid Den
- **Today:** A raid starts like any other battle, with a screen fade.
- **New animation:** A red pillar of light stands over the den. Step in and the light swallows the screen, the storm spiral opens, and a giant shadow rises out of it while the star rating counts up. Your team of four lines up underneath it, tiny.
- **See it:** pass 7, clip "Max Raid Den"

### 45. Raid Barrier
- **Today:** Not in the game: raid bosses have no barrier.
- **New animation:** At half HP the boss throws up a barrier of six plates. Each hit cracks one off, the counter under its HP bar empties, and when the last one goes the whole barrier shatters and the boss staggers, wide open.
- **See it:** pass 7, clip "Raid Barrier"
- **Note:** Needs a rule first: raid bosses have no barrier in the game today.

### 46. Champion Time

- **See it:** pass 7, clip "Champion Time"

### 47. Eternamax

### 52. Rival Board

## Impact frames

### 61. Finishing Blow
- **Today:** The last knock-out of a battle looks like every other.
- **New animation:** The hit that ends a battle gets the full treatment: time stops on a negative frame, the screen goes black and white with lines racing in on the target, one beat of silence, and then colour snaps back as it is thrown clear off its base. K.O. is stamped across the screen.

## Light

### 62. Move Light
- **Today:** Moves never light the arena. A Flamethrower in a cave looks the same as one at noon.
- **New animation:** Moves are light sources. In a dark place you can see it best: the charge lights the user from below, the hit throws the type's colour across the floor and onto the other Pokémon, and the glow takes a moment to die. A fire hit is orange, an electric one is yellow, a water one is blue.
- **See it:** pass 18, clip "Move Light"


## Trainer and Pokémon together

### 63. Trainer Reactions
- **Today:** You are never seen or heard in a battle.
- **New animation:** Your trainer stays off the field, as decided, but is no longer silent. At the moments that matter a small face pops up at the right of your bar with one word: a wince when your Pokémon takes a big hit, a shout when yours lands, and a cheer on the knock-out. It never covers the message or the plates and is gone in a second.
- **See it:** pass 19, clip "Trainer Reactions"


## Arenas

### 64. Meadow
- **Today:** A flat striped backdrop. Since pass 8 a cloud shadow and a few motes drift over it.
- **New animation:** Clouds cross the sky, twelve tufts of grass sway out of step, two butterflies wander through. Every few seconds a gust runs across: the tufts bend in a wave from left to right and three white wind lines pass.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Meadow"

### 65. Cave
- **Today:** A flat brown backdrop.
- **New animation:** A ragged ceiling of rock hangs over the top of the screen. One shaft of daylight leans in from the left with dust turning in it, and water drips from three points, each drop opening a ring where it lands. Now and then something small flies across.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Cave"

### 66. Open Sea
- **Today:** A flat blue backdrop; both Pokémon stand on still platforms.
- **New animation:** Six lines of swell slide past at different speeds and the two platforms ride them. Gulls cross the sky and the sun glints off the water. When a big wave comes through, everything lifts and drops together and spray comes off both platforms.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Open Sea"

### 67. Volcano
- **Today:** A flat red backdrop.
- **New animation:** The horizon glows and pulses, ash clouds crawl the other way across the sky, and embers rise through the whole frame. Every so often the mountain behind coughs: a flash on the horizon, a shake, and three lava bombs arc across the sky.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Volcano"

### 68. Ice Cave
- **Today:** A flat grey-blue backdrop.
- **New animation:** Icicles hang across the top, the ice glints at random, fine snow falls through the frame, and both Pokémon breathe out small clouds. A cold place should look cold before anyone uses an Ice move.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Ice Cave"

### 69. Time of Day
- **Today:** Hub Town has a day and night clock. Battles are always lit the same.
- **New animation:** The run's clock reaches into battle. At dusk the whole picture warms and the shadows stretch; at night it turns blue, stars come out in the sky strip, the moon climbs, and fireflies drift over the grass. It is one tint layer and a handful of sprites, so it costs almost nothing.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Time of Day"

### 70. The Field Remembers
- **Today:** A move leaves nothing behind. The ground looks the same on turn ten as on turn one.
- **New animation:** A big hit leaves its type's mark under the target and the mark stays: a scorch that smokes, a patch of ice that glints, a puddle with rings, cracks in the ground. By the end of a long fight the arena tells you what happened in it. Marks are only pictures and never change the rules.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "The Field Remembers"

### 71. Arena Arrival
- **Today:** The battle screen appears complete after the transition.
- **New animation:** The place arrives before the fight does. The sky drops in from the top and the ground rolls up from the bottom, the two platforms slide in from opposite sides, the grass pops up tuft by tuft, and only then does the Pokémon step in. It takes under a second and a half.
- **See it:** pass 21, clip "Arena Arrival"


## Flow

### 72. EXP for the Whole Team
- **Today:** Only the active Pokémon's bar fills. The rest of the party gains experience in a text line.
- **New animation:** The fallen Pokémon's experience leaves it as lights. Most fly to the bar of the one that fought; the rest split off to five small portraits that slide in on the right, each with its own little bar. The bars fill in order, top to bottom, and the one that levels up flashes, hops, and shows its new level.
- **See it:** pass 23, clip "EXP for the Whole Team"

### 73. New Move
- **Today:** A text prompt and a list. The old move is replaced without motion.
- **New animation:** The four moves are four tiles in their type colours. The new move comes down from above as a fifth tile, glowing, and waits. You pick the one to lose: it counts One, Two, and with a Poof it turns white, cracks and drops out of the frame, and the new tile swings into the gap and locks with a ring.
- **See it:** pass 23, clip "New Move"

### 74. Held Items Speak Up
- **Today:** A held item that triggers gets a text line.
- **New animation:** When a held item does its job you see the item. A Focus Sash appears across the Pokémon, pulls tight as the bar stops one point short of empty, and snaps in two. A Shell Bell pops up beside the attacker after its hit, swings twice with two rings of sound, and a small green number follows. Every held item gets a two-beat cameo like these.
- **See it:** pass 23, clip "Held Items Speak Up"

### 75. Who Goes First
- **Today:** Turn order is never shown. The faster Pokémon simply acts first.
- **New animation:** For half a second at the top of each turn, two small portraits race along a strip. The faster one gets there first and its Pokémon gives a little hop on the field. When something changes the order, you see why: a Quick Claw flashes on the slower portrait and it leaps the other. Then the strip folds away and the turn plays.
- **See it:** pass 23, clip "Who Goes First"

### 76. Saving and Notices
- **Today:** Saving shows nothing. Notices are plain text lines.
- **New animation:** A small Poké Ball spins in the corner while the game saves and stops with a tick when it is safe to close. Notices slide in from the top as a card with an icon, shine once, and leave the same way; two at once stack and take turns. Nothing here ever covers the middle of the screen.
- **See it:** pass 23, clip "Saving and Notices"
